# Calculated dates

Kevin's ruling, 2026-11-08, 11:29 AM CT: "Naamah should be dated, but properly tiered so that as higher tiers arrive, they set the rule. This should be true for any calculable date for a person."

A calculated year is arithmetic on figures the repo already prints. It is stored on the person in `calculated`, not in a source entry's `year_bc_ad`. Precedence is: a tier 1–4 birth, then a tier 1–4 reign start, then a provisional printed year, then one calculated year, then none. Within calculated dates the order is birth, reign start, death, translation. A printed `year_bc_ad` on birth blocks every later slot. Two different calculated birth years do not fall through. The page shows `calc.` and the arithmetic only when that slot has no printed year. Historical years have no year 0.

Reign lengths were not turned into an end year. A lifespan in years was not added to a birth year. Those would mix a length from one chronology with a year from another, or would be off by a year. See N29 and N30.

People with a calculated value: 200. Of those, 140 had no sort year and now have one. 37 already had a higher sort year; that year is unchanged.
