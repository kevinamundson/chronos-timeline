## Judgement calls

- The Preface names many princes and gives most of them no year and no length. They were not made into people. Henry VIII is already `henry-viii`. The sentences read for him in the Preface give no year and no reign length, so nothing was added.
- sig. 16a–16b: “That gaue vnto Darius the part of the greatest Emperour, and the part of the most miserable begger, a begger begging water of an Enemie”. No year and no length. It was not attached to `darius-d31`. See open question N2.
- sig. 22a: “some few enterprises, greater in fame than faith, of Ninus and Semiramis excepted.” No new figure. Both people already carry their table reigns. Nothing was added.
- sig. 48b: “Now that this supposed first Moueable, turneth it selfe so many hundred thousand miles in an instant”. He declines the supposition (“let those that can beleeue mens imaginations, apprehend it, for I can not”). It was not entered. The figure is a distance, and the time word is “an instant”.
- Chapter I names many fathers and philosophers (Augustine, Basil, Plato, Aristotle, and others) and gives none of them a year, an age, or a reign. They were not made into people. This file does not claim a complete index of those names.
- Scripture quoted inside Chapter I (the tower, Sodom, Elijah at the brook, and the like) was not copied onto those records as a Raleigh date. He is quoting the passage, not giving a yeere of the World.
