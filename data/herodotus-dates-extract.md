## Unmapped statements

Statements with "— not mapped" in the tables are recorded for completeness. They concern genealogies or eras with no record (the Heraclid kings of Lydia, the Egyptian priests' 11,340 years, Heracles 17,000 years before Amasis, Dionysus and Pan), or general statements about how Herodotus counts. None of them can be placed on the site's time line without inventing a date.

## Anchor used for "before my time"

Herodotus counts several intervals back from his own day ("not 900 years", "about 400 years before my time", "1,600 years"). CFAH-3 dates his visit to Egypt to 450 BC (AP-17, AP-21), and that is the only anchor used for these derivations. The derived years are labelled as such and are never shown as Herodotus's figures.
