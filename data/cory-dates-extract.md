## Figures seen and not entered

- **Abibalus** is named on p. 28 as the father of Hiromus. The edition prints no length for him. No record.
- **The eldest of the four sons of Abdastartus's nurse** reigned twelve years (p. 29). He is not named. The statement is in the table above and is not mapped.
- **Phulus (Pul)** is named on p. 86. The passage that was read prints no length. No record, and he is not linked to any existing Pul.
- **Xerxes** is named on p. 89 after Darius and is given no length. No Xerxes row. The 36 years stay on Darius I.
- **Ships and men** in the Elulæus blockade (p. 31) are not reign lengths and were not entered. The five years of the blockade were entered.
- **Hodges's footnotes** (Jezebel and Ahab, Lulia and the Taylor cylinder, a Hebrew form, and *Records of the Past* citations) are the editor's notes. They are not fragment dates and were not entered.
- **Hodges's introduction** (printed pp. 43–50) prints BC dates, including a Nabonassar era and an eclipse. Those pages are the editor's chronology, not a fragment. Nothing from them is entered (G5).
- **Sanchoniathon** (printed pp. 1–22) was not image-read. OCR of those leaves showed no year, sarus, month or B.C. figure, so none was entered.
- **Not read, so not entered:** Manetho, the Old Chronicle, the Laterculus, Josephus's Berossus on pp. 64–67, and the Periplus of Hanno.
- **Not filled in:** the eleven kings whose years are printed as dots (p. 85). CFAH's 33 years for Babylon dynasty 3 were not copied into the blank (G9).
- **Not identified:** Xisuthrus with Noah (he is not on the Flood's people list); Ninus with Nimrod; Salmanasar with Shalmaneser I; the two Hiromus figures with each other; the two Ithobalus figures with each other; Sammuges, his unnamed brother, and Sardanapallus with each other (G1, G3, G7, G8, G10).
