## Figures seen and not entered

- **Abibalus** is named on p. 28 as the father of Hiromus. The edition prints no length for him. No record.
- **The eldest of the four sons of Abdastartus's nurse** reigned twelve years (p. 29). He is not named. The statement is in the table above and is not mapped.
- **Phulus (Pul)** is named on p. 86. The passage that was read prints no length. No record, and he is not linked to any existing Pul.
- **Xerxes** is named on p. 89 after Darius and is given no length. No Xerxes row. The 36 years stay on Darius I.
- **Ships and men** in the Elulæus blockade (p. 31) are not reign lengths and were not entered. The five years of the blockade were entered.
- **Hodges's footnotes** (Jezebel and Ahab, Lulia and the Taylor cylinder, a Hebrew form, and *Records of the Past* citations) are the editor's notes. They are not fragment dates and were not entered.
- **Hodges's introduction** (printed pp. 43–50) prints BC dates, including a Nabonassar era and an eclipse. Those pages are the editor's chronology, not a fragment. Nothing from them is entered (H5).
- **Sanchoniathon** (printed pp. 1–22): the thirty-second year of Ilus (p. 15) is entered. No other reign length was found. "Every year" on p. 7 is a feast. The later reign "by the consent of Kronus" gives no length.
- **Hodges's introduction** (printed pp. 43–50), including the supposed Berossus and Syncellus lists on p. 46, stays out (H5). The biographical notices on pp. 95–97 and 101–108 stay out, except the Africanus table and intervals (pp. 98–100) and the one Syncellus sentence quoted on p. 104. The editor's century-split after that sentence was not entered. pp. 106–108 discuss Eratosthenes; the list itself was entered from pp. 138–141, and the editor's 1,076 years were not entered as if Eratosthenes had printed them.
- **Not a reign, so not entered:** army, navy, acre and ship counts (including Hanno's sixty ships on p. 36, and the troop counts in the Josephus narrative); the ten days and the talents in the Nanarus story (p. 199); stadia and furlongs; the index (pp. 205–209). The same kings are entered from Josephus on pp. 65 and 67. "Fifteen days" on p. 72 is the time in which the walls were built, not a reign.
