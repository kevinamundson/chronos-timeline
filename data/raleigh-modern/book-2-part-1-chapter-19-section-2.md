# Book II, Part I, Chapter XIX, §. 2

## p02812 · sig. 308b

§. II. Of REHOBOAM his impiety; for which he was punished by Shishak *SESAC*: of his end and Contemporaries.

## p02813 · sig. 308b

While Jeroboam *Ieroboam* was occupied in setting up his new Religion, Rehoboam on the other side, having now little hope to recover the Provinces *Prouinces* lost, strengthened the principal places remaining with all endeavour: for he fortified and victualled fifteen Cities of 〈◊ 〉 and Benjamin *Bcniamin*: not that he feared Jeroboam alone, but the Egyptians *Aegyptians*, to whom Jeroboam had not only fastened himself, but withal invited them to invade Judea *Iudaea*: laying perchance before them the uncountable riches of David *Dauid* and Solomon *Salomon*, which might now easily be had, seeing ten of the twelve Tribes were revolted, and become enemies to the Judeans *Iudaeans*. So as by those two ways( of late years often trodden) to wit, change of Religion, and invitation of foreign force, Jeroboam hoped to settle himself in the seat of Israel, whom yet the powerful God for his Idolatry in a few years after rooted out, with all his. Rehoboam also, having as he thought, by fortifying divers places, assured his estate, forsook the Law of the living God, and made high Places, and Images, and Groves *Groues* on every high Hill, and under every green Tree.

## p02814 · sig. 308b

And therefore in the fifth year of his reign, Shishak or Shishac before spoken of, being now King of Egypt *Aegypt*, and with whom as well Adad of 〈◊ 〉, as Jeroboam, were familiar, and his instruments, entered Judea with twelve thousand Chariots, and three score thousand Horse, besides foot-men, which Josephus *Iosephus* numbers at four hundred thousand. This Army was compounded of four Nations; *Egyptians, Lubaeans, Succaeans*, and 〈◊ 〉. The Lubaeans were Libyans *Lybaeans*, the next bordering Region to Egypt, on the West side. The 〈◊ 〉 were of Petraea, and of the Desert Arabia, which afterward followed Zerah against 〈◊ 〉 King of Judah *Iuda*. The Succaeans according to Junius *Iunius* his opinion, were of Succoth, which signifies Tents: he does suppose that they were the Trogloditae, mentioned often in *〈◊ 〉, Ptolemy* *Ptolomie*, and other Authors. The Troglodites inhabited not far from the banks of the red Sea, in 22. degrees from the line Northward, about six hundred English mile from the best and Maritime part of Egypt: and therefore I do not think that the Sukkiim *Succims*, or 〈◊ 〉 were those Trogloditae, but rather those Arabians which Ptolemy calls Arabs *Arabes* 〈◊ 〉, or, Ichthyophagi, which possess that part of Egypt between the mountains called Alabastrini, and the red Sea far nearer Egypt, and readier to be levied then those removed Savages *Sauages* of the Troglodytae.

## p02815 · sig. 308b

With this great and powerful Army, Shishak invaded Judea, and( besides many other strong Cities) wan Jerusalem *Ierusalem* itself; of which, and of the Temple, and Kings house, he took the spoil, carrying away( besides other treasures) the golden shields which Solomon had made, 〈◊ 〉 imitation of those which David recovered from Hadadezer *Adidezer*, in the Syrian war: these Rehoboam supplied with Targets of brass, which were fit enough to guard a King of his quality: whom Syracides calls, The foolishness of the people.

## p02816 · sig. 309a

From this time forward the Kings of Egypt claimed the sovereignty of Judea, and held the Jews *Iewes* as their Tributaries: Shishak, as it seems, rendering up to Rehoboam his places on that condition. So much may be gathered out of the words of God, where promising the deliverance of Judah after their humiliation, he does notwithstanding leave them under the yoke of Egypt, in these words: *Nevertheless, they*( to wit, the 〈◊ 〉) *shall be his servants, that is, the servants of *Shishak.

## p02817 · sig. 309a

After this overthrow and dishonour, Rehoboam reigned twelve years, and his losses received by Shishak notwithstanding, he continued the war against Jeroboam all his life time. After his death Jeroboam governed Israel four years.

## p02818 · sig. 309a

Rehoboam lived 58. years, and reigned 17. his story was written at large by Shemaiah *Shemeiah* and Iddo *Hiddon* the Prophets, but the same perished with that of Nathan &amp; the rest.

## p02819 · sig. 309a

With *Rehoboam, Archippus*, and Tersippus, the third and fourth Archons *Archontes* or Governors *Gouernours* for life after Codrus, governed in *Athens. Abdastrartus*, or Abdastartus *Abstrartus*, in *Tyre. Doristhus *the fifth *fift* of the Heraclidae in Sparta, according to Eusebius( others make him the sixth) and Priminas the fourth in Corinth. Over the Latins *Latines* reigned Silvius *Syluius* Alba, and Silvius Atys, the fourth and fifth of the Syluij.

## p02820 · sig. 309a

About the 12. of *Rehoboam, Abdastrartus *King of Tyre was murdered by his Nurses sons, or foster brethren, the elder of which usurped the Kingdom twelve years.

## p02821 · sig. 309a

Towards his latter times Periciades, or Pyrithiades, began to govern Assyria, the 34. King thereof: and not long after Astartus, the son of Baleastartus recovered the Kingdom of Tyre from the Usurpers *Vsurpers*.
