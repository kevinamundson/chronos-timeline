## p01085 · sig. 91a

§. VI. Of HAM *CHAM*, and other wicked ones, whereof some got, some affected the name of Gods.

## p01086 · sig. 91a

OF Jupiter Belus, the son of Saturn *Saturnus* Babylonicus, otherwise Nimrod, it seems that Cicero had not heard, ( at least by that name) who was more ancient than any of the former three by him remembered: for long after these times were the Greeks *Greekes* but Salvages *Saluages*, if they seek no farther off for their Gods.

## p01087 · sig. 91a

But the Egyptians *Aegyptians*, even after the Flood *floud*, began( somewhat before this Chaldean *Chaldaean* Jupiter) to entitle Ham, the parent of their own *Mizraim, Jupiter Chammon*, or Ammon *Hammon*. For the Etymology of this word( Ammon) which the Greeks deduce *ab arenis; from the sands, *is ridiculous( says Peucer; ) neither yet is his own much better, who brings it from Hamath *Hammath*, which signifies heat: because the said Temple of Jupiter Ammon was seated in Libya, where the air is exceeding hot and scorching. And as for the antiquity of the latter Jupiter( among the Greeks and Romans the most renowned) it is certain that he was borne not long before the war of Troy, as by many of his sons is made manifest; namely, *Castor, 〈◊ 〉, Hercules, Sarpedon, *and others, which lived in that age of Priam *Priumus*, under whom, and with whom Troy was destroyed.

## p01088 · sig. 91b

Now seeing that mortal men, and the most wicked, were esteemed immortal among the Heathen; it was not to be wondered at, that *Alexander Macedon, Tyberius, Nero, Caligula, *and others, sought to be numbered among them, who were as deformed monsters as the rest: For by what reason could the same Deity be denied unto Laurentia and Flora, which was given to Venus? seeing they were as notorious and famous Harlots as she was.
