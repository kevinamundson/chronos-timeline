§. I. *The Proaeme to the description of the whole Land of Canaan, with an exposition of the name of Syria.*

## p02017 · sig. 217a

THE Story of the Judges *Iudges* ought to follow that of Joshua *Iosua*, after whom the Common-wealth of the Jews *Iewes* was governed by Kings, of which So many of them as ruled the ten Tribes, shall be remembered when we come to the description of Samaria: but because the Land of Canaan, and the borders thereof, were the Stages and Theaters, whereon the greatest part of the Story past, with that which follows, has been acted, I think it very pertinent( for the better understanding of both) to make a Geographical description of those Regions: that all things therein performed by the places known, may the better be understood, and conceived. To which purpose( besides the addition of the Neighbour Countries) I have bestowed on every Tribe his proper portion: and do show what Cities and Places of strength, were by the Jews obtained: and what numbers it pleased God to leave unconquered: by whom he might correct and scourge them, when ungrateful for his many graces, they at sundry times forgot or neglected the Lord of all power, and adored those deaf and dead Idols of the Heathen. Divina *Diuina* bonitas( says AVGUSTINE *AVGVSTINE*) *ideo maxime irascitur in hoc saeculo, ne 〈◊ 〉 in 〈◊ 〉: & misericorditer temporalem adhibet severitatem, ne aeternam 〈◊ 〉 inferat ultionem; The divine goodness is especially therefore* ("Therefore he is most angry in this world, lest [illegible] in [illegible]; and mercifully applies temporal severity, lest he inflict eternal [illegible] vengeance.") *angry in this World, that it may not be 〈◊ 〉 in the World to come, and does mercifully use temporal severity, that it may not justly bring upon us eternal vengeance.*

## p02018 · sig. 217a

To the Cities herein described, I have added a short Story of the beginnings and ends of divers Kingdoms *Kingdomes* and common-weals: and to help my self herein, I have perused divers of the best Authors upon this subject: among whom, because I find So great disagreement in many particulars, I have rather in such cases adventured to follow mine own reason, than to borrow any one of their old patterns.

## p02019 · sig. 217b

And because Canaan, with Palestine *Palaestina* of the Philistines *Philistims*, and the Lands of Og and Sihon Kings of Bashan *Basan*, and the Arabian Amorites, were but small Provinces *Prouinces* of Syria: it shall be necessary, first to divide and bound the general, and So to descend to this particular, now called the holy Land.

## p02020 · sig. 217b

Syrra, now Soria, according to the largest description, and as it was anciently taken, embraced all those Regions from the Euxine Sea, to the Red Sea: and therefore were the Cappadocians, which look into Pontus, called Leucosyrians, or white Syrians. But taking it shorter, and from the coast of Cilicia, which is the North border, unto Idumea *Idumaea* towards the South, Tigris towards the Sun rising, and the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea Westward: it then contains besides *Babylonia, Chaldea* *Chaldaea**, Arabia *the Desert, and Arabia 〈◊ 〉, that Region also which the Greeks *Greekes* call Mesopotamia, the Hebrews *Hebrewes* Syria, of the two Rivers *Riuers*, to wit, Tigris and Euphrates, for So Aram-Naharaim *Aram-Nahairaijm* is expounded: also Padan Aram: that is, Jugum Syriae, because the two Rivers go along in it as it were in a yoke.

## p02021 · sig. 217b

Edessa, sometime Rages, now Rage, was the Metropolis of this Region of Syria. In Syria taken largely, there were many small Provinces, as 〈◊ 〉 which the Latins *Latines* call Syria Cava, because it lay in that fruitful Valley between the Mountains *Mountaines* of Lybanus, and Anti-lybanus, in which the famous Cities of *Antioch, Laodicea, Apamea*, with many others were seated. Then Damascena or Syria Libanica *Lybanica*, taking name of the City Damascus, and the Mountains of Lybanus, the Regal seat of the Adads *Adades*, the first Kings of Syria. Adjoining to it was the Province of Sophene, or Syria Zobah *Soba*, Choba, or Zobah: over which Hadadezer *Adadezer* commanded in Salomons time. Then Phoenicia and the people Syrophoenicians *Syraphoenices*: and lastly Syria Palestine bordering Egypt *Aegypt*: of which Ptolemy *Ptolomie* makes Judea *Iudaea* also a part: and to that Province which Moses calls Seir and *Edom, Pomponius Mela *gives the name of Syria Judas.
