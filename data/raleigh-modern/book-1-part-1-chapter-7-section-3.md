§. III. Of DEVCALIONS Flood *Floud*: and that this was not NOAHS Flood: nor the Umbri *Vmbri* in Italy *Italie* a remnant of any universal Flood.

## p01117 · sig. 95b

A Second Flood of great fame, and of which the time is more certain, was that of Deucalion in Thessalia, of which Saint Augustine out of *Varro: His temporibus( ut *VARRO *scribit) regnante Atheniensibus* ("he writes) reigned over the Athenians")* *Cranaus *CRANAO*, successor CECROPIS( *ut autem nostri*, EVSEBIVS & HIERONYMUS *HIERONYMVS*) adhuc 〈◊ 〉 CECROPE *permanente, diluvium fuit, quod appellatum est* ("they remain, there was a flood, which was called [the flood of Ogyges];") DEVCALIONIS: ( that is) *In these times( as *VARRO reports) Cranaus *the successor of *CECROPS *governing the Athenians, or( as our *EVSEBIVS and Jerome *HIEROME* say) CECROPS *yet living, that Flood*( called DEVCALIONS) happened.

And in the beginning of the eleventh Chapter of the same eighteenth Book, he uses these words: Eduxit ergo MOSES *ex Aegypto populum Dei novissimo tempore* ("to lead the people of God out of Egypt in the last time")* *CECROPIS *Atheniensium Regis, cum apud Assyrios* ("of the king of the Athenians, when among the Assyrians [illegible] [reigned];")* 〈◊ 〉 *ASCATADES, apud Sicyonios MARATHUS *MARATHVS*, apud Argivos *Argiuos* TRIOPAS: MOSES *led the People of God out Egypt* *Aegypt** about the latter times of *CECROPS *King of the Athenians*, ASCATADES *reigning over the Assyrians, over the Sicyonians *MARATHUS, *and over the Argives* *Argiues** *TRIOPAS: So as leaving the curiosity of a few years, more or less, it appears, that this Flood of Deucalion was either at the departure of the children of Israel out of Egypt, or near it: and then after Noah 753. years, according to Funck *Functius*, who makes Cecrops to live in the year of the World 2409. or if we follow MERCATOR, then 739. years after Noah, and in the year of the World 2395. But if Deucalion were borne in the age of the World 2356. according to Codomannus *Codoman*; then giving unto Deucalion forty years of age when this Flood happened, it falls within one year of Mercator's *Mercators* account. But Deucalion by all approved Historians is said to have been 82. years old at that time. Now Clemens Alexandrinus dates the time of this Flood of Deucalion, and the conflagration and burning in Phaethon's *phoëtons* time, by the Reign of Crotopus King of the Argives; but Crotopus lived King of the Argives six years after Israel departed Egypt, which makes twenty years difference, according to Funck, who will have this Flood and burning to have fallen fourteen years before Moses left Egypt: for he gave of the Worlds years to the Flood and burning the year 2440. and to Moses his departure the year 2454. And yet Cedrenus thinks that Moses was more ancient, and lived with Inachus; but that cannot be true: for then had the Flood of Deucalion, and the burning of 〈◊ 〉, preceded the Flood of Ogyges, which is denied by all: for that of 〈◊ 〉( called Deucalions) followed that of Attica( called Ogygia) at least 250. years or thereabouts. Eusebius in his Chronology makes it 230. and So does *P. Orosius: Eusebius *about the 50. year of Moses life, and Cyril *Cyrillus* about the 67. and both after Noahs Flood 770. years: for these be Clemens 〈◊ 〉 his words: *Fuit autem in Graecia tempore quidem* ("But there was in Greece at that time indeed [a flood];")* *PHORONEI, qui fuit post INACHUM *INACHVM* ("who was after Inachus"), *inundatio quae fuit tempore* ("a flood which was at the time [of Ogyges];")* *OGYGIS, *There happened in Greece in the time of *Phoroneus *PHORONEVS*, *who lived after *INACHUS *INACHVS*, *the Flood of *OGYGES. Now if the Flood of OGYGES in Attica were 1020. or 1016. years before the first Olympiad, according to Eusebius and Orosius; ( as before) then is it manifest, that taking 763. out of this number of 1020. it falls out that Ogyges Flood happened before the Hebrews *Hebrewes* left Egypt 250. years, or 260. years, according to the difference between the opinions of Eusebius and Orosius. And for my self( who rather follow those Chronologers, which give 60. years more to Abraham after the Flood, then the rest) I reckon the times which come between these Floods *Flouds* in this sort. The general Flood was in the year of the World 1656. Jacob *Iacob* was borne in the year of the World 2169 So as from the beginning of the Flood to Jacobs *IACOBS* birth. there were consumed 513. years. Ogyges Flood happened 100. years after Jacob was borne; and therefore after the general Flood 613. years. Now Deucalion was borne in the year of the World 2356. and had lived 〈◊ 〉 years, when his Kingdom of Thessaly *Thessalie* was overwhelmed; ( which added to 2356. make 2438.) his Flood was after Noahs Flood ended 782. years. And hereto Annius his Xenophon agrees, who makes 700. years between the general Flood and Deucalions birth; to which ad 82. years of his Age( as before) and then the Flood of Thessaly followed the general 782. years. The words of that Xenophon are these: *Ab inundatione terrarum adortum *DEVCALIONIS, *secundo anno Sphaeri, septingenti supputantur anni, qui natus anno duos & octoginta Thessaliam vidit inundatam, From the drowning of the World to the birth of* ("In the second year of Sphaerus, seven hundred years are reckoned; who, born in the eighty-second year, saw Thessaly inundated.")* *DEVCALION, *in the second year of Sphaerus, are numbered *700. *years, and when *DEVCALION was 82. *years old, he saw Thessalia drowned. *This Flood happened in the Winter time about Parnassus: witness Aristotle in the first of his Meteors. And Varro( whom Saint Augustine So often cites for his excellent learning, especially in Antiquities) finds this Flood of Deucalion to have happened in the time of Cranaus, who succeeded *Cecrops: Orosius *thinks it somewhat later, Amphitryon reigning in Athens, the third from Cecrops. Only this of Deucalion was very great, and reached not only over Thessaly itself, and the Regions adjoining Westward; but it covered the greatest part of Italy: and either the same, or some other particular Flood then happening oppressed Egypt, says Eusebius. And therefore did the Greeks *Greekes* either think it, or said it to be universal; and Deucalion, then King, saving himself and some others on the Mountain of Thessaly( of all other the highest, says Solinus) was by reason thereof( as Strabo witnesses) said to be the preserver of Mankind. That this Flood covered a great part of *Italy, Pliny* *Plinie** *and Solinus make it probable: who affirm that the people then inhabiting Italy were therefore called 〈◊ 〉: *quia ab imbribus diluvij superfuissent* ("because they had survived the rains of the flood"); and therefore also were they esteemed the most ancient Nation, as Strabo confirms in his first Book, and Trezenius in his second: which Umbri these Authors *Authours* make the Parents of the Sabines, and the Sabines to be the Parents of the *Samnites, Piceni, Lucani, Brutij, *and all others inhabiting anciently the banks of the Mediterrane Sea. But that these Umbri were not the Inhabiters of Italy before the Flood of Noah, and So took name by saving themselves upon the Appenine Mountains *Mountaines*, the Scriptures teach us: showing who, and who only then were preserved, which is sufficient. Report has adventured further, telling us that the first people which after the general Flood inhabited Italy, were the Camesenes; ( So named from Cames *Camese*, whom CATO in originibus ("in the Origines"), another of Annius his Authors names for a consort of Janus) which people lived altogether a savage life; till such time as Saturn arriving on those Coasts, devised Laws *Lawes* to govern them by: the memory of whose acts in that Region Diodor and Thallus among the *Greeks, Nepos Cassius *and Varro among the Latins *Latines* have preserved; and of whom Virgil:

Of these Sicani( which left Spain *Spaine* and sate down in Italy) Thucydides and Pliny give testimony: who were again expelled by the Ligurians *Ligij*, says Thucydides. After all these plantations and replantations came the Umbri, descended of the Gauls *Gaules*( says Annius) not of those Gauls of France, but of those of Scythia, who commanded a great part of Italy, even all Etruria *Hetruria* and Campania, as *Herodotus, Pliny*, and Dionysius have assured us; and therefore this Flood of Deucalion was long after that of Noah. For all those Nations were planted in Italy, and dispossessed of Italy again, before the Umbri were ever heard of, or had being. So that Kingdom was first called Camasene, then Latium or Saturnia, then Ausonia, then 〈◊ 〉; before the Umbri( in whose time Deucalions Flood happened) possessed the same, about 306. years before the war of *Troy: Lycaon *then governing Arcadia; who being the Father of two and twenty Sons, the youngest called Oenotrius *Ocnotrius* invaded Italy, who gave it the name of Oenotria. This name it held until Italus of the same Nation changed it into Italy, after his own name, about 250. years before the fall of Troy. After these came the Pelasgi, of whom Pliny in his third Book and fifth *fift* Chapter; and Strabo in his fifth; Thucydides in his sixth speaks at large: and after them the Lydi under Tyrrhenus their Captayne, that gave name to the Tyrrheni; who casting thence the Umbri, took from them three hundred Castles, and built therein twelve Cities; to which( after they had possessed and past over the Appenine Mountains) they added divers others, whereof Telsina( afterward Bononia) was one.

Now that there was not anciently such a Nation, as these Umbri, in those parts, I do not affirm: having respect to the testimonies before repeated. And Stephanus thinks, that the name was derived from the Greek *Greeke* word Ombros; but that these Umbri of Italy were descended of the Nation of Scythians( called Galli) it shall be showed hereafter.
