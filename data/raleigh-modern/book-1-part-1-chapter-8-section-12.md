§. XII. Of the eleven sons of CANAAN, the fourth son of HAM.

## p01306 · sig. 127b

†. I. *Of the bounds of the Land of Canaan: with the names of his eleven sons.*

## p01307 · sig. 127b

CANAAN( the fourth son of Ham) possessed all that Region called by the Romans *Romanes* Palestine *Palaestina*; in the Scriptures *〈◊ 〉, Samaria*, and Judea *Iudaea*; in the latter times known by the name of the Holy Land and Jury: the limits whereof are precisely set down by MOSES, Genesis the tenth, *Then the border of the Canaanites was from Sidon* *Zidon** as thou go to Gerar until Azzah, and as thou go unto Sodom and Gomorha, and Admah, and Zeboim, even unto Lasha. *Now howsoever these words of the Hebrew Text( *as thou go*) be converted, Moses meaning was that Gerar was the South bound of Canaan and Sidon the North; Sodom and Gomorha the East, and the other Cities named stood on the Frontiers thereof. For Gerar stands in a right Line from Gaza in the way of Egypt *Aegypt*, the uttermost Territory of Canaan Southward: and this was properly the Land of Canaan.

## p01308 · sig. 127b

Now the sons of Canaan which possessed this Country, and inhabited some part of the borders thereof, were in number eleven:

## p01309 · sig. 128a

1. Sidon. 2. Heth or Heth *Chethus*. 3. Jebusi or Jebusite *Iebusaeus*. 4. Emori or Emoreus, or Amoreus. 5. Girgeshi or Girgashite *Girgeseus*. 6. Hevi or Hivite. 7. Arki or Arkite *Harkeus*. 8. Seni or Sinite *Sinaeus*. 9. Araadi or Arvadite *Aruadaeus*. 10. Zemari or Samareus, or Zemarite *Tzemareus*. 11. Hamathi or Hamatheus, or Hamathite *Chamaethaeus*.

## p01310 · sig. 128a

Of which the most renowned were the *Hethites, Gergesites, Amorites, Hevites* *Heuites**, Jebusites* *Iebusites**, *and Perizzites: which Perizzites were descended of Zemari or Samareus, or from some of his.

## p01311 · sig. 128a

†. II. *Of the portions of *Sidon and HETH.

## p01312 · sig. 128a

Sidon the first borne of Canaan, built the famous City of Sidon in Phaenicia, which afterward fell in partition to the Tribe of Asher *Asser*: for *Asher, Zebulun* *Zabulon*, and Nephtali had a great part of the ancient Phoenicia distributed among them; but the Asserites could never obtain Sidon itself.

## p01313 · sig. 128a

The second son of Canaan was Heth or Heth: of whom came the Hethites, or Hittites, one of those seven principal Nations( Commanders of Canaan) appointed by God to be rooted out; namely, the Gergesites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, and Hevites, and the Jebusites. The Hittites inhabited about Bersabe, and towards Hebron near the Torrent Besor, and about Gerar, which Moses makes the uttermost limit of Canaan, having the Desert of Paran *Pharan* to the South: for about Bersabe( otherwise Puteus juramenti) four miles from Gaza dwelt Heth and his Posterity, as far to the Northeast as Hebron, and Mamre; and of Ephraim the Hittite did Abraham buy the field of Sarah's *Sarahs* burial. Of which Nation Rebecca bewailed her self to Isaac, saying, *That she was weary of her life for the Daughters of *HETH. The Giants Anakim were of these Hittites, a strong and fierce Nation, whose entertainment by the Kings of Israel against them the Syrians greatly feared: as in the fourth of the Kings; *Israel has hired against us the Kings of the Hittites.*

## p01314 · sig. 128a

†. III. *Of the Jebusites and Amorites*.

## p01315 · sig. 128a

JENUSAEUS *IENVSAEVS*, the third son of Canaan, of whom came the Jebusites, and whose principal seat was Jebus, ( afterward Jerusalem *Hierusalem*) were also a valiant and stubborn Nation, and held their City and the Country near it, till such time as David *Dauid* by Gods assistance recovered both: yet were not the Jebusites extinguished, but were Tributaries to Solomon *Salomon*.

## p01316 · sig. 128a

Amoreus was the fourth son of Canaan, of whom the Amorites took name, who inhabited that Land to the East of Jordan below the Sea of Galilee, having Arnon and the Mountains *Mountaines* of Gilead *Galaad* on the East, and Jordan on the West: of whom Og( King of Bashan *Basan*) and Sihon( overthrown by Moses) were Princes.

## p01317 · sig. 128a

The 〈◊ 〉 had also many other habitations dispersed within the bounds of Canaan: as behind Lebanon *Libanus* in the edge of Caelesyria, or Syria Libanica. They had also their being in the Mountains of Judah *Iuda*, and in Idumea *Idumaea* near the Metropolis thereof, called Duma. And hereof it came that all the Canaanites were sometime called Amorites: as in Genesis the fifteenth: *For the wickedness of the Amorites is not yet full. *And that this was also a powerful Nation, we find in the Prophet AMOS: Yet destroyed *I the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of a Cedar, and he was strong as the Okes.*

## p01318 · sig. 128b

†. IIII. *Of the Gergesites, Hevites, and Harkites.*

## p01319 · sig. 128b

The fifth *fift* son of Canaan was Girgashite or Girgashite, ( otherwise Girgasi) who inhabited on the East side of the Lake of Tiberias, or the Sea of Galilee, where Ptolemy *Ptolemie* sets the City Gerasa, which Josephus *Iosephus* calls Gesera in the Territory of Decapolis. Here it was that CHRIST dispossessed the possessed with Devills *Deuills*; and the Gergesites desired him to depart their Coasts: because their Swine filled with the evil spirits, drowned themselves in the Sea of *〈◊ 〉. Girgashite *also built Berytus *Berytas*( sometime Geris) afterward Felix *Foelix* Julia, three miles from the River Adonis in Phoenicia: in which the Romans held a Garrison: and to which Augustus gave many large privileges.

## p01320 · sig. 128b

〈◊ 〉 the sixth son; and Father of the Hevites, inhabited under Lebanon near Hamath *Emath*. These 〈◊ 〉, howsoever the Caphthorim expelled a good part of them( as in Deuteronomy the second is remembered) yet many of them remained all the war of Joshua *Iosua*, and afterward to the time of Solomon. For God was not pleased utterly to root out these Nations, but they were sometimes made Tributaries to the Israelites, and at other times served( in their falling away from the true worship of God) to afflict them: for as it is written, Judges *Iudges* the third: *They remained to prove *ISRAEL *by them, whether they would obey the Commandments* *Commandements** of God.*

## p01321 · sig. 128b

The seventh *seuenth* son was Arkite or Arkite, who between the foot of Lebanon and the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea, ( over against Tripolis) built the City of *Archas, Arce*, or Arca, afterwards Arachis.

## p01322 · sig. 128b

†. V. Of SINI and ARVADI.

## p01323 · sig. 128b

Sineus *SINEVS* the eighth son, Hierosolymitanus sets at Caparorsa, which Ptolemy finds in Judea, not far from Jebus; to the South thereof, says Junius *Iunius*. But it is more probable, that Sineus founded Sin, which S. Jerome *Hierome* calls *Sim; Ptolemy, Simyra: Mela *and *Plinte, Simyrus: Brochard, Sycon, *( called Synochis) near *Arca. Pereira* *Pererius** *thinks that Sineus inhabited the Deserts *Desarts* of Sinai or thereabout; but hereof there is no other certainty then the report of Brochard, who took view of all these places, affirming, that Sineus built Synochis, as Sidon built Sidon. There is also another Nation of Kenites *Cini*( written with the letter C. otherwise Kenites) who descended of Hobab the son of Raguel the Madianite, who assisted the Israelites in their conduction through the Wilderness of Paran. But these Cinaei were admitted among the Israelites, and had a portion of Land with the Nephtalims, beside their habitations with the Amalekites: against these *Cinaei, Balaam *prophesied, that they should be destroyed by the Assyrians.

## p01324 · sig. 128b

The ninth son was Arvadite or 〈◊ 〉, who in the Isle of Aradus built the City Arados: opposite against which Island on the Main of Phoenicia, they founded another City of that name, which for opposition was afterwards called Antaradus. To this City came S. Peter( says Clement) and in this Isle preached the Gospel, and founded a Church in honor of our Lady: but we find no such work of his in the Acts of the Apostles. Both these two were very famous, and places of skillful Sea-men: whom Ezekiel *Ezechiel* remembers in his prophecies against the *Tyrians. The Inhabitants of Sidon and 〈◊ 〉 were thy Mariners.*

## p01325 · sig. 129a

†. VI. Of ZEMARI.

## p01326 · sig. 129a

OF Samareus, or Zemari, the tenth son, there are divers opinions. Some think that he first inhabited in Coelesyria at Edessa, and founded Samaraim, which in Joshua is placed in the Tribe of Benjamin *Beniamin*. There is also Samaraim *Samaraijm*( of the same Orthography) upon the Mountains of Ephraim( says Beroaldo *Beroaldus*) mentioned in the second of *Chronicles c. 13. v. 4. *which the Latine converts amiss( says he) by Semeron. The Hierosolymitan Paraphrast makes Samareus( of whom were the Perizzites) the parent of the Emissani, which Pliny *Plinie* calls the Hemisceni, in Coelesyria; and it may be that it was their first habitation, and that they afterwards inhabited those other places before remembered. But that they founded Samaria, both the Hebrew Orthography, and this place in the first of Kings( speaking of Omri) disproves: *And he bought the Mountain Samaria or Shemeron of one *SHEMER, *for two talents* *of silver, and built in the Mountain: and called the name of the City which he built, after* *the name of *SHEMER, *Lord of the Mountain Samaria*. But of all these places I shall speak more at large in the conquest of the holy Land, by the children of Israel. Of whomsoever the Samaritans *Samaritanes* were descended, sure I am, that they were ever a perfidious Nation, and base: for as long as the state of the Jews *Iewes* stood up, they always called themselves Jews: when it suffered or sank, they then utterly denied to be of that Nation or Family; for at such time as they were returned from their first Captivity *captiuitie*, they became a mixed Nation; partly of the Colonies of the Assyrians; and partly of the naturals.

## p01327 · sig. 129a

†. VII. Of HAMATHI.

## p01328 · sig. 129a

The last of Canaans sons was Hamatheus, or( according to the Hebrew) Hamathi, of Hamath: ( says Beroaldo) of which( the aspiration taken away) the same is pronounced Hamath, whereof Hamatheus was parent. Josephus and S. Jerome confound Hamath with Antioch, not that Antioch which stands on the River Orontes, on the frontier of Comagena, between the Mountain Cassius and the Province of Pieria, and Seleucis, of which S. Peter was Bishop, and in which S. Luke and Ignatius were borne: but Antioch, surnamed Epiphania, as Beroaldo supposes, which stands between Apamea and Emesa in Cassiotis. Yet, indeed, Hamath cannot be taken for either: for both that Antioch upon Orontes, and that which neighbours Emesa, are farther off seated from Canaan, then ever any of those Nations straggled. And whereas S. Jerome sets Hamath, which he confounds with Epiphania, in the Tribe of Nephtali; it is manifest, that Epiphania, which stands to the North of Emesa, has all the Province of Laodicea, between it and any part of the Land divided. And if Libanon itself were not shared among the Tribes, then could not Epiphania belong unto them: for both the Provinces *Prouinces* Laodicea and Libanica, are between Epiphania and any part of the holy Land: and therefore Hamath So taken could not be a part of Nephtali, as in the thirteenth of Joshua is directly proved. For Joshua counting the lands that remained unpossessed, reckons all Mount Libanon towards the Sun-rising *Sunne-rising*, from Baal-Gad *Baalgad* under mount Hermon, until we come to Hamath. And this reason( among others) is used, that Hamath was not in Naphtali *Nephtalim*, or any way belonging to the children of Israel: because David accepted the presents of Tohu King of Hamath, and( therewith) conditions of peace: which he would not have done, if that Territory had ever belonged to the children of Israel, but would have recovered it without composition, and by strong hand, as he did the rest. But this Argument( as I take it) has no great weight. For if the promise which God made, be considered, as it is written in Deuteronomy, then might Hamath be comprehended, though seated altogether without the bounds of the Land promised, according to the description of Moses and Joshua: for Hamath is indeed situate on the other side of the Mountain of Hermon, which joins to Lebanon: and is otherwise called Iturea. But whereas Hamath is named in Joshua the 19. v. 35. and written in the Latine conversion Hamath, therein( says Beroaldo) was S. Jerome mistaken. Hamath or Iturea is that over the Mountains, and the City in Naphtali should be written Hamath: and So the Septuagint( understanding the difference) write it Ammath and not Hamath, the same which indeed belonged to the Nephtalims, seated on the South side of Lebanon, to the East of Assedim: which City S. Jerome writes *Hamath; Josephus, Hamath*; others, Emathin, or Amatheos, and the people Amathein; of which( as I take it) Rabsakeh vaunts in the second of KINGS: *Where is the god of Hamath?*
