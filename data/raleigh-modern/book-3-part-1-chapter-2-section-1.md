# Book III, Part I, Chapter II, §. 1

> Sir Walter Raleigh, *The History of the World* (1617). Modern edition. Original spelling: EarlyPrint A10357 (EEBO-TCP), London 1617, Stansby for Walter Burre. The original text is untouched; this file is the stored modern copy.

## p03426 · sig. 393b

§. I. *That the Medes were chief actors in the subversion of the* Babylonian Empire.

## p03427 · sig. 393b

THE Line of Belochus being now extinguished in Belshazzar *Balthasar*, the Empire of Babylon, and of Assyria, was joined first to that of Media, which then was governed by Cyaxares or Darius Medus, after whom Cyrus became Lord and Monarch, both of Assyria and of Media itself.

## p03428 · sig. 393b

Of the race of Pul *Phul* Belochus there were ten Kings besides himself, and of Arbaces as many are found by Metasthenes. These two Provincial Governors *Gouernours* having cut down the last branch of Ninus in Sardanapalus, divided between them the Eastern Empire. Cyaxares( whom the Scriptures call Darius Medus) the last of the race of Arbaces, dying about two years after that the line of Belochus was ended in Belshazzar; the Dominions as well of the Conqueror, as of the conquered, fell to a third Family, namely, to Cyrus of the house of Achaemenes, the Princes of which blood reigning in Persia, had formerly been dependants on the Medes, and were of as little power at home, as of fame abroad in the World.

## p03429 · sig. 393b

Of the Family of the Achaemenes, and Line of the Persian Kings, we shall hereafter find occasion in due place to entreat.

## p03430 · sig. 393b

The Nation of the Medes descended from Madai the third son of Japheth *Iaphet*; that they had Kings soon after the Flood *floud*, Lactantius and Diodorus have found record; For Lactantius remembers an ancient King of the Medes called Hydaspes, and Diodor speaks of Pharnus with his seven Sons, slain by the Assyrian in the beginning of their Empire.

## p03433 · sig. 394a

And though the Greeks *Greekes* ascribe the conquest of Babylon to Cyrus alone, yet the Scriptures teach us that Darius was not only King of Media, and had the Persians his followers, but that the Army victorious over Balthazar was his; as the Assyrian and Babylonian Empire also was during his own life. For we find in Daniel, that Darius of the Medes took the Kingdom being threescore and two years old: And further, what Officers it pleased him to set over the Kingdom. And So was it prophesied by Isay long before: *Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them, &c.* And by JEREMIAH *IEREMIE*; *The Lord has raised up the Spirit of the King of the Medes: for his purpose is against Babel to de destroy it*; and in the eighth and twentieth Verse, *Prepare against her the Nations, with the King of the Medes, the Dukes thereof, the Princes* *thereof, and all the Land of his Dominion. *These Scriptures Julius *Iulius* Affricanus does well open, who taking authority from *Diodor, Castor, Thallus*, and others, delivers that Babylon was taken before Cyrus began to reign, which also agrees with Strabo, where he says, That as the Medes were subjugated by the Persians, So before that, both the Babylonians and Assyrians were mastered by the Medes. And therefore the reports of Justin *Iustine*, and Herodotus, are not to be received, who attribute the taking of Babylon to Cyrus alone.
