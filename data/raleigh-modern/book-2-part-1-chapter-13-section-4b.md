§. IIII. Of DEBORA and her Contemporaries.

## p02495 · sig. 265b

After Israel had lived in peace and plenty to the end of these 80. years, they again began to forget the giver of all goodness, and many of those being worn out, which were witnesses of the former misery, and of Gods deliverance by Ehud, and after him by Samgar, the rest began to return to their former neglect of Gods commandments. For as Plenty and Peace are the parents of idle security; So is security as fruitful in begetting and bringing forth both danger and subversion: of which all estates in the World have tasted by interchange of times. Therefore when their sins were again ripe for punishment, Jabin *Iabin* King of Hazor, after the death of Ehud, invaded the Territory of Israel, and having in his service 〈◊ 〉. iron Chariots, besides the rest of his forces, he held them in subjection twenty years, till it pleased God to raise up Deborah, the Prophetess, who encouraged Barak *Barac* to levy a force out of Nepthalim, and Zebulun *Zabulon*, to encounter the Canaanites. That the men of Nepthalim were more forward than the rest in this action, it may seem to have proceeded partly from the authority that Barak had among them, being of the same Tribe; and partly from their feeling of the common grievance, which in them was more sensible, than in others, because Hazor and Haroseth the chief holds of Jabin, were in Nepthalim. So in the days of Jephthah *Ieptha* the Gileadites took the greatest care, because the Ammonites with whom the War was, pressed most upon them, as being their borderers. Now as it pleased God by the left hand of Ehud to deliver Israel from the Moabite: and by the counsel and courage of a woman, to free them from the yoke of Canaan, and to kill the valiant Sisera by Jael *Iael* the Kenites wife: So was it his will at other times, to work the like great things by the weakest means. For the mighty Assyrian Nebuchadnezzar *Nabuchodonosor*, who was a King of Kings, and resistless, he overthrew by his own imaginations, the causers of his brutish melancholy: and changed his matchless pride into the base humility of a Beast. And to approve that he is the Lord of all power, he sometime punishes by invisible strength, as when he slaughtered the Army of Sennacherib *Senacherib* by his Angel: or as he did the Egyptians *Aegyptians* in Moses time: sometime by dead bodies, as when he drowned Pharaoh *Pharao* by the waves of the Sea: and the Canaanites by hail-stones in the time of Joshua *Iosua*: sometimes by the ministry of men, as when he overthrew the four Kings of the East, Chedorlaomer, and his companions, by the household servants of Abraham. He caused the Moabites and Ammonites to set upon their own confederate the Army of the Edomites; and having slain them to kill one another in the sight of Jehoshaphat *Iehosaphat*: and of the like to these a volume of examples may be gathered. And to this effect did Deborah the Prophetess speak unto Barak in these words: *But this journey that thou take, shall not be for thine honour,* *for the Lord shall sell *SISERA *into the hands of a Woman. *In which victory all the strength of the Canaanite Jabin fell to the ground, even to the last man: in the end of which War it seems that Jabin himself also perished, as appears by the last Verse of the fourth of Judges *Iudges*.

## p02496 · sig. 265b

After all which Deborah gives thanks to God, and after the acknowledgment of all his powerfulness, and great mercies, she shows the weak estate into which Israel was brought for their Idolatry by the Canaanites, and other bordering Nations, in these words: *Was there a shield or spear seen among forty thousand of Israel?* she also shows how the Israelites were severed and amazed, some of them confined over Jordan, and durst not join themselves to the rest; as those of Reuben in Gilead *Gtlead*: that the Asherites kept the Sea-coast, and forsook their habitations towards the Land, and the children of Dan who neighboured the Sea, crept into their ships for safety, showing thereby that all were dispersed, and all in effect lost. She then curses the inhabitants of Meroz, who dwelling near the place of the battle( probably fearing the success) came not out to assist Israel, and then blesses Jael the wife of Eber *Heber* the Kenite, who nailed Sisera in her Tent: showing the ancient affection of that race to the Israelites. For though the Family of Eber were enforced in that miserable time of subjection, to hold correspondence with Jabin the Canaanite, yet when occasion offered them means, they witnessed their love and faith to their ancient friends. Lastly, she derides the Mother of Sisera who promised her son the victory in her own hopes: and fancied to her self, and described the spoils both of Garments and Maidens by him gotten. For conclusion, she directs her praises and thanks to God only victorious.

From the beginning of Jabins *Iabins* oppression to the end of that peace, which Deborah *Deborath* and Barak purchased unto Israel, there passed 40. years. In which time the Kingdom of Argos which had continued 544. years was translated to Mycenae *Mycaenae*: The translation of this Kingdom Vives *Viues* out of Pausanias writes to this effect: After *〈◊ 〉, Lynceus *succeeded in Argos, after whom the children of Abas the son of Lynceus divide the Kingdom: of which Acrisius being eldest held Argos itself: Praetus his brother possessed Ephyra *Epbyra* or Corinth, and Tirynthos, and other Cities with all the Territory towards the Sea, there being many monuments in Tirynthos, which witness Praetus possession, says Pausanias.

## p02498 · sig. 266a

Now Acrisius was foretold by an Oracle, that he should be slain by the son of his Daughter Danae: whereupon he caused her to be enclosed in a Tower, to the end that no man might accompany her. But the Lady being exceeding fair, it is feigned that Jupiter turned himself into a golden shower: which falling into her lap, begat her with child: the meaning whereof was, that some Kings son, or other Worthy man, corrupted her keepers with gold: and enjoyed her, of whom Perseus was borne; who when he grew to mans estate, either by chance( says Ctesias) or in showing his grand-father the invention of the discus, or leaden ball, slew him unwillingly. After this Perseus, to avoid the infamy of Patricide in Argos, changed Kingdoms *Kingdomes* with his Uncle Praetus: and built Mycenae. This imprisonment of *Danae, Sophocles* *Sophoeles** *reports otherwise: and that she was enclosed in a brazen vault, under the Kings Hall with her Nurse and Keepers.
