§. II. *THE TRIBE OF SIMEON*.

## p02291 · sig. 241a

The Tribe of Simeon takes up the rest of the Sea-coast of Canaan, to the border of Egypt *Aegypt*: who being the second Son of Jacob *Iacob* by Lea, there were increased of that family while they abode in Egypt, as they were numbered at Mount Sinai, 59300. able men, all which ending their lives in the Deserts *Desarts*, there entered the Land of Promise of their issues 22200. bearing arms, who were * in part mixed with Judah *Iuda*, and in part severed, inhabiting a small Territory on the Sea-coast, belonging to Idumea *Edumaea*, of which the first City adjoining to Dan, was Ashkelon *Ascalon*.

## p02292 · sig. 241a

The Reguli or petty Kings thereof were called Ascalonitae: of which Volaterran out of Xanthus, in the History of the Lydians, reports, that Tantalus and Ascalus were the sons of Hymenaeus: and that Ascalus being employed by Aciamus King of the Lydians, with an Army in Syria, falling in love with a young Woman of that Country, built this City and called it after his own name: the same has Nicolaus in his History, says Volaterran.

## p02293 · sig. 241a

Diodorus Siculus in his third Book remembers a Lake near Ashkelon, wherein there has been a Temple dedicated to Derceto, the Goddess of the Syrians: having the face of a Woman and the body of a Fish: who, as I have said before, in the story of Ninus, was the Mother of Semiramis, feigned to be cast into this Lake, and fed and relieved by Doves *Doues*. And therefore was the Dove worshipped both in Babylonia and Syria, of which Tibullus the Poet:

## p02294 · sig. 241a

It was one of the chiefest and strongest Cities of the Philistines *Philistims*. It bred many learned men( says Volaterran) as *Antiochus, Sosus, Cygnus, Dorotheus *the Historian, and Artemidorus who wrote the story of Bithynia.

## p02295 · sig. 241a

In Ashkelon, as some say, was that wicked Herod borne, that seeking after our Saviour, caused all the Male-children of two years old and under to be slain. In the Christian times it had a Bishop, and after that when it was by the Saladine defaced, Richard King of England, while he made War in the holy Land, gave it a new wall and many buildings. *Eius muros cum* ("Bozor in the wilderness of Misor")* *SALADIN *SALADINVS* diruisset, RICHARDUS *RICHARDVS* Anglorum Rex ("Richard, King of the English") instauravit, says Adrichem *Adrichomius*.

## p02296 · sig. 241b

In Davids *Dauids* time it was one of the most renowned Cities of the Philistines: for he names Gath and Ashkelon only, when he laments the death of Saul, and 〈◊ 〉, not speaking of the other three. *Tell it not in Gath, nor publish it not in the streets of Ashkelon: *It is now called *Ascalon* *Scalone**. Gabinius *restored it as he did Ashdod *Azotus* and 〈◊ 〉.

## p02297 · sig. 241b

Next to Ashkelon stood Gaza or Gaza *Gazera*, which the Hebrews *Hebrewes* call Gaza, the Syrians Gaza: of Azonus( as they say) the son of Hercules. Other profane writers affirm, that it was built by *Jupiter. Pomp. Mela *gives the building thereof to Cambyses the Persian: because probably he rebuilt it, and Gaza in the Persian tongue is as much to say as Treasure. This Gaza was the first of the five Satrapies of the Philistines: and the South bound of the Land of Canaan towards Egypt. But this City was far more ancient than Cambyses, as it is proved by many Scriptures. It was once taken by Caleb: but the strength of the Anakim *Anakims* put him from it. At such time as Alexander Macedon invaded the Empire of Persia, it received a Garrison for Darius: in despite whereof it was by the Macedonians after a long siege demolished, and was called Gaza of the Desert.

## p02298 · sig. 241b

Alexander Janneus *Ianneus* King of the Jews *Iewes* surprised it: and slew 500. Senators in the Temple of Apollo which fled thither for Sanctuary: but this Gaza was not set up in the same place again, to wit, on the foundations which Alexander Macedon had over-turned, but somewhat nearer the Sea side: though the other was but two mile off. It was a Town of great account in the time of the Machabees, and gave many wounds to the Jews till it was forced by Simon: of which he made So great account as he purposed to reside therein himself, 〈◊ 〉 in his absence left John *Iohn* his Son and Successor to be Governor. In Brochard his time it was still a goodly City, and known by the name of Gaza.

## p02299 · sig. 241b

At the very out-let of the River of Besor, stands Maiuma *Maioma* the Port of Gaza: to which the privilege of a City was given by the great Constantine, and the place called Constance after the name of the Emperors *Emperours* Son. But Julian *Iulian* the Apostata soon after favouring the Gazans *Gazeans*, made it subject unto them, and commanded it to be called Gaza maritima.

## p02300 · sig. 241b

On the other side of Besor, stands Anthedon, defaced by Alexander Janneus, restored by Herod, and called Agrippias, after the name of Agrippa, the favourite of Augustus.

## p02301 · sig. 241b

Then Raphia, where Philopater overthrew the great Antiochus: and beyond it Rhinocorura *Rhinocura*, whose Torrent is known in the Scriptures by the name * of *the Torrent of Egypt*, till the Septuagint converted it by Rhinocorura: to difference it, Es. 27. 12. giving the name of the City to the Torrent that waters it. Pliny *Plinie* calls it Rhinocorura; and *Josephus* *Iosephus**, Rhinocorura*: a Epiphanius reports it as a Tradition, that at this place the World was divided by lots, between the three Sons of Noah.

## p02302 · sig. 241b

Within the Land and upon the River of Besor they place Gerar: which the Scripture places between Kadesh and *Shur, Gene*. 20. 1. That it was near to the Wilderness of Beer-shebah, it appears Gene. 20. 31. and therefore no marvel that as elsewhere Beer-shebah, So sometime Gerar, be made the South bound of Canaan. It was of old a distinct Kingdom from the Philistine *Philistim* Satrapies, the Kings by one common name were called Abimelechs; Saint Jerome *Hierome* says that afterward it was called *Regio salutaris; the healthy Country: *So that it was no marvel that Abraham and Isaac lived much in these parts. Of King Asa's conquest of the Cities about Gerar, see 2. Chron. 14. 14.

## p02303 · sig. 241b

More within the Land was Ziklag *Siceleg* or Ziklag, which was burnt by the Amalekites, when David *Dauid* in his flying from Saul to the Philistines had left his carriages there, but David followed them over the River of Besor, and put them to the slaughter, and recovered the prey.

## p02304 · sig. 242a

Next Dabir sometime Cariath-Sepher, the City of Letters, the University, as they say, or Academy of old Palaestine. In S t. Jerome's *Hieromes* time it seems it had the name of *Daema: Joshua* *Iosua** *15. 49. it is called Urbs *Vrbs* Sannae ("the city of Sanna"): from the name, as it seems, of some of the Anakim, as Hebron was called Urbs Arba *Arbahi*. For even hence also were these Giants expelled. It was taken chiefly by Othniel *Othoniel*, encouraged by Calebs promise of his Daughter in marriage: but that Joshua and the Host of Israel were at the surprise, it appears Josh.. 10. 39. This City Josh.. 21. 15. is named among those which out of Simeon and Judah were given to the Levites *Leuites*. And hence it seems they attribute it to this Tribe.

## p02306 · sig. 242a

In the same places of Joshua and of the Chronicles Chorma is named, which they think to be the same with that of which Numb. 14. 45. to which the Amalekites and Canaanites pursued the Israelites. But that Chorma cannot be in Simeon, nor within the Mountains *Mountaines* of Idumea. For Israel fled not that way: but back again to the Camp, which lay to the South of Idumea, in the Desert.

## p02307 · sig. 242a

The same places also name Beersheba in this Tribe: So called of the Oath between Abraham and 〈◊ 〉: near unto which Hagar wandered with her Son Ism ael. It was also called the City of Isaac, because he dwelt long there. While the Christians held the Holy Land, they laboured much to strengthen this place, standing on the border of the Arabian Desert, and in the South bound of Canaan. It has now the name of Ibelin *Gibelin*. The other Cities of Simeon which are named in the places of Joshua, and of the Chronicles, above noted, because they help us nothing in story, I omit them. In the time of Hezekiah *Ezekia* King of Judah, certain of this Tribe being straitened in their own Territories, passed to * Gedor, as it is 1. Chron. 4. 39.( the same place which Josh.. 15. 36. is called Gedera and Gederothaima) which at that time was inhabited by the issue of Ham *Cham*: where they seated themselves: as also five hundred others of this Tribe, destroyed the Relics *Reliques* of Amalek *Amalec* in the Mountains of Edom, and dwelt in their places.

## p02308 · sig. 242a

The Mountains within this Tribe are few, and that of Sampson the chiefest: unto which he carried the Gate-post of Gaza. The Rivers *Riuers* are Besor, and the Torrent of Egypt called Shihor *Shichar*, as is noted in Asher *Asser*.
