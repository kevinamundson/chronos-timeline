## p02309 · sig. 242b

§. III. *THE TRIBE OF JUDA* *JVDA*.

## p02310 · sig. 242b

OF Judah *Iuda* the fourth Son of Jacob *Iacob* by Lea, there were multiplied in Egypt *Aegypt* 74600. all which( Caleb excepted) perished in the Deserts *Desarts*. And of their sons there entered the Land of Canaan 76500. bearing arms: Agreeable to the greatness of this number was the greatest Territory given, called afterward Judea *Iudaea*: within the bounds whereof were the portions allotted to Dan and Simeon included. And many Cities named in these Tribes, did first, as they say, belong unto the children of Judah: who had a kind of Sovereignty over them: as *Succoth, Cariathiarim, Lachish* *Lachis**, Bethsemes, Ziklag* *Tsiglag**, Beersheba, *and others. The multitude of people within this small Province( if it be meted by that ground given to this Tribe only) were incredible, if the witness of the Scriptures had not warranted the report. For when David *Dauid* numbered the people, they were found five hundred thousand fighting men.

## p02311 · sig. 242b

The Cities of Judah were many. But I will remember the chiefest of them: beginning with Arad, or Horma, which stands in the entrance of Judea from Idumea *Idumaea*: whose King first surprised the Israclites as they passed by the border of Canaan towards Moab, and took from them some spoils, and many prisoners: who being afterward overthrown by the Israelites, the sons of Kenites *Keni*, the Kinsmen of Moses, obtained a possession in that Territory: who before the coming of the Israelites, dwelt between Midian *Madian* and Amalek.

## p02312 · sig. 242b

Following this Frontier towards Idumea and the South, Ascensus Scorpionis, or Acrabbim is placed, the next to Arad: So called because of Scorpions, which are said to be in that place: from which name of *Acrabbim, Jerome* *Hierome** *thinks that the name of the Toparchy called Acrabattene *Acrabathena* was denominated: of which we have spoken in Manasseh *Manasses*. On the South side also of Judea they place the Cities of *Jagur* *Iagur**, Dimona, Adada, Cedes, Ashnah* *Ashna**, Ithnan* *Iethnam**, *and Asor or Chatsor, most of them Frontier Towns *Townes*.

## p02313 · sig. 242b

And then Ziph: of which there are two places So called: one besides this in the body of Judah, of which the Desert and Forrest adjoining took name: where David hid himself from Saul. After these are the Cities of *Hezron* *Esron**, Adar, Karkah *and Azmon *Asemona*, or Azmon, of no great fame.

## p02314 · sig. 242b

Turning now from Idumea towards the North, we find the Cities of *Dannah* *Danna**, Shemah, Amam*, the other Asor, or *Chatsor, Bealoth* *Behaloth*, and the two Socohs *Sochoes*: of all which see Josh.. 15. also Kerioth *Carioth* by Joshua *Iosua* c. 15. v. 25. called Kerioth: whence Judas *Iudas* the Traitor was called Is-Kerioth *Is-carioth*, as it were a man of Kerioth. Then Etam *Hetham* the abode of Sampson which Rehoboam rebuilt. Beyond these towards the North border, and towards Eleutheropolis, is the City of Jattir *Iethar*, or Jattir, belonging to the Levites *Leuites*. In Saint Jerome's *Hieromes* time it was called Jattir: and inhabited altogether with Christians: near unto this City was that remarkable battle fought between Asa King of Judah, and Zerah *Zara* King of the Arabians, who brought into the field a Million of fighting men: and was notwithstanding beaten and put to flight: Asa following the victory as far as Gerar, which at the same time he recovered.

## p02315 · sig. 242b

Not far from Jether *Iether*, stands Jarmuth *Iarmuth*, whose King was slain by Joshua, and the City overturned. Next unto it is Mareshah *Maresa* the native City of the Prophet Micah *Michaea*: Between it and *Adullam* *Odolla**, Judas Maccabeus* *Macchabaeus** *overthrew Gorgias: and sent thence ten thousand Drachmas *Dragmas* of silver to be offered for Sacrifice.

## p02316 · sig. 242b

Adullam or Adullam itself was an ancient and magnificent City, taken by Joshua, and the King thereof slain. Jonathas *Ionathas* Maccabeus beautified it greatly. Then Keilah *Ceila* or Keilah afterward Keilah, where David sometime hid himself: and which afterward he delivered from the assaults of the Philistines *Philistims*: near which the Prophet Habakkuk *Abacuc* was buried: whose monument remained and was seen by St. Jerome.

## p02317 · sig. 243a

Near it is Hebron sometime called *the City of Arbah*, for which the Vulgar has Kiriath-arba *Cariatharbe*: the reason of this name they give as if it signified the City of four: because the four *Patriarchs, Adam, Abraham, Isaac, *and Jacob, were therein buried, but of Adam it is but supposed: and it is plain by the places Joshua 14. 14. and 15. 13. and 20. 11. that Arbah here does not signify four, but that it was the name of the Father of the Giants called Anakim, whose son as it seems Anak was: and *Achiman, Sheshai*, and Talmai( whom Caleb expelled Josh.. 15. ) were the sons of this *Anak, Num*. 13. 23. The name of Anak signifies Torquem, a chain worn for ornament: and it seems that this Anak enriched by the spoils which himself and his Father got, wore a chain of gold, and So got this name: and leaving the custom to his posterity, left also the name: So that in Latine the name of Anakim may not amiss be expounded by Torquati.

## p02318 · sig. 243a

The City Hebron was one of the 〈◊ 〉 Cities of Canaan, built seven years before Zoan *Tsoan* or Tanis in Egypt: and it was the head and chief City of the Anakim *Anakims*, whom Caleb expelled: to whom it was in part given, to wit, the Villages adjoining, and the rest to the Levites. It had a Bishop in the Christian times, and a magnificent Temple built by Helen the Mother of Constantine.

## p02319 · sig. 243a

Not far hence they find Eleutheropolis or the free City, remembered often by St. Jerome. Then Eglon whose King Dabir associated with the other four Kings of the Amorites, to wit, of *Jerusalem* *Hierusalem**, Hebron, Jarmuth*, and Lachish, besieging the 〈◊ 〉, were by Joshua utterly overthrown. From hence the next City of fame was Emaus, afterward Nicopolis, one of the Cities of Government or Praesidencies of Judea. In sight of this City Judas Maccabeus( after he had formerly beaten both Apollonius and Seron) gave a third overthrow to Gorgias Lieutenant to Antiochus.

## p02320 · sig. 243a

In the year 1301. it was over-turned by an earth-quake, says Eusebius. In the Christian times it had a Bishops seat of the Diocesse of Caesarea *Caesaria* of Palaestine.

## p02321 · sig. 243a

From Emaus toward the West Sea there are the Cities of *Naamah* *Nahama**, Bethdagon*, and Gederah *Gader* or Gedera, or Gederothaima, of which and of Gederoth * Josh.. 15. v. 36. & 41. Then Azekah *Azecha*, to which Joshua followed the slaughter of the five Kings before named, a City of great strength in the Valley of a Terebinth or Turpentine, as the Vulgar reads 1. Sam.. 17. 2. whence( as it seems) they seat it near unto Socoh *Soco*, and unto Lebna of the Levites. It revolted from the subjection of the Jews *Iewes* while Joram *Ioram* the son of Jehoshaphat *Iosaphat* ruled in Jerusalem: And next unto this stands Makkedah *Maceda*, which Joshua utterly dispeopled.

## p02322 · sig. 243a

On the other side of 〈◊ 〉 towards the East stands Bethsur, otherwise Beth-zur *Bethsora*, and Beth-zur: one of the strongest and most sought for places in all Judah. It is seated on a high Hill; and therefore called *Bethsur( the house on the Rock, *or of strength) It was fortified by Roboam, and afterward by *Judas Maccabeus. Lysias *forced it, and Antiochus Eupator by famine: Jonathas regained it: and it was by Simon exceedingly fortified against the Syrian Kings.

## p02323 · sig. 243a

Bethleem is the next unto it within six miles of Jerusalem, otherwise Bethlehem *Lehem*: sometime Ephrathah *Ephrata*; which name, they say, it had of Calebs wife, when as it is So called by Moses before Caleb was famous in those parts Gen. 38. 16. Of this City was Ibzan *Abessan* or *Ibzan, Judge of Israel*, after Jephtah *Iephtah*, famous for the thirty Sons and thirty Daughters, begotten by him. Elimelech *Elimelec* was also a Bethlehemite, who with his wife 〈◊ 〉 sojourned in Moab during the famine of Judah in the time of the Judges *Iudges*, with whom Ruth the daughter in law of Naomi returned to Bethleem: and married Booz, of whom Obed, of whom Ishai, of whom David. It had also the honour to be the native City of our Saviour Jesus *Iesus* Christ; and therefore shall the memory thereof never end.

## p02324 · sig. 243a

In Zebulun *Zabulon* of Galilee there was also a City of the same name: and therefore was this of our Saviour called Bethleem Judah.

## p02325 · sig. 243a

From Bethleem some four or five mile stands Thecua, the City of Amos the Prophet: and to this place adjoining is the City of Beth-zechariah *Bethzacaria*, in the way between Beth-zur and Jerusalem: on whose Hills adjoining the glorious gilt shields of Antiochus shined like lamps of fire in the eyes of the lewes. The City of * Bezek was also near unto Bethleem, which Adoni-bezek-Bezek *Adoni-bezec* commanded; who had during his reign tortured 70. Kings, by cutting off the joints of their Fingers and Toes: and made them gather bread under his Table: but at length the same end befell himself by the sons of Judah, after they had taken him prisoner.

## p02326 · sig. 243b

The rest of the Cities in this part( most of them of no great estimation) we may passe by until we come to the magnificent Castle of Herodium, which Herod erected on a Hill, mounting thereunto with 200. Marble steps, exceeding beautiful and strong. And towards the Dead Sea, and adjoining to the Desert of 〈◊ 〉, between it and Tekoa, is that 〈◊ 〉 floridus ("flowery; blooming"), where in the time of Jehoshaphat, the Jews stood and looked on the *Moabites, Ammonites*, and Edomites, massacring one another, when they had purposed to join against Judah: near which place is the Valley of blessing, where the Jews the fourth day after, solemnly came and blessed God for So strange deliverance.

## p02327 · sig. 243b

Now the Cities of Judah which border the Dead Sea, are these; 〈◊ 〉 beautified by Roboam: and Tsohar which the 〈◊ 〉 calls * Zoar *Segor*: So called because Lot in his 〈◊ 〉 for it urged that it was but a little one: whence it was called Tsohar, which signifies a little one: when as the old name was Belah, as it is Gen. 14. 2. In the Romans times it had a Garrison, and was called( as they say) Pannier: in Jerome's time Balezona. Then Engaddi or Hen-gaddi, first Hazazon-tamar *Asasonthamar*: near unto which are the Gardens of Balsamum, the best that the World had called 〈◊ 〉: the most part of all which Trees Cleopatra Queen of Egypt sent for out of Judea, and Herod who either feared or loved Anthonie her husband, caused them to be rooted up and presented unto her: which she replanted near Heliopolis in Egypt. This City was first taken by Chedorlaomer, and the Amorites thence expelled. It was one of the most remarkable Cities of Judea: and one of the Praesidencies thereof.

## p02328 · sig. 243b

The rest of the Cities are many in the In-land, and among them Jezreel *Iesrael*; not that which was the City of Naboth, of which already: but another of the same name, the City of Ahinoam *Achinoan*, the wife of David, the mother of that Ammon, whom 〈◊ 〉 slew: also as some think the City of *Amasa, Absaloms *Lieutenant, and the commander of his Army. But this seems to be an error grounded upon the nearness of the words, Israel and Jezreel: and because the 2. Sam.. 17. 25. Amasa's *Amasaes* father is called a Israelite, who first of the Chron. 2. 17. is called an 〈◊ 〉: indeed the Hebrew Orthography shows that Amasa's Father is not said to be of the City Jezreel, but an Israelite in Religion, though otherwise an Ishmaelite.

## p02329 · sig. 243b

In this Tribe there were many high Hills or Mountains *Mountaines*, as those of Engaddi upon the Dead Sea: and the Mountains of Judah, which begin to rise by Emaus, and end near Taphna, and these part Judah from Dan and Simeon. Of others which stand single there is that of Hebron: at the foot whereof was that Oak of Mamre *Mambre*, where the three Angells appeared to Abraham, which St. Jerome calls a Fir-tree; and says, that it stood till the time of Constantine the younger *yonger*. There is also that Mountain called Collis Achillae, on the South side of Ziph: on the top whereof the great Herod, enclosing the old Castle, erected by Jonathas 〈◊ 〉, and called Masada *Massada*, garnished it with seven and twenty high and strong Towers: and therein left Armour and furniture for an hundredth thousand men; being as it seems a place inaccessible, and of incomparable strength.

## p02330 · sig. 243b

In the Valley afterward called the Dead Sea, or the Lake Asphaltitis, this Country had four Cities, *Admah* *Adama**, Sodom, Zeboiim* *Seboim*, and Gomorra, destroyed with fire from Heaven for their unnatural sins.
