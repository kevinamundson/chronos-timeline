# Book I, Part I, Chapter VIII, §. 13

## p01329 · sig. 129b

§. XIII. *Of the sons of *CUSH *CHVSH*( excepting Nimrod) *of whom hereafter*.

## p01330 · sig. 129b

The sons of Cush were, Seba, Havila *Hauila*, Sabta, Raamah *Raama*, And the sons of Raamah were, Sheba, and Dedan. Sabteca, Nimrod.

## p01331 · sig. 129b

†. I. *That the most of the Sons of *CUSH *were seated in Arabia the Happy: and of the Sabaeans that robbed *JOB *IOB*: *and of the Queen that came to *Solomon *SALOMON*.

## p01332 · sig. 129b

SEBA or Sheba *Saba* was the eldest son of Cush, the eldest son of Ham, to make a difference between Him and his Nephew Sheba, the son of his brother Raamah, or Regma( or Ragma after Montanus) his name is written with a single *( (S) Samekh* *Samech*, and Sheba the son of Regma with an( S) aspirate, which is the *Hebrew Shin* *Schin**. Seba *the eldest son of *Cush, Regma *his brother, and Sheba the son of Regma possessed-both the shores of *Arabia Felix* *Foelix**. Sheba *took that part toward the Red Sea, as nearest his Father Cush, and the Land of the *Cushites* *Chusites**: Regma *and Sheba the East coast of the same Arabia, which looks into the Gulf of Persia; of which Pliny *PLINIE*: *Sabaeans* *Sabaei** Arabum populi propter thura clarissimi ad utraque maria porrect is gentibus habitant; The Sabaeans people of Arabia, famous for their Frankincense, extending their Nations, dwell along both the Seas, *( to wit) the *Persian, and the Arabian*, or Red Sea. This Country was afterwards called *Arabia, a populi mixtione* ("Arabia, from the mixture of the people."), says Postellus. To this agrees Ptolemy *Ptolemie*, who sets the City of Sheba towards the Arabian or Red Sea, and the City Rhegama towards the Persian, with whom also we may leave Sabta: for So much Montanus gathers out of Ptolemy, because he remembers a Nation( called Stabaei) near the Persian Sea; and Massabathae which descended of them. But Montanus has sent Regma, or( as he calls him Rahma) into Carmania, for which I see no reason. Josephus *Iosephus*, who only attended his own fancies, has banished Sheba or Seba to the border of Ethiopia *Aethiopia*. But Beroaldo *Beroaldus* thinks it strange, that the Sabaeans, which stole away Jobs *Iobs* Cattle, should run through all Egypt, and all Arabia Petraea, and find out Job in Trachonitis *Traconitis*, between Palestine *Palaestina* and Coelesyria 1200. miles off. Now, as this conjecture was more than ridiculous, So do I think, that neither the Sabaeans on the Red Sea, nor those toward the Persian Sea, could by any means execute the stealth upon Job, which soever Beroaldo shall take for nearest. But these were the Sabaeans of Arabia the Desert, where Guilandinus Melchior affirms out of his own experience, that the City Sheba is seated: the same which Ptolemy calls Save, now Semiscasac: and from this Sheba in Arabia the Desert, came those Magi or Wise men which worshipped Christ, says Melchior, whose words are these: *The Magi came neither out of Mesopotamia*( as *Chrysostom* *Chrysostome**, Jerome* *Hierome*, and Ambrose supposed) *nor out of Arabia the Happy, as many wise men do believe, but out of Sheba in Arabia the Desert: which City when my self was there, was( as I judged it) called Semiscasac. *And to approve this opinion of Guilandinus, it appears that these Shabaei were neighbours to Job, and lay fit to invade and rob him. For both the other Nations( as well those on the Persian Sea, as those on the Red Sea) are So disjoined with large Deserts *Desarts*, as there is no possibility for strangers to passe them, especially with any numbers of Cattle, both in respect of the mountains, of the sands, and of the extreme want of water in those parts: *Ubi nec homines, nec bestiae videntur, nec aves, 〈◊ 〉 nec arbors, nec germen aliquod, sed non nisimontes saxosi, altissimi, asperrimi; Where there are found neither men, nor beasts, no not So much as birds* ("Where neither men nor beasts are seen, nor birds, [illegible] nor trees, nor any shoot, but only rocky mountains, very high, very rough.")*,* *or trees, nor any pasture or grass, but only sharp, and high stony, and craggy mountains. Beroaldo *and Pereira *Pererius* conceive, that the Queen of Sheba which came to visit Solomon, was of the Sabaeans on the East side of Arabia Felix; but the contrary seems more probable, and that she was Queen of Sheba towards the Red Sea: for Solomon at that time commanding all that part of Arabia Petraea, twixt Idumea *Idumaea* and the Red Sea, as far down as Midian or Midian *Madian*, and Ezion Gaber: and this Queen of Sheba which inhabited the West part of Arabia Felix, being his neighbour, might without any far travail enter his Territories, free from all danger of surprise by any other Prince or Nation.

## p01333 · sig. 130a

But to avoid tediousness, it is manifest that Seba or *Sheba, Sabta, Raamah*, or Regma, with his Sons Sheba and Dedan, and Sabteca, were all the possessors of Arabia the Happy, and the Desert: only Havilah *Hauilah* and Nimrod dwelt together on the East side Cush, who held Arabia Petraea. Now for Sabta, there is found of his name the City of Sabbatha or Sabota in the same Arabia: of which both Pliny and Ptolemy; who withal names Sabotale, within the walls whereof there were sometimes found sixty Temples. Ezekiel *Ezechiel* joins the Father and the Son together, The Merchants *of Sheba and Raamah were thy Merchants. *And that they were the Eastern Arabians their merchandise witnesses, formerly repeated in the Chapter of Paradise. For Josephus his fancies, that Sheba was the parent of the Ethiopians *Aethiopians* about Meroe, and Sabta of the Ethiopians Astabari, they be not worthy any farther answer then has already been given: especially seeing these Cities *Citties* preserving the memory of the names of Sheba, and of Sabta in Arabia were yet remaining in Ptolemy's *Ptolomtes* time, though in some letters changed. As also in the coasts adjoining, the names of other of the brethren of the family of Cush, with little alterations are preserved. In Arabia the Desert is found the City Sheba or Save, ( now Semiscasac) with the City of Rhegana for Rhegma; and the Nation by Ptolemy himself called Raabeni of Raamah. In Arabia the Happy is found the City of Rhegama, and Rabana; which also keeps the sound of Rhegma, the City of Saptha or Saptah, not far from the East coast of Arabia; as also the Metropolis and chief City in the body of the South part of Arabia, called without difference or alteration Sabatha; and to the West of Sabatha towards the Red Sea the great City of Sheba; and the Nation adjoining, Sabaeans: & to the South thereof again toward the strait entrance of the Red Sea, the Region of Sabe. To all these his Brothers and Nephews *Nephewes* which were seated on the East side of *Arabia, Havilah *by the passage of Tigris was a neighbour, to whom he might passe by Boat even unto Rhegma the City of Raamah, or Rhegma, set near the River of Lar towards the mouth of the Persian Sea, which stood in Ptolemies time.

## p01334 · sig. 130b

†. II. JOSEPHUS *his opinion of *DEDAN, *one of the issue of *CUSH *to have been seated in the West Ethiopia, disproved out of *EZECH: and Jeremiah *HIEREMIE*.

## p01335 · sig. 130b

And whereas Josephus( whom in this S. Jerome follows, as not curious herein) sent Dedan the son of Raamah into West Ethiopia, it is strange that Ezekiel should couple *Sheba, Raamah*, and Dedan together; Dedan in the 15. Verse, and Sheba, and Raamah in the 22. Verses to be the Merchants of Tyre, if Dedan had dwelt in West Ethiopia, which is distant from Raamah and Sheba( the habitation of his father and brother) above 4000. miles. Besides which the merchandise that the Dedanites brought to Tyre does not make them naked Black-mores. For they of Dedan( says Ezekiel) were thy Merchants in precious clothes for thy Charriots; and these Western Ethiopians never saw cloth, till the Portugals seeking those Coasts traded with them: the merchandise of the Country being Hides, Elephants teeth, some Gold and Amber, Civet Cats, and Rice, but nothing at all of any manufacture: and all these they exchanged for linen, or iron chiefly.

## p01336 · sig. 130b

But in those days the West part of Africa within the body of the land was known only by imagination: and, being under the burnt Zone was held uninhabitable. And therefore that the Negroes *Negros* of the West Ethiopia, which inhabit about Sierra *Serra* Leone *Liona*, or Niger, could either passe by Sea or Land to Tyre in the bottom of the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea, were a strange, or rather a foolish fancy. Now to put it out of dispute that Dedan also dwelt by the rest of the children of Cush, which seats they held by that name in the time of Jeremiah the Prophet, let us hear Jeremiah his own words: *Fly, ye Inhabitants of Dedan, for I have brought the destruction of *Esau *ESAV* upon him. Hereby it appears that Dedan was a Neighbour to the Idumeans *Idumaeans*: and Idumea is a Province of Arabia Petraea: and Dedan which dwelt on the North part of Arabia Felix, joined in that part to Petraea, the seat of his Grand-father Cush; which neighbourhood and fellowship of Dedan and the Idumeans, is also confirmed by Ezekiel: *I will stretch out mine hand upon Edom, and destroy Man and Beast out of it, and I will make it desolate from Teman: and they of Dedan shall sal by the sword.*
