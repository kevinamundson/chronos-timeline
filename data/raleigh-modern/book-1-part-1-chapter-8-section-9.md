§. IX. Of the four Sons of JAVAN *IAVAN*: and of the double signification of Tarshish *Tharsis*, either for a proper name or for the Sea.

## p01258 · sig. 120a

OF Elisa or Elishah *Elipha*, came the Aeoles: and of this Elisa all the Greeks *Greekes* were called Hellenes, says *Montanus. Melanchthon* *Melanchton** *makes Elisa the Father of the Aeoles in Asia side: others of Elis in Peloponnesus, or of both. And seeing the Greeks were descended in general of Javan, it is probable that the Aeoles and the Elei, took name of Elisa, his eldest Son. Ezekiel *Ezechiel* in the 27. speaking of Tyre, names the Isles of *Elisa. Hyacynthus* *& purpur a de insulis Elisae facta sunt operimentum tuum: Blue Silk and Purple, brought from the Isles of Elisa, was thy covering: *The Chaldeans *Chaldaeans* for Elisa write Italia: but the Vulgar, the Tigurine, the Geneva, and Junius *Iunius*, keep the word Elisa: and So I think they might do with reason. For there was not found any such Purple Dye in Italy *Italie* in those days nor since, that I can read of: but those Isles of Elisa, were by a better conjecture the Isles of Greece; and the best Purple was found afterward at Tyre it self e and before that, among the Cyclades, and on the Coast of Getulia.

## p01259 · sig. 120b

Tarshish, the second Son of Javan, inhabited Cilicia, of which Tarshish is the *Metropolis. Montanus *for Tarshish in Cilicia, understands Carthage in Africa; but( reserving the respect due to So learned a man) he was much mistaken in that conjecture. The Chaldean *Chaldaean* Paraphrast puts Carthage for Tarshish, but it has no authority, nor warrant of reason therein. So likewise, where it is written, that the Ships of Solomon *Salomon* went every three years to Tarshish, and brought thence Gold, Silver, Elephants teeth, &c. the Chaldean Paraphrast translates *Tarshish( Africa. *) But Salomons Ships were prepared in the Red Sea at Esion Gaber, in the Bay of Elana, near unto Midian *Madian*, where *Jethro( Moses *Father in Law) inhabited; a Province of *Arabia Petraea, Idumea* *Idumaea*, or of the Cushites *Chusites*; and they sailed to the higher part of the East India. For it had been a strange Navigation to have spent three years in the passage between Judea *Iudaea* and Carthage, or any other part of Africa, which might have been sailed in six or ten days. And if So great riches might have been found within the bounds of the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea, all other neighbouring Princes would soon have entertained that Trade also. But this enterprise of Solomon is in this sort written of in the first of Kings: Also King Solomon *Made a Navy of Ships in Esion Gaber, which is beside Elath and the brink of the Red Sea in the Land of Edom: and *HYRAM *sent with the Navy his servants, that were Mariners, and had knowledge of the Sea, with the servants of *Solomon: *and they came to Ophir, and fetched from thence *420. *Talents of Gold, &c. *But as the Nations about Pontus thought no Sea in the World like unto their own, and doubted whether there were any other Sea but that only: ( whereof it came, that Pontus was a word used for the Sea in general) So, because the Israelites and the Phoenicians knew no other Sea then that of the Mediterranean in the beginning; and that the people of Tarshish had the greatest Ships, and were the first Navigators *Nauigators* in those parts with such Vessels, they were therefore called men of the Sea: and the word Tarshish used often for the Sea. And whereas it is said that the Ships of Solomon went every three years to Tarshish, that phrase is not strange at all; for we use it ordinarily wheresoever we navigate, ( namely) that the Kings Ships are gone to the Sea, or that they are set out every year, or every three year to the Sea, and therefore Tarshish was not therein named, either for *Carthage, Africa*, or India, but used for the Sea it self. But in this place Tarshish is truly taken for Tarshish, the chief City in Cilicia, founded by Tarshish the second son of Javan, or by his Successors *Successours* in memory of their first parent. To this City arrived *Alex. Macedon*, before he gave the first overthrow to Darius, and casting himself into the River to bathe and wash his body, he fell into an extreme Fever, and great danger of death: and in this City of Tarshish was Saint Paul borne. Now this agrees with the reason and nature of a Plantation. For( Gomer and his other sons inhabiting Asia the less, and that part of Syria adjoining) Javan, who was to passe over the Sea into Greece, took the edge of the same Coast, and first planted the Jones on that shore: gave the Islands between Asia the less and Greece, to Elisa, and left Tarshish upon the Sea-side in Cilicia; of whom that City took name.

The third son of Javan was Kittim *Cethim*, of whom were the Romans and Italians, says 〈◊ 〉, but I allow better of Melanchthon's *Melanchtons* opinion, who makes Kittim the Father of the *Macedonians. Kittim *is a voice plural( says he) and signifies percussors, though in that respect it may be meant by either. But it seems more probable, that the place of Isa *Esai*.. 23. ( according to Melanchthon) had relation to Alexander and the *Macedonians: Hac calamitas ab *Isa. *praedicta est, quicapite vicessimo tertio inquit, 〈◊ 〉 esse eversores Tyriex terra Cittim, This calamity*( says Melanchthon) *was foreshowed by *Isa. *the Prophet, who in the three and twentieth Chapter pronounced, that the Destroyers of Tyre were to come out of Cittim. *And although the children of Israel esteemed all men Islanders, which came unto them by Sea, and separate from that Continent; ( and So also Cittim might be taken for Italy, says Beroaldo *Beroaldus*) yet we must take the first performance of the former Prophesy, which took effect with the destruction of the Tyrians by Alexander, who after seven Months *Moneths* siege entered that proud City, and cut in pieces 7000. principal Citizens; strangled 2000. and changed the freedom of 13000. others into bondage and slavery. Now, that Macedon was taken for Kittim, it appears plainly in the first of the Maccabees, in these words. After that ALEXANDER *the Macedonian, the Son of *PHILIP, *went forth of the Land of Kittim, and slew *DARIVS *King of the Persians and Medes. *JOSEPHUS *IOSEPHVS* sets Kittim in the Isle of Cyprus, in which( says he) there remains the City Citium, the Country of Zeno the Philosopher( witness Laertius) which City Pintus upon Ezekiel affirms, that it stood in Saint Jerome's *Hicromes* time. So it may be that all the Islands in ancient times by the Hebrews *Hebrewes* were called the Islands of Kittim: and in that sense might Cyprus be So called also; and yet because Tarshish was the very next Port to Cyprus, and directly over against it, it is also very probable, that Kittim dwelt by his brother Tarshish: and finding that Island too strait for his people after they were increased, and that the rest of the Coasts, both on Asia side and Greece, were inhabited by his Father and Brothers, he sent Colonies over the Aegaean Sea, and inhabited Macedonia.

Dodanim the fourth son of Javan, and the youngest Brother( by the most opinions) sate down at Rhodes, as near *Kittim, Tarshish*, and Elisa, as he could. For Dodanim and Rhodanim are used indifferently by many Translators: the *Hebrew( D*) and the *Hebrew( R*) are So like, as the one may easily be taken for the other, as all Hebraists *Hebricians* affirm. There is also found in Epirus the City of Dodona, in the Province of Molossia. And as Kittim, when he wanted soil in Cyprus: So Dodanim( seated in a far less Island) did of necessity send his people farther off; and keeping along the Coast, and finding Peloponnesus in the possession of Elisa, he passed a little farther on the Westward, and planted in Epirus. And though the City of Dodona was not then built, or( perchance) not So ancient as Dodanim himself, yet his Posterity might give it that name in memory of their first parent, as it happened all the World over. For names were given to Cities, Mountains *Mountaines*, Rivers *Riuers*, and Provinces *Prouinces*, after the names of Noahs children, and grand-children; not in all places by themselves, but by their successors many years after: every of their Families being desirous to retain among them by those memories, out of what branch themselves were taken, and grafted elsewhere. And because great Kingdoms *Kingdomes* were often by new Conquerers newly named, and the greatest Cities often fired and demolished: therefore those that hoped better to perpetuate their memories, gave their own names, or the names of their Ancestors, to Mountains and Rivers, as to things( after their judgements) freest from any alteration.

Thus then did Javan settle himself and his children, in the edge and frontier of Asia the less, towards the Sea-shore: and afterward in Greece, and the Islands, and neighbour Provinces thereof, as Japheth *Iapheth* their Father had done in the body of the lesser Asia, together with Javans *Iauans* brethren, *Gomer, Magog, Madai, Tubal, Meshech* *Mesech**, *and the rest round about him. And in like sort did 〈◊ 〉( the son of Ham *Cham*) people *Babylonia* *Bahylonia**, Chaldea* *Chaldaea*, and the borders thereof towards the West and Southwest: and the sons of Cush *Chush*( all but Nimrod, who held Babylonia itself) travailed Southward in Arabia Felix *foelix*, and Southwestward into Arabia Petraea: the rest of his children holding the Regions adjoining to *Nimrod. Mizraim *the brother of Cush in like manner took the way of Egypt *Aegypt*: and his brother Canaan the Region of Palestine *Palestina* adjoining. The Sons of Canaan had their portions in Canaan, of whom all those Nations came, which were afterward the Enemies both to the Hebrews, and to those of the sons of Shem, which spread themselves towards the West, and the borders of the Mediterranean Sea: of which I shall speak hereafter.
