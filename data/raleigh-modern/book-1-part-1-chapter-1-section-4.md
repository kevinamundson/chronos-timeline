§. IIII. *Of the meaning of the words Heaven and Earth, Genesis *2. 1.

## p00812 · sig. 45a

The universal matter of the World( which Moses *Moyses* comprehends under the names of *Heaven and Earth*) is by divers diversely understood: for there are that conceive, that by those words, was meant the first matter, as the Peripatetikes understand it, to which, S. Augustine and 〈◊ 〉 seem to adhere. 〈◊ 〉 mundum( says S. Augustine) *de materia informi, quam fecisti de nulla re, pen nullam rem* ("concerning formless matter, which you made out of nothing, by means of nothing:")*: *( that is) *Thou hast made the World of a matter without form; which matter thou made of nothing, and being made, it was little other than nothing.*

## p00813 · sig. 45a

But this potential and imaginary materia prima, cannot exist without form. *Peter Lombard; the School-men* *Schoole-men**, Bede* *Beda**, Lyranus, Comestor, Tostatus *and others, affirm, that it pleased God first of all to create the Empyrean Heaven: which at the succeeding instant( says Bede and 〈◊ 〉) he filled with Angels. This Empyrean Heaven Steuchius Eugubinus calls *Divine clarity, and uncreated*: an error, for which he is sharply charged by Pereira *Pererius*, though( as I conceive) he rather failed in the subsequent, when he made it to be a place, and the seat of Angels, & just Souls *Soules*, then in the former affirmation: for of the first, That God lives in eternal light, it is written; My *soul, praise thou the Lord, that covers himself with light: *and in the Revelation; *〈◊ 〉 the City has no need of Sun, neither of the Moon to shine in it: for the glory of God didlight it. *And herein also John *Iohn* Mercer upon Genesis differs not in opinion from Eugubinus: for as by Heaven created in the beginning, was not meant the invisible or supercelestial, So in his judgement, because it was in all eternity, the glorious seat of God himself, it was not necessary to be created; *Quem mundum supercoelestem meo indicio creari* ("which supercelestial world, in my judgment, was created [first];")( says MERCER) *non erat necesse* ("it was not necessary").

## p00814 · sig. 45a

But as Moses forbore to speak of Angels, and of things invisible, and incorporate, for the weakness of their capacities, whom he then cared to inform of those things, which were more manifest, ( to wit) that God did not only by a strong hand deliver them from the bondage of Egypt, according to his promise made to their forefathers: but also that he created, and was the sole cause of this aspectable, and perceivable Universal; So on the other side I dare not think, that any supercelestial Heaven, or whatsoever else( not himself) was uncreated and eternal: and as for the place of God before the World created, the finite wisdom of mortal men has no perception of it, neither can it limit the seat of infinite power, no more than infinite power it self can be limited: for his place is in himself, whom no magnitude else can contain: *How great is the House of God*( says BARUCH *BARVCH*) *how large is the place of his possessions! it is* *great, and has no end, it is high and immeasurable.*

## p00815 · sig. 45a

But leaving multiplicity of opinion, it is more 〈◊ 〉 and allowed, that by the words, *Heaven and Earth*, was meant the solid matter 〈◊ 〉 substance, as well of all the Heavens, and Orbs *Orbes* supernal, as of the Globe of the Earth and Waters, which covered it over, ( to wit) that very matter of all things, *materia, Chaos, possibilitas, 〈◊ 〉 posse fieri. *Which matter( says Calvin *Caluin*) was So called, *quodtotius mundi semen fuerit* ("that it was the seed of the whole world"); *Because it was the seed of the Universal: *an opinion of ancient Philosophers long before.
