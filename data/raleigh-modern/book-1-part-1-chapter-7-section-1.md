§. I. Of Gods fore-warning: and some humane testimonies: and some doubting touching the truth of NOAHS Flood *Floud*.

## p01104 · sig. 94a

OF this destruction it pleased God to give warning unto Noah: who( says Josephus *Iosephus*) fearing to perish among the rest, *Secedens cum suis in aliam regionem migravit: He departed with his children, and travailed into another Region. *And of these Giants from whom Noah withdrew himself, Berosus writes in this manner; *That they exceeded in all sorts of inhumane and unnatural wickedness, *and that they were *contemptores & 〈◊ 〉 & Deorum, contemners of religion and of the Gods: *among which mighty men( says Berosus) *unus erat qui Deorum venerantior, & prudentior cunctis, &c. 〈◊ 〉 nomen erat* ("there was one who was more reverent toward the gods, and wiser than all, etc. [illegible] was his name")* *NOAH, *There was one more wise and 〈◊ 〉 the Gods then the rest, whose name was *NOAH: who with his three sons *Shem* *Sem**, Japheth* *Iaphetus** *and Ham *Cham*, and with their Wives *Wiues*, and the Wife of Noah, ( namely Titea the great, *Pandora, Noela*, and Noegla) preserved themselves in the Ark. This Ark God commanded Noah to prepare: *And God said unto *NOAH, *Make thee an Ark of Pine-trees: thou shalt make Cabins* *Cabines** in* *the Ark, and shalt pitch it within, and without with Pitch. *For God made Noah to know that an end of all flesh was at hand, and that the graves of the rebellious and cruel Generations were already fashioned in the Clouds *Cloudes*, which soon after should swallow up and cover all living creatures, which breathed in the Air: Noah and his Family excepted.

## p01105 · sig. 94a

But this universal grave of waters, and general Deluge has not been received by all: for divine testimonies do not persuade all natural men to those things, to which their own reason cannot reach: *Dum obuoluta in obscuro verit as latet: Whilst* *the truth lies wrapped up in obscurity. *Many there are who have disputed against the universality of this overflowing, and have judged that this Flood of Noah fell but on some particular places and Kingdoms *Kingdomes*: moved So to think, because in elder ages there have been many other Floods *Flouds*( as they suppose) of that nature. Hereof Nicholaus 〈◊ 〉 writes in this manner, as his words are cited by JOSEPHUS: *Est super Minyadam excelsus mons in Armenia, qui Baris appellatur, in quo multos confugientes* ("There is above Minyas a high mountain in Armenia, which is called Baris, on which many fleeing [took refuge];") *sermoest diluvij tempore liberatos, & quendam simul in 〈◊ 〉 devectum in huius vertice haesisse, 〈◊ 〉 relliquias multo tempore conseruatas, qui fortasse is 〈◊ 〉, de quo etiam* ("there is a report that [those] delivered at the time of the flood, and that a certain [man] carried together in [illegible] stuck fast on the top of this [mountain], [and that] the remains were preserved for a long time, who perhaps is that [illegible], concerning whom also")* *MOSES *〈◊ 〉 Legislator scribit* ("[illegible] the Lawgiver writes."): thus far this Author. There is( says he) above Minyada( or the Country of *〈◊ 〉) an exceeding high Mountain in Armenia, called Baris: on which it is reported, that many having fled thither, were saved in the time of the Deluge: and that one was carried in an Ark, and rested upon the top of the Mountain, whereon there remained a long time after certain pieces thereof; and this might be the same, of which *MOSES *the Law-giver* *Law-giuer** of the Jews* *Iewes** makes mention. *And of this opinion were the Thalmudists( says Annius) that many Giants saved themselves upon Mount 〈◊ 〉.

But Berosus( who after Moses was one of the most ancient, howsoever he have been since deformed and corrupted) does in the substance of all agree with Moses as touching the general Flood, taking from thence the beginning of his History in these words: *Ante aquarum 〈◊ 〉 famosam, qua universus perijt Orbis, &c. Before that famous destruction of Waters, by which the World Universal perished: *witnessing withal, that Noah with his Wife Titea, and his three Sons with their Wives( in all eight persons) were only saved.
