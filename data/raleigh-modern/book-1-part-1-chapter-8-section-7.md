§. VII. Of JAVAN *IAVAN* the fourth son of JAPHETH *IAPHETH*: and of MESCH, of ARAM, and MESHECH of JAPHETH.

## p01245 · sig. 118a

OF Javan the fourth son of Japheth came the Jones, which were afterwards called the Greeks *Greekes*: and So the Latine and Greek *Greeke* Interpreters for Javan write Greece, as in Isa *ESAT*.: *Et mittam ex 〈◊ 〉 qui 〈◊ 〉 fuerint ad gentes, in 〈◊ 〉, in Italiam, & Graeciam; And I will send those that escape of them to Nations in the Sea, in Italy* *Italie** and in Greece. *The Genevan *Geneus* here uses the word( Tarshish *Tarshich*) for Tarsus, a City in Cilicia, though Tarshish in many places be taken for the Sea. The Tigurine and the Geneva use the names Tubal and Javan, and not Italy and Greece: keeping the same Hebrew words. Of these Jones were the 〈◊ 〉, though themselves dream that they were 〈◊ 〉, or men without Ancestors *Ancesters*, and growing( as it were) out of the soil it self: who abounding in people sent Colonies into Asia the less, of whom came the Jones of those parts. Others derive the Athenians from Ion the son of Xuthus, the son of Deucalion; but the antiquity of Javan mars the fashion of that supposition, who So many years preceded *Xuthus, Ion*, or *Deucalion. 〈◊ 〉 *tells us that Xuthus stole out of Thessaly *Thessalie* with all his Fathers treasure, and his Brothers portions, and arriving at Athens, he was graciously received by Erechtheus *Erictheus*, who gave him his Daughter in marriage; of whom he received two sons, Ion, and Achaeus, the supposed Ancestors of the Athenians. For Attica was called Ionia( says Plutarch in the life of Theseus) who, when he had joined Megara to Attica, erected a pillar in that Isthmos or Strait, which fastens Peloponnesus to the other part of Greece: writing on that part which looks towards the East, these words: *Haec non sunt Peloponnesus, ast Ionia* ("These are not the Peloponnesus, but Ionia."); *These Countries are not of Peloponnesus, but of Ionia: *and on the other side which looked towards the South and into Peloponnesus, this: *These parts are Peloponnesus, and not Ionia.*

## p01246 · sig. 118b

Strabo out of Hecataeus affirms, that the Jones came out of Asia into Greece, which is contrary to the former opinion: That the Jones of Greece transporting certain companies into Asia the less, the name of Jones was thereby therein retained. And though Strabo knew no more thereof then he learned of the Greeks themselves, yet I find this conjecture of Hecataeus reasonable enough. For though it were to him unknown, yet sure I am that Asia the less had people before Greece had any: and that Javan did not fly from Babylonia into Greece, but took Asia the less in his passage; and from thence past over the nearest way, leaving his own name to some Maritime Province on that side, as he did to that part of Greece So called. But yet Strabo himself believed, that 〈◊ 〉 took the name from Ion the son of 〈◊ 〉: for So much he had learned from themselves; which was also the opinion of Pausanias. True it is, that the Greeks in after-times cast themselves into that part of Asia the less, opposite unto them, which they held for divers years. And howsoever the Greeks vaunt themselves to be the Fathers of Nations, and the most ancient; yet all approved Historians( not their own) deride and disprove their pride, and vanity therein. For this dispute of Antiquity( among profane Writers) rested between the 〈◊ 〉 and the Egyptians *Aegyptians*, as Justin *Iustine* out of Trogus, in the war between Vexoris of Egypt *Aegypt*, and Don *Tanais* of Scythia, witnesses: which preceded far the reign of Ninus, and was long before the name of Greece was ever heard of. And it is also manifest, that in Cecrops time the Greeks were all salvages without law or religion, living like brute beasts in all respects: and 〈◊ 〉( says S. Augustine) lived together with Moses.

The sixth son of Japheth was Meshech, whom the Septuagint cal Mosoch: ( a part of those Nations commanded by Gog the chief Prince of Meshech and Tubal. ) But this we must remember, that between Meshech *Mesech* the son of Aram, and Meshech( or Mosoch) the son of Japheth, there is little difference in name, and both by divers Interpreters diversely written. Montanus with the Vulgar writes Mesch, the son of *Aram, Mes*; the *Geneva, Mash; Junius* *Iunius**, Mesch. *But it may be gathered out of the 120. Psalm, that either Meshech the son of Japheth, was the parent of those people, or gave name to that Province wherein David *Dauid* hid himself: or else( which may rather seem) that it took name from Mesch the son of Aram. For David bewailing his exile( while he lived among a barbarous and irreligious People) uses these words: *Woe is me that I remain in Meshech, and dwell in the Tents of Kedar: *which Junius converts thus: *Hei mihi quia peregrinor tam 〈◊ 〉: habito tanquam Scenitae Kedareni: *The Septuagint gives it this sense: *Woe is me because my habitation*( or abode) *is prolonged, who dwell with the inhabitants of Kedar*; with which this of the Latine agrees: *Heu mihi, 〈◊ 〉 incolatus meus prolongatus est, 〈◊ 〉 cum habitantibus Kedar* ("Woe is me, [illegible] my sojourning is prolonged, [illegible] with the inhabitants of Kedar:")*: *The Chaldean *Chaldaean* otherwise, and in these words: *O me miserum, quia peregrinatus sum Asianis, habitavi cum taber 〈◊ 〉 Arabum; O wretch, that I am, for I have travailed among those of Asia: I have dwelt in the Tabernacles of the Arabians. *But howsoever or which soever conversion be taken for the best, yet all make mention of Kedar: which is a Province of Arabia Petraea; and the Chaldean puts Asia in stead of Meshech, but the Hebrew itself has Meshech. And if it be to be taken for a Nation, ( as it is most likely, because it answers to Kedar, the name of a Nation) seeing Mesh the son of Aram, 1. Chron. 17. is called Meshech, it is indifferent whether this Nation took name from Meshech or Mesh, both bordering Judea *Iudaea*, and like enough to be commanded by one Prince; for So Ezekiel *Ezechiel* makes Meshech and Tubal. But as for those that take Meshech out of the word Mosoch( given by the Septuagint) to be the 〈◊ 〉: sure they presume much upon the affinity of names, as aforesaid. And sure I am that David never travailed So far North; ( for to him Muscovia *Muscouia* was utterly unknown) but about the border of Kedar( it may be) he was often in all the time of his persecution: the same being a City on the Mountains *Mountaynes* of Sanir or Gilead *Galaad*. And yet Arias Montanus makes Mosoch the father of the Muscovians *Muscouians*: and herein also Melanchthon *Melanchton* runs with the tide of common opinion, and sets Meshech in 〈◊ 〉, though with some better advice of judgement; as, first seated in Cappadocia, and from thence travailing Northward: expounding the places of the 120. *Psalm, ( Hei 〈◊ 〉 quod exulo in Meshech) *to signify, *Gentis eius feritatem insignem esse; That the ferity of that Nation exceeded: *which fierceness or brutality of the *Muscovites* *Muscoutans**, David *never proved, or( perchance) never heard of. But the same ferity or cruelty which those Northern 〈◊ 〉 had, may as well be ascribed to the Arabians and Kedarens. For this Country took name of Kedar the second son of Ismael, of whom a people of equal fierceness to any of the World were begotten, both in those times and long after, even to this day( if the *Arabians, 〈◊ 〉*, and Saracens, may be accounted one people: ) the same being foreshowed by the speech of the Angel to *Hagar, Gen. *16. v. 12. *And he shall be a wild man: his hand shall be against every man, and every mans hand against him. *Now Arabia the Desert( says Pliny *Plinie*) confronts the Arabians Cochlei on the East, and the Cedraei Southward, both which join together upon the 〈◊ 〉. So it appears( as before) that *Meshech, Tubal, Gomer, Togarmah* *Togorma**, *and Magog, neighboured Canaan *Canan* and Israel, and that Kedar also did join to Meshech: all which were Regions of Syria, or of Asia the less, commanded by the Successors of Seleucus, enemies of the reestablishment of Israel and Judah *Iuda*. But( as I have already said) it might well be, that long after the first plantation the issue of Meshech( or Mosoch) might passe into Cappadocia, and thence into Hyrcania, and give names, both to 〈◊ 〉 in the one, and to the Mountains Moschici in the other, and from thence might send people more Northerly into 〈◊ 〉, and So all opinions saved. But all salvage Nations over-grown and uncultivated, do( for the most part) show a late plantation, even as Civility, Letters, and magnificent Buildings, witness antiquity.
