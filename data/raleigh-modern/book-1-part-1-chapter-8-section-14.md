## p01337 · sig. 130b

§. XIIII. *Of the issue of *MIZRAIM: *and of the place of *Jeremiah *HIEREMIE*, *Chap. 9. Verse. 7.*

## p01338 · sig. 130b

After Cush *Chush* it follows to speak of Mizraims sons, whose names( says Saint Augustine) were plural, to signify the Nations, which came of them. Ludim, the eldest son of Mizraim, was the father of the Lybians in Africa: and the rest of his brothers dispersed themselves into all Regions adjoining. Among the sons of Shem there is also Lud; but he is difference from Lud the son of Mizraim by the singular number: the son of Shem being written Lud, the son of *Mizraim, Ludim*: and yet these Names and Nations are often confounded, notwithstanding the apparent difference both of Names and Nations. For that Ludim the son of Mizraim was the parent of the Lybians in Africa, and that he was seated not far from Mizraim his father, appears by the Prophet Jeremiah, who joins them in this sort together: *Come up ye* *Horses, and rage ye Charriots, and let the valiant men come forth, the Black-moors* *Black-moores**, and the Lybians which bear the shield: *for those Nations assisted the Egyptians *Aegyptians* being of one parent descended. And in *Ezekiel* *Ezechiel**, Phut *and Lud are joined together. Ethiopia *Aethiopia*( or Cush) says Ezekiel c. 30. v. 3. And PHUT *PHVT* and LUD *LVD*, *and all the common people, and the men of the Land that are in league shall fall with them by the sword: *which is as much to say, as the sons of Cush; ( which were the Cushites *Chusites*) the sons of Mizraim, which were the Egyptians) and the Lybians( descended of his son Lud) with other the inhabitants of Egypt *Aegypt* and Africa shall fall together. Hierosolymitanus finds also in Africa a Nation of the Lydians. And I believe it: because Jeremiah joins the Lybians and Lydians together in the place before remembered. But Libya *Lybia* in Africa is by the Hebrews *Hebrewes* called Ludim( says Arias Montanus) though 2. Chron. 12. 3. they seem to be called Lubim or Lubaei, a name somewhat nearer the word Lybies, and by which it may seem that the truer writing is, not Libyans *Libyes*, but Lybies. Neither is it here to be omitted, that Pintus( upon the thirtieth of Ezekiel) understands that which is spoken in the third Verse of Lud, not to be meant of the Libyans at all: for he will have this threatening to be meant against the people of Lydi, a City( says he) between Egypt and Palestine *Palaestina*, which opinion I could not dislike if the City of Lyda were So seated. But Lyda( which should be written Lydda with a double D, and is the same City which was afterward Diospolis, in which S. Peter cured Aeneas of the palsy) stands near the Torrent 〈◊ 〉, not far from Joppa *Ioppe* the Port of Jerusalem *Hierusalem*. Yet it is not impossible but that this City might have Lud for the Founder. For there are many Cities of one name founded in all the Regions of the World, and far asunder; as after the names of *Alexander, Seleucus*, and Antiochus, many Cities called *Alexandria, Seleucia*, and Antiochia, So of divers others. S. Jerome *Hierome* makes Lehabim to be the Father of Libya, who was the third son of Mizraim: and So does Postellus; and either opinion may be true.

The rest of Mizraims sons have no proper Countries given them in the Scriptures, saving 〈◊ 〉 and Caphtorim, of whom came the Philistines *Philistims*, whom the Scriptures call Peleset:

These 〈◊ 〉 inhabited Cassiotis, a Region lying in the entrance of Egypt from Palestine, in which the Lake Sirbonis, and the Mountain Cassius are found: not far from whence Pompey was buried.

## p01341 · sig. 131a

Caphtorim seated near Casluhim *Cosluhim* in that Tract of Egypt called Sethroitis, not far from *Pelusium. Strabo *calls it *Sethroitis; Stephanus *and *Pliny* *Plinie**, Sethroites*, of the City Sethron: which Ortelius takes to be the same which 〈◊ 〉 calls Hercules parua. Of the Casluhim and Caphtorim came the Philistines, which are called by the Septuagint Allophyli( which is) Alienigenae, Strangers, or of a strange kindred. These Philistines inhabited the South part of the holy Land towards Egypt, of whom Palestine took name. For the Hebrews( says Isidore) do not use the letter( P) but in stead of it( ph.) Their principal Cities were *Gaza, Ashkelon* *Ascalon**, Ashdod* *Azotus**, Gath* *Geth**, *and Accaron: and the people of them called *Gasaei, Ascalonitae, Azotij, Gethaei, *and *Accaronitae. Isidore *affirms, that Ashkelon was first called Philistine *Philistim*: and of that City the Country adjoining. But where Isidore had it, I know not.

## p01342 · sig. 131a

The first known King of the Philistines was that Abimelech, who had a liking to Abrahams wife; with whom Abraham made a covenant and league. This Abimelech dwelt indeed at this time in Gerar; but it is written that he was also King of the Philistines, in these words: Wherefore ISAAC went to ABIMELECH *King of the* *Philistines unto Gerar*. Now in regard that this or some ancienter Abimelech governed the commonwealth greatly to his glory, the rest of his successors called themselves by the same name. The Philistines commanded that Tract of land upon the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea to the Northward, from the Castle of Pilgrims( otherwise Caesarea Palestine, or Straton's *Stratons* Tower) which was the South-border of Phoenicia, to Gaza *Gasa*, or to the River of Egypt. The Anakim *Anakims* or strong Gyants were of these Philistines: and Goliath *Goliah* was of Gath one of the five Cities above named. They had sometimes five Kings, says Lyranus. They mastered the Israelites at several times above 150. years, and kept them Tributaries, till they were weakened by Sampson and Samuel, but in the end this yoke was taken off by David *Dauid*, and laid on themselves.

## p01343 · sig. 131b

It is objected, that because these Cities and the Countries adjoining, were held by the sons of Mizraim, therefore did the Israelites dispossess the sons of Mizraim, and not of Canaan, by forcing those places.

## p01344 · sig. 131b

To this says Pereira *Pererius*, that although the Philistines or Philistines held it in the time of Joshua *Iosua*, yet at the time of the promise it was possessed by the Canaanites; as in the second of Deuteronomy. The Hevites *Heuites* dwelt in the Villages unto Gaza. And what marvel, if( the Canaanites being the greater part) the denomination were from them? For that the Philistines were of Caphtor, and So of Mizraim and not of Canaan, besides Moses the Prophet Jeremiah witnesses: *The Lord will destroy the Philistines,* *the remnant of the issue of Caphtor: *and in like manner in the ninth of Amos, the Philistines are said to be the relics of *Caphtorim; Have not I brought up *ISRAEL out *of the Land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor, and *ARAM from Kir? So I read this place with divers of the learned. For whereas the Vulgar has, & *Palaestinos de Cappadocia, & Syros de Cyrene* ("Palestinians from Cappadocia, and Syrians from Cyrene,")*, *this conversion Beroaldo *Beroaldus* condemns; where Caphtor is taken for Cappadocia, and Cyrene for Kir. For Cyrene is a City directly West from Egypt, between Ptolemais or Barce and Apollonia: but Kir in Asia under the *Assyrians: 〈◊ 〉 *has it Kir, and not Cyrene: and So has the Geneva. But Pereira calls Caphtorim Cappadocia, according to the Vulgar translation, to which he is bound: and yet it is not altogether improbable if he mean Cappadocia in Palestine and not that Cappadocia by the Sea Pontus in the North of Asia the less. For whether they inhabited 〈◊ 〉, or Cappadocia of Palestine, it is not certainly known. And sure in this manner he may expound Cappadocia to be ambiguous, as well as he does Cyrene: taking it here not for Cyrene in Africa, but for a place in Media. For it is written in the second of Kings, that Tiglath-Pileser *Teglatphalasser* King of the Assyrians carried away the Inhabitants of Damascus into Kir: and So Josephus *Iosephus* seems to understand this Kir, for Cyrene in Media, calling this *Cyrene, Media superior*: for it was the manner and policy of the Assyrians to transplant the people conquered by them, as they did the Samarians or Israelites, and other Nations. And hereof it came that Kir was called Syro-Media: because the Syrians by the Assyrians were therein captured.
