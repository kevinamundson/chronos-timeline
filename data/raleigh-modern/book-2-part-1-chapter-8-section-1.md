## p02172 · sig. 231b

§. I. *The bounds and chief Cities, and Founders, and Name, of this Kingdom: and of the invention of Letters ascribed to them.*

## p02173 · sig. 231b

BECAUSE these five Tribes, of *Asher* *Asser**, Naphtali* *Nephtalim**, Zebulun* *Zabulon**, Issachar, *and the half of Manasseh *Manasse*, possessed the better part of that ancient Kingdom of Phoenicia, to wit, of So much as lay to the South part of Anti-Lebanon *Anti-libanus*: I have therefore gathered a brief of those Kings which have governed therein: at least So many of them as time( which devours all things) has left to posterity: and that the rest have perished, it is not strange: seeing So many volumes of excellent learning in So long a race and revolution, and in So many changes of Estates and Conquests of Heathen Princes, have been torn, cast away, or otherwise consumed.

## p02174 · sig. 231b

The limits of this Kingdom, as touching the South parts, are very uncertain: but all Cosmographers do in effect agree, that it takes beginning from the North, where that part of Syria, which is called Casiotis, 〈◊ 〉: most of them bounding it by Orthosia, to the North of *Tripolis. Ptolemy* *Ptolomie** *makes it a little larger, as reaching from the River Eleutherus, that falls into the Sea at the Island of Aradus, somewhat to the North of Orthosia, and stretching from thence along the coast of the Mediterranean *Mediterran* sea, as far as the River of Chorseus, which seems to be that which the Jews *Iewes* call the Torrent, or River of *Maggeddo. Pliny* *Plinie** *extends it farther and comprehends Joppa *Ioppe* within it: Corvinus *Coruinus* and *Budaeus, Joppa *and *Gaza. Phoenicia apud priscos appellata* ("Gaza. Phoenicia, as it was called by the ancients")( says BUDAEUS *BVDAEVS*) *quae nunc Palestine* *Palaestina** Syriae dicitur; It was called Phoenicia of old* ("Which is now called Palestine of Syria.")( says he) *which now is called Palestine of Syria.*

## p02175 · sig. 231b

Strabo comprehends in this Country of Phoenicia, all the Sea side of Judea *Iudaea*, and Palestine, even unto 〈◊ 〉, the first Port of Egypt *Aegypt*. On the contrary Diodorus Siculus folds it up in Coelesyria, which he bounds not. But for my self I take a middle course, and like best of Ptolemies *Ptolomies* description, who was seldom deceived in his own Art. It had in it these famous Maritime Cities( besides all those of the Islands *Ilands*) to wit, *Aradus, Orthosia, Tripolis, Botrys, Byblus, Berytus, Sidon, Tyre, Ptolemais* *Ptolomais*( or Acre *Acon*. Dora, and Caesarea *Caesaria* Palestinae: and by reason of the many Ports and goodly Sea-towns *Sea-townes*, it anciently commanded the Trades of the Eastern World: and they were absolute Kings of the Mediterranean Sea.

## p02176 · sig. 232a

The ancient Regal Seat of those Princes was Sidon *Zidon*, built by Sidon the first son of Canaan: and the people then subject to that people were called Sidonians *Zidonians*: the same slate continuing even unto Josuas *Iosuas* time. For till then it is probable that there was but one King of all that Region; afterward called Phoenicia: which Procopius also confirms in his second Book of Vandal Wars *Warres*. But in process of time the City of Tyre adjoining became the more magnificent: yet according to the Prophet, it was but a Daughter of Sidon, and by them first built and peopled.

## p02177 · sig. 232a

But after the death of Moses, and while Joshua *Iosua* yet governed *Israel, Agenor *an Egyptian *Aegyptian* of Thebes, or a Phoenician bred in Egypt, came thence with his sons *Cadmus, Phoenix, Cyrus*, and Cilix *clilix*, ( say Cedrenus and Curtius) and built and possessed the Cities of Tyre and Sidon: to wit, the new Tyre *Tyrus*, and brought into Phoenicia( So called after the name of his second Son) the use of letters: which also Cadmus in his pursuit after his sister Europa taught the Grecians *Graecians*. For Taurus King of Crete, when he surprised Tyre, had stolen her thence: of which the Poets devised the fable of Jupiters *Iupiters* transformation into a Bull, by whom that stealth was also supposed to be made. Pomponius Sabinus makes Belus the first King of Phoenicia: and finds Cadmus his successor: whom he calls his grand-child: and it seems that 〈◊ 〉 was the Father of Agenor, and not Neptune: because the successors of Dido held that name always in reverence, making it a part of their own, as *Hasdrubal* *Asdrubal**, Hannibal*: which memory Virgil also touches in these Verses:

## p02178 · sig. 232a

Whether this Belus were Father or Grand-father to Agenor, the matter is not great. But it seems to me by comparing of times, that Belus was Ancestor to these Phoenicians, and preceded Agenor. For were Belus, or Jupiter Belus, the son of Neptune by Libya, the Daughter of Epaphus, or were he the Son of Telegonus, according to Eusebius; yet it is agreed that Cecrops then ruled in Attica: and in the end of Cecrops time, says S. *Augustine, Moses *left *Egypt: Agenor's* *Agenors** *successor living at once with Joshua. Now that Agenor returned about the same time into the Territory of Sidon, I cannot doubt: neither do I deny, but that he gave that Region the name of Phoenicia, in honour of his Son. But in stead of the building of Tyre, and Sidon, it is probable that he repaired and 〈◊ 〉 both: and therefore was called a Founder, as Semiramis and Nebuchadnezzar *Nabuchodonosor* were of Babylon.

## p02179 · sig. 232a

For be it true that Agenor was of the same Nation, and brought up in Egypt: where he learnt the use of letters( Egypt flourishing in all kind of learning in Moses time) or were he by Nation an Egyptian: yet is it very likely that either he came to save his own Territory: or otherwise to defend the 〈◊ 〉 of Canaan, from the Israelites: who were by Moses led out of Egypt, to the great loss and dishonour of that Nation: and by Joshua conducted over Jordan, to conquer and possess the Canaanites Land. For though the Egyptians *Aegyptians*, by reason of the loss which they received by the hand of God, in the Red Sea, and by the ten plagues cast on them before that, and by the slaughter of So many of the Male children at the same time, could not hinder the Hebrews *Hebrewes* from invading Canaan by Land: which also they knew had So many powerful Nations to defend it: the Deserts *Desarts* inter-jacent, and the strong *Edomites, Moabites, Emorites*, and Ammonites their borderers: yet Egypt having such Vessels *Vessells*, or Ships, or Galleys *Gallies*, as were then in use: did not in all probability neglect to Garrison the Sea coast, or assist Agenor with such forces, as they had to spare; and which they might perform with the greater facility, in that the Philistines *Philistims* which held the shores of Canaan, next adjoining unto them, were their Friends and Confederates.

## p02180 · sig. 232b

Now as it appears by the course of the story, those Cities of Phoenicia, which Agenor was said to have built( that is, to have fortified and defended against Joshua, and against the Tribes after him, as *Sidon, Sor*, or Tyre, by Joshua called the strong City, Acco *Accho* after ward *Ptolemais, Achzib* *Aczib** *and Dor) were all that Phoenicia had in those days.

## p02181 · sig. 232b

That the Kings of Phoenicia were mighty, especially by Sea, it appears, first by their defence against Israel: secondly by this, that David *Dauid* and Solomon *Salomon* could not master them: but were glad of their alliance: thirdly, that one of their Cities, though they were then but Reguli, defended it self 13. years against a King of Kings, Nebuchadnezzar: and that Alexander the great( who being made victorious by the providence of God, seemed irresistible) spent more time in the recovery of Tyre; than in the conquest of all the Cities in Asia.

## p02182 · sig. 232b

Other opinions there are, as that of Berosus out of Josephus *Iosephus*, who conceives that Tyre was founded by Tyras the son of Japheth *Iaphet*. And for the Region it self, though Callisthenes derive it *ab arbor dactylorum* ("from the date-palm trees"); and the Greeks *Greekes* from the word Phonos, of slaughter, because the Phoenicians slew all that came on their coasts, yet for my self I take it that Phoenix the son of Agenor gave it that name. But that either Agenor in Phoenicia, or Cadmus his son in Greece, were the Inventors *Inuentors* of Letters, it is ridiculous: and therefore the dispute unnecessary.

## p02183 · sig. 232b

The Ethiopians *Aethiopians* affirm that *Atlas, Orion, Orpheus, Linus, Hercules, Prometheus, Cadmus, *and others, had from them the first light of all those Arts, Sciences, and civil Policies, which they afterward professed, and taught others: and that Pythagor as himself was instructed by the Libyans: to wit, from the South and Superior Egyptians: from whom those which inhabited nearer the out-let of Nile *Nilus*, as they say, borrowed their Divinity and Philosophy: and from them the Greeks, then barbarous, received Civility. Again, the Phoenicians challenge this invention of Letters and of Learning: acknowledging nothing from the Egyptians at all; neither do they allow that Agenor and his sons were Africans *Affricans*; whence Lucan.

## p02185 · sig. 233a

Out of doubt the Phoenicians were very ancient: and from the Records and Chronicles of *Tyre, Josephus *the Historian confirms a great part of his Antiquities. The Thracians again subscribe to none of these reports: but affirm constantly, that the great Zamolxis flourished among them: when Atlas lived in *Mauritania: Nile *and Vulcan in Egypt: and Ochus in Phoenicia. Yea, some of the French do not blush to maintain, that the ancient Gauls *Gaules* taught the Greeks the use of Letters, and other Sciences. And do not we know that our Bards *Bardes* and Druids are as ancient as those Gauls, and that they sent their sons hither to be by them instructed in all kind of learning?

## p02186 · sig. 233a

Lastly, whereas others bestow this invention on Moses, the same has no probability at all, for he lived at such time as learning and arts flourished most, both in Egypt, and Assyria, and he himself was brought up in all the learning of the Egyptians, from his infancy.

## p02187 · sig. 233a

But true it is that letters were invented by those excellent Spirits of the first age, and before the general Flood *floud*: either by Seth, or Enosh *Enos*, or by whom else God knows; from whom all wisdom and understanding has proceeded. And as the same infinite God is present with all his Creatures, So has he given the same invention to divers Nations: whereof the one has not had commerce with the other; as well in this as in many other knowledges: for even in Mexico, when it was first discovered, there was found written Books *Bookes* after the manner of those Hieroglyphics *Hieroglyphicks*, anciently used by the Egyptians, and other Nations: and So had those Americans a kind of Heraldry; and their Princes differing in Arms and Escutcheons *Scutchions*, like unto those used by the Kings and Nobility of other Nations. *Jura naturalia communia, & generalia, &c.* *Natural Laws* *Lawes** are common and general.*
