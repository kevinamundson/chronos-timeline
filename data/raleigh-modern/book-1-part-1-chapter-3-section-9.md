## p00945 · sig. 66a

§. IX. Of the change of the names of places: and that besides that Eden in Coelesyria, there is a Country in Babylon, once of this name, as is proved out of Isa *ESA*.. 37. and EZECH. 27.

## p00946 · sig. 66a

These opinions answered, and the Region of Eden not found in any of those imaginary worlds, nor under Torrida Zona; it follows that now we discover and find out the seat thereof, for in it was Paradise by God planted. The difficulty of which search rests chiefly in this, That as all Nations have often changed names with their Masters; So are most of these places, by Moses remembered, forgotten by those names of all Historians and Geographers, as well ancient as modern.

## p00947 · sig. 66a

Besides, we find that the *Assyrians, Babylonians, Medes *and Persians( Cyrus only and few other excepted) sought to extinguish the Hebrews *Hebrewes*. The Grecians *Graecians* hated both their Nation and their Religion; and the Romans *Romanes* despised once to remember them in any of their stories. And as those three Monarchies succeeded each other: So did they transform the names of all those principal Places & Cities in the East: and after them, the Turk *Turke* has sought( what he could) to extinguish in all things, the ancient memory of those people; which he has subjected and enthralled.

## p00948 · sig. 66a

Now besides those notable marks, Euphrates &amp; Tigris, the better to find the way, which leads to the Country of Eden, we are to take for guides these two considerations( to wit) That it lay Eastward from Canaan &amp; Judaea *Iudaeae*; and that it was of all other the most beautiful and fertile. First then in respect of situation, the next Country to Judea *Iudaea* Eastward was Arabia Petraea; but in this Region was Moses himself when he wrote: and the next unto it Eastward also was Arabia the Desert, both which in respect of the infertility could not be Eden, neither have any of the Arabians any such Rivers *Riuers*, as are expressed to run out of it: So as it follows of necessity, that Eden must be Eastward, and beyond both Arabia Petraea, and Deserta. But because Eden is by Moses named by it self, and by the fertility, and the Rivers only described, we must seek it in other Scriptures, and where it is by the additions of the neighbour Nations better described. In the Prophet Isay I find it coupled and accompanied with other adjacent Countries, in these words, spoken in the person of Sennacherib *Senacherib* by RABSAKEH: *Have the gods of the Nations delivered them, which my Fathers have destroyed, as *GOSAN, and HARAN, and RESEPH, *and the children of *EDEN, *which were at Telassar*? and in Ezekiel *Ezechiel*, where he prophesies against the *Tyrians: They of* *Haran, and Calneh* *Canneh**, and Eden, the Merchants of Sheba, Asshur* *Asbur**, and Chilmad, were thy Merchants, &c.*

## p00949 · sig. 66b

But to avoid confusion, we must understand that there were two Edens, one of which the Prophet Amos remembers, where he divides Syria into three Provinces *Prouinces*, whereof, the first he makes Syria Damascene, or Decapolitan: the second part is that Valley called Avenis *Auenis*, otherwise Conuallis, or the Tract of Hamath *Chamath*, where Assyria is joined to Arabia the Desert, and where Ptolemy *Ptolemie* places the City of Averia *Aueria*: and the third is known by the name of Domus Edenis, or Coelesyria, otherwise Vallis 〈◊ 〉, or *the hollow Valley*, because the Mountains *Mountaynes* of Lebanon *Libanus* and Antilibanus, take all the length of it on both sides, and border it: for Coele in Greek *Greeke* is Cava in Latine. But this is not that Eden, which we seek: neither does this Province lye East from Canaan, but North, and So joins unto it, as it could not be unknown to the Hebrews. Yet, because there is a little City therein called Paradise, the Jews *Iewes* believed this Coelesyria to be the same which Moses describes. For the same cause does Hopkins in his Treatise of Paradise reprehend Beroaldo *Beroaldus*, in that he confounds this Eden, with the other Eden of Paradise: though to give Beroaldo his right, I conceive that he led the way to Hopkins, and to all other later Writers, saving, That he failed in distinguishing these two Regions, both called Eden: and that he altogether misunderstood two of the four Rivers( to wit) Pishon *Pison* and Gihon *Gehon*, as shall appear hereafter. Now to find out Eden, which( as Moses teaches us) lay Eastward from the Deserts *Desarts*, where he wrote, after he had passed the Red Sea; we must consider where those other Countries are found, which the Prophet Isay and Ezekiel joins with it. For( says Isaiah *ESAIAH*) *Gosan, Haran, and Reseph, and the children of Eden, which were at Telassar. *Also Ezekiel joins Haran with Eden, who, together with those of *Sheba, Ashur*, and Chilmad, were the Merchants that traded with the City of Tyre, which was then( says Ezekiel) *the Mart of the people for many Isles. *And it has ever been the custom, that the Persians conveyed their merchandise to Babylon, and to those Cities upon Euphrates, and Tigris, and from thence transported them into Syria, now Soria, and to the Port of the Mediterrane Sea: as in ancient times to the City of Tyre, afterward to Tripoly, and now to Aleppo, from whence they embark them at the Port of Alexandretta, in the Bay of Issicus, now *Ayas* *Laiazzo**. Ezekiel *in the description of the magnificence of Tyre, and of the exceeding trade that it had with all the Nations of the East, as the only Mart-town *Mart-towne* of that part of the World, recites both the people, with whom they had commerce, and also what commodities every Country yielded: and having counted the several People and Countries, he adds the particular trade, which each of them exercised: They were thy Merchants( says the Prophet) *in all sorts of things, in garments of blue silk, and of embroidered works, fine linen, corral and pearl: *and afterwards speaking of the Merchants of Sheba and Raamah, and what kinds they traded, he has these words: *The Merchants of Sheba and Raamah were thy Merchants, they occupied in thy Fayres, with the chief of all Spices, and with all precious stones and gold. *Now these be indeed the riches which Persia and Arabia Felix *Foelix* yield: and because Sheba and Raamah *Raama* are those parts of Arabia, which border the Sea, called the Persian Gulf, therefore did those Nations both vent such Spice, sweet Gums *Gummes*, and Pearls *Pearles*, as their own Countries yielded, and( withal) having trade with their Neighbours of India, had from them also all sorts of spices, and plenty of gold. The better to convey these commodities to that great Mart of Tyre, the Shebans or Arabians entered by the mouth of Tigris, and from the City of Terredon( built or enlarged by Nebuchadnezzar *Nabuchodonozor*, now called Basra *Balsara*) thence sent up all these rich merchandises by boat to Babylon, from whence by the body of Euphrates, as far as it bent Westward, and afterward by a branch thereof, which reaches within three days journey of Aleppo, and then over Land they past to Tyre, as they did afterward to Tripoly( formerly Hieropolis) and thence to Alexandretta, as aforesaid. Now the Merchants of Calneh, which Ezekiel joins with Eden, inhabited far up the River, and received this trade from Arabia &amp; India, besides those proper commodities which themselves had, and which they received out of Persia, which bordered them. S. Jerome *Hierome* understands by *Calneh, Seleucia* *Selencia*, which is seated upon *Euphrates, where it breaks into four heads, *and which took that name from Seleucus, who made thereof a magnificent City. Hierosolymitanus thinks it to be Ctesiphon, but Ctesiphon is seated down low upon Tigris, and Calneh cannot be on that side, I mean on the East-side of Tigris, for then were it out of the Valley of *Shinar. Pliny* *Plinie** *places the Schenite upon Euphrates, where the same begins to be fordable, which is toward the border of Syria, after it leaves to be the bound of Arabia the Desert, & where the River of Euphrates reflects from the Desert of Palmirena: for these people of Calneh( afterward Schenitae) inhabited both borders of Euphrates, stretching themselves from their own City of Calneh in Shinar Westward along the banks of Euphrates, as far as the City of Thapsacus, where Ptolemy appointed the Fords *Foords* of Euphrates: which also agrees with the description of the Schenitae by Strabo, whose words are these: *Mercatoribus ex Syria Seleuciam* ("Merchants from Syria [going] to Seleucia") *& Babyloniam euntibus iter est per Schenitas; The Merchants which travail from Syria to Seleucia and Babylon, take their way by the Schenitas. *Therefore those which take Calneh for Haran *Charran*, do much mistake it. For Haran, to which Abraham came from Ur in Chaldea *Chaldaea*( called by God) stands also in Mesopotamia, not upon Euphrates itself, but upon the River of Chaboras, which falls into Euphrates: and the Merchants of Haran are distinctly named with those of Calneh in Ezekiel( as) *they of Haran, and Calneh, and Eden, the Merchants of Sheba, Ashur, and Chilmad were thy Merchants. *Wherefore Haran which is sometime called Char, and Haran, and Aran, is but the same Haran of Mesopotamia; and when it is written Aran, then it is taken for the Region of Mesopotamia: or Aran 〈◊ 〉, the Greek word( Mesopotamia) importing, a Country between Rivers: for Mesos in Greek, is Medius in Latine, and *Potamos, 〈◊ 〉*; and when it is written Haran or Aran, it is then taken for the City it self, to which Abraham came from Ur( as aforesaid. ) For Strabo in the description of Arabia, gives that Tract of Land from the borders of Coelesyria, to the edge of Mesopotamia, to the Schenitae, who also inhabited on both sides of Euphrates, and were in later ages accounted of these Arabians which inhabit Batanea, and the North part of the Deserts, stretching themselves toward the uninhabited Solitude of Palmirena, which lies between Syria, and Arabia the Desert So as these of Calneh lay in the very high-way from Babylon to Tyre, and were Neighbours( indifferent) to Haran and to Eden: and therefore they are by the Prophet Ezekiel coupled together, *They of Haran, and Calneh, and Eden, &c. *But S. Jerome made a good interpretation of Calneh, or Calneh, by Seleucia: for Seleucia was anciently called Chalanne( witness Appian; ) and So Rabanus Maurus calls it in his Commentaries upon Genesis; the name by time and mixture of languages being changed from Calneh or Calneh, to Chalanne: of which name there are two other Cities, standing in Triangle with Seleucia, and almost the next unto it, ( as) Thelbe-can *Thelbe-canne*, and Mann can, the one a little to the West of Seleucia, and the other opposite unto it, where these Rivers of Tigris and Euphrates are ready to join. Therefore, which of these the ancient Can was, ( being all three within the bound of the Valley Shinar) it is uncertain: but it is a note as well of the importance of the place, as of the certain 〈◊ 〉 thereof, that So many other Cities did retain a part of the name in So many ages after. Neither is it unlikely, that these additions of 〈◊ 〉 and Mann to the word Can, were but to make difference between the East and the West, or the greater and the less Can, or between Can the old, and the new: which additions to distinguish Cities by, are ordinary in all the Regions of the World.

But this City Can or Calneh is made manifest by Moses himself, where it is written of Nimrod: *And the beginning of his Kingdom was Babel, and 〈◊ 〉, and* *Acad, and Calneh, in the Land of Sinaar, *or Shinar: where Moses shows the first composition position of the Babylonian Empire, and what Cities and People were subject unto Nimrod, all which lay in the said Valley of Shinar or near it; and this Valley of Shinar is that Tract, afterwards called Babylonia and Chaldea, into which also Eden stretches it self. *Chaldea, Babylonia, Sinaar, idem sunt* ("Chaldea, Babylonia, Shinar are the same [place].")( says COMESTOR. ) *Three names of one Country*: which Region of Babylonia took name of the 〈◊ 〉 Babel; and the Tower, of the confusion of tongues. And that Shinar was Babylonia, it is proved in the eleventh Chapter of Genesis, in these words: *And as they went from the* *East, they found a Playne in the Land of Shinar, and there they abode: *in which Playne Babyton was built( as aforesaid. )

## p00955 · sig. 67b

Now Shinar being Babylonia, and Calneh, in the first beginning of Nimrods greatness, and before he had subdued any strange, or far-off Nations, being 〈◊ 〉 part 〈◊ 〉 his Dominion, and also named by Moses to be in Shinar, it proves, that Calneh joins to Babylonia; which also Ezekiel couples with Eden, and( further) affirms, that those of Eden were also the Merchants, which traded with the Tyrians: and Isaiah in the threats of Sennacherib against Jerusalem *Hierusalem*( with other Nations that Sennacherib vaunted that his Fathers had destroyed) names the children of Eden which were at Telassar. But before I conclude where Eden itself lies, it is necessary to describe those other Countries, which Ezekiel joins therewith in the places before remembered, as, those of Sheba and Raamah. It is written in Genesis the tenth: Moreover *the sons of *HAM were CUSH *CVSH*, *&c. And the sons of *CUSH were SEBA, and HAVILAH, and SABTAH. & RAAMAH, *&c. And the sons of *RAAMAH were SHEBA, & c. and anon after, CUSH begat Nimrod: So as Sheba was the grand child of Cush, and Nimrod the son of Cush, whose elder brother was Sebah: though some there are that conceive to the contrary, that Nimrod was the elder in valour and understanding, though not in time and precedence of birth; who inhabited that part of Shinar, where Babel was built, afterwards Babylonia. His brother Raamah or Regma took that part adjoining to Shinar, toward the Sea side and Persian Gulf( called afterward Raamah and Sheba, by the Father and his Sons, which possessed it. ) For( says Ezekiel) *the Merchants of 〈◊ 〉 and Sheba were thy Merchants, they occupied in thy Fair's* *Faire's** with the chief of all spices, and all precious stones, and gold. *So as Sheba was that Tract of Country, which parts Arabia Deserta from Arabia Felix, and which joins to the Sea where Tigris and Euphrates fall out, and render themselves to the Ocean. This part, and the confining Country Strabo calls Catabaria, where the best Myrrh and Frankincense is gathered; which people have an interchange or trade with Elana, lying on the East side of the Persian Gulf. By this it appears who were the Shebans *Shebeans*, spoken of by Ezekiel, and said to have been the Merchants of Tyre, for gold, spices, and precious stones: of which they had not only plenty of their own, but were also furnished from that part of India( called Elana, according to Strabo) for exchange of their Aromatics *Aromatiques*, and other proper commodities. For as Strabo reports out of ERATOSTHENES: *In Persicae 〈◊ 〉 initto Insula est, in qua multi & pretiosi unions gignantur: in alijs vero, clari & 〈◊ 〉 lapilli* ("In [illegible] at the beginning of Persia there is an island, in which many precious pearls are produced: but in others, bright [illegible] stones.")*. *ERATOSTHENES( says Strabo *STRRBO*) affirms, that in the *beginning of the Persian Gulf, there is an Island, in which there are many precious pearls bred: and in other, very clear and shining stones. *Now, the difference between Sheba, the son of Raamah, and Seba, the son of Cush, is in this, That Seba is written with the Hebrew( Samekh *Samech*; ) and Sheba with( Shin *Schin*: ) but whatsoever the difference may be in the Hebrew Orthography, their Countries and habitations are divers. For Sheba is that part which borders the Persian Sea; and Sheba *Saba*( whence the Queen of Sheba) neighboured the red Sea; and So that place of the 72. Psalm, expounded, *Reges Arabum & Sheba*, has in the Hebrew this sense: *Reges Shebae & Sabae*.

## p00956 · sig. 68a

The *Shebans, Ezekiel *names together with the Edenites, because they inhabited upon the Out-let of the same river, upon which the Edenites were seated: and So those of Sheba, towards the Sea-coast, and upon it, past up the Country, by Tigris and Euphrates, being joined in one main stream, and So through the Region of Eden, which Tigris bounds, thereby the better to convey their merchandise toward Tyre. And as the Cities of Haran, and Calneh, border Eden on the West and Northwest: So does Sheba on the South, and Chilmad on the North-east. Chilmad being a Region of the higher Media, as appears in the Chaldean *Chaldaean* Paraphrast, which Country by the Geographers is called Coromitena, ( L) placed by exchange for( R: ) which change the Hebrews also often use.

## p00957 · sig. 68a

Thus much of those Countries which border Eden, and who altogether traded with the Tyrians: of which, the chief were the Edenites, inhabiting Telassar: for these Senacharib vaunted, that his Fathers had destroyed; and this place of Telassar lay most convenient, both to receive the Trade from Sheba and Arabia, and also to convey it over into Syria, and to Tyre *Tyrus*. Now to make these things the more plain, we must remember, that before the death of Senacharib, many parts of the Babylonian Empire fell from his obedience, and after his death these Monarchies were utterly disjoined.

## p00958 · sig. 68a

For it appears both in Isa.. 37. and in the second of Kings, by the threats of Rabsache, the while the Army of Assyria lay before Jerusalem, that the Cities of *Gosan, Haran, Reseph*, and the Edenites at Telassar, had resisted the Assyrians, though by them( in a sort) mastered and recovered. *Have the gods of the Nations delivered* *them, whom my Fathers have destroyed, as Gosan, and Haran, Reseph, and the children of Eden, which were at Telassar? *But it appeared manifestly after Sennacherib's *Senacharibs* death, that these Nations formerly contending, were then freed from the others subjection: for Esar-Haddon held Assyria, and *Merodach Baladan, Babylonia*. And after that the Army of Senacharib, commanded by Rabsache, which lay before Jerusalem( Hezekiah *Ezechias* then reigning) while Senacharib was in Egypt, was by the Angel of God destroyed: the King of Babel sent to Hezekiah, both to congratulate the recovery of his health, and his victory obtained over the Assyrians. After which overthrow, Senacharib himself was slain by his own sons in the Temple of his Idols *Idoles*, Esar-Haddon succeeding him in Assyria. To the Babylonian Ambassadors *Ambassadours* sent by *Merodach, Hezekiah* showed all his treasures, as well proper as consecrate, which invited the Kings of Babylon afterward to undertake their conquest and subversion. So as, the suspicion of 〈◊ 〉 increasing between Babylon and Assyria, the Edenites which inhabited the borders of Shinar towards the North, and towards Assyria, were employed to bear off the incursions of the Assyrians; and their Garrison-place was at Telassar: and the very word( Telassar) says Junius *Iunius*, signifying as much, as a Bulwark against the Assyrians. This place Hierosolymitanus takes for Resem, others for Seleucia: but this Telassar is the same, which Am. Marcellinus in the History of Julian *Iulian*( whom he followed in the enterprise of Persia) calls Thilutha in stead of Telassar, who describes the exceeding strength thereof in his 24. Book: It is seated in an Island of Eupbrates upon a steep and unassaultable Rock, in So much as the Emperor Julian durst not attempt it; and therefore it was a convenient place for a Garrison against the Assyrians, being also a passage out of Mesopotamia into Babylonia, and in which the Edenites of the Country adjoining were lodged to defend the same. This place Ptolemy calls Teridata, having Reseph( which he calls Resepha) on the left hand, and Calneh, ( which he calls Thelbe-can) on the right hand, not far from whence is also found the City of Mann-can *Mann-canne* upon Tigris, and all these seated together, as Isaiah and Ezekiel have sorted them. But the understanding of these places is the more difficult, because Assyria( which the Chaldeans *Chaldaeans* call Atturia) and Mesopotamia, were So often confounded: the one taken for the other by interchange of Dominion. *Assyria & Mesopotamia in Babyloniae nomen transier unt* ("Assyria and Mesopotamia passed into the name of Babylonia.")( says NIGER, ) *Assyria and Mesopotamia* *took the name of Babylonia*. Lastly, it appears by those adjacent Regions by the Prophets named, in what part of the World Eden is seated, as, by Haran or Haran in Mesopotamia: also by Calneh and Reseph, according to the opinion of Vatablus, who in these words translates this place: *Plantaver* *Plantauer** at autem* ("But he had planted [a garden];")* *JEHOVAH *Deus hortum in Eden, ab Oriente, The Lord God planted a garden in Eden, Eastward: *that is( says he in his Annotations. ) *Jusser* *Iusser** at nasci arbors in Eden, Regione Orientali, in finibus Arabiae & Mesopotamiae, He commanded trees to grow in Eden, an Eastern Region in the borders of Arabia and Mesopotamia.*
