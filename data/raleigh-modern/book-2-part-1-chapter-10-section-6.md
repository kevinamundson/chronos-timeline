## p02379 · sig. 249b

This Tribe of Gad possessed half the Country of the Ammonites, who together with the Moabites, held that part of Arabia Petraea called Nabataea *Nabat* 〈◊ 〉, as well within as without the mountains of Gilead: though at this time when the Gadites wan it, it was in the possession of Sihon *Sehon* and *Og, Amorites*: and therefore Moses did not expel the Ammonites, but 〈◊ 〉 Amorites, who had thrust the issues of Lot over the mountains Trachonitis *Tracbones* or 〈◊ 〉, as before. After the death of Othniel *Othoniel* the first Judge of Israel, the Ammonites joined with the Moabites against the Hebrews *Hebrewes*, and So continued long. Jephthah *Iephta* Judge of Israel had a great conquest over one of the Kings of Ammon, but his name is omitted. In the time of Samuel they were at peace with them again.

## p02380 · sig. 249b

Afterward we find that cruel King of the Ammonites, called 〈◊ 〉: who besieging Jabs *Iabes* Gilead, gave them no other conditions but the pulling out of their right eyes. The reason why he tendered So hard a composition, was( besides this desire to bring shame upon Israel) because those Gileadites using to carry a Target on their left arms, which could not but shadow their left eyes, should by losing their right, be utterly disabled to defend themselves: but Saul came to their rescue, and delivered them from that danger. This Nahas, as it may, seem, became the confederate of David *Dauid*, having friended him in Sauls time, though Josephus *Iosephus* thinks that this Nahas was slain in the battle, when Saul raised the siege of 〈◊ 〉, who affirms that there were three Kings of the Moabites of that name.

## p02381 · sig. 249b

Hanun succeeded Nahas: to whom when David sent to congratulate his establishment, and to confirm the former friendship which he had with his Father, he most contemptuously and proudly cut off the Ambassadors *Ambassadours* garments to the knees, and shaved the half of their 〈◊ 〉. But afterward notwithstanding the aids received from the Aramites subject to Hadadezer *Adadezer*, and from the Reguli of Rehob, and Maacah, and from Ish-tob *Istob*, yet all those Arabians, together with the Ammonites, were over-turned: their chief City of Rabbah *Rabba*, after Philadelphia, was taken, the Crown which weighed a talent of gold was set on Davids *Dauids* head, all such as were prisoners, David executed with strange severity; for with Saws *Sawes* and Harrows *Harrowes* he tare them in pieces, and cast the rest into Lime-kills.

Jehoshaphat *Iosaphat* governing Judah *Iuda*, they assisted the Moabites their neighbours against him, and perished together. Uzziah *Osias* made them Tributaries, and they were again by 〈◊ 〉 enforced to continue that Tribute, and to increase it, to wit, a hundredth talents of silver, ten thousand measures of wheat, and ten thousand of barley: which the Ammonites continued two years.
