§. II. *Of the Kings of the Canaanites and Midianites* *Madianites**, mentioned in the ancient Wars* *Warres** of the Israelites.*

## p01918 · sig. 208a

OF the Kings of the Canaanites, descended of Ham *Cham*, ( for Melchizedek may be thought to be of a better Pedigree) we find four named by Moses: and one and thirty remembered by Joshua *Iosua*, though few of these named, otherwise than by the Cities over which they commanded: to which each of them had a small Territory adjoining, and no other Dominion. These Canaanites in a general consideration are to be understood for all those Nations, descended of Ham by Canaan, as the *Hittites, Jebusites* *Iebusites**, Amorites, Gergesites, Hevites* *Heuites**, & c* ("Hittites, Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites, Hivites, etc.")*. *and So here we understand this name in speaking of the Kings of the Canaanites: and So also we call the Country of their habitation, the holy Land, or the Land of promise: for God had appointed that the seven principal Families should be rooted out: and that his own people should inherit their Lands and Cities. But if we consider of the Name and Nation in particular, then is their proper habitation bounded by Jordan, on the East, and by the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea on the West: in which narrow Country, and in the choicest places thereof, those Canaanites which held their Paternal name chiefly inhabited.

## p01919 · sig. 208b

The first King of these Nations, named in the Scriptures, was Hamor or Hamor *Hemor*, of the Hevites, whom Simeon and Levi *Leui* slew, together with his Son Shechem *Sichem*, in revenge of their Sisters ravishment.

## p01920 · sig. 208b

Arad was the second King which the Scriptures have remembered, who had that part of Canaan towards the South, neighbouring Edom and the Dead Sea; the same which surprised Israel, as they encamped in the Wilderness in the edge of Idumea *Edumaea*.

## p01921 · sig. 208b

The third named was Sihon *Sehon* King of Essebon, who before Moses arrival had beaten the Moabites out of the West part of Arabia 〈◊ 〉 or Nabathea, and thrust them over Arnon 〈◊ 〉 〈◊ 〉 Deserts *Desarts*, the same whom Moses over-threw in the plains of Moab: at which time he took Essebon, and all the Cities of the Amorites.

## p01922 · sig. 208b

Presently after which victory, Og was also slain by Israel, who commanded the North part of that Valley between the Mountains *Mountaines* Traconi or Gilead *Galaad* and Jordan, who was also a King of the Amorites.

After these Adonibezek that notorious Tyrant is named: who confessed that he had cut off the thumbs of the hands, and feet, of 70. Kings, enforcing them to gather crumbs under his Table: who, after Judah *Iuda* and Simeon had used the same execution upon himself, acknowledged it to be a just revenge of God: this King was carried to Jerusalem where he died.

## p01930 · sig. 208b

The last King named is Jabin the second, who as it seems had rebuilt Hazor, burnt by Joshua. For at such time as he employed Sisara against Israel, whom he oppressed twenty years, after the death of Ehud, He inhabited Hazor. This *Jabin, Barak* *Barac*( encouraged by Debora) overthrew; and his Captain Sisara had by Jael *Iael*, the Wife of Eber *Heber* the Kenite, a nail driven into his head while he slept in her Tent: Jabin himself perishing afterward in that war.

## p01931 · sig. 208b

The Midianites had also their Kings at times, but commonly mixed with the Moabites: and they held a corner of Land in Nabathea: to the South-east of the Dead Sea. They descended from *Midian* *Madian**, Abrahams *son, by *Cethura. Raguel *surnamed Gethegleus or Jethres *Iethres*, says Josephus *Iosephus*, called Jethro in *Exodus, Kenite* *Kenis** *in the first of Judges *Iudges*, the son of Dathan, the grand-child of Jokshan *Iexanis*, or Jokshan, the great grand-child of Abraham by Cethura, was Priest or Prince of the Midianites by the Red Sea: whose Daughter, or Niece, Moses married: and of whom I have spoken elsewhere more at large. This Jethro if he were not the same with Hobab, must be his Father: and this Hobab had seven Daughters. He guided Moses in the Wilderness: and became one of the Israelites: of him descended the Kenites So called of his Father Reuel's *Raguels* surname, of which Kenites was Eber, which had peace with Jabin the second, even now remembered.

## p01932 · sig. 208b

At such time as Saul invaded the Amalekites, he knowing the good affection of the Kenites to Israel, gave them warning to separate themselves: and yet the Kenites had strong seats, and lived in the mountains of the Deserts.
