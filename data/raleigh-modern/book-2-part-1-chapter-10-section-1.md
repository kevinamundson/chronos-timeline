# Book II, Part I, Chapter X, §. 1

> Sir Walter Raleigh, *The History of the World* (1617). Modern edition. Original spelling: EarlyPrint A10357 (EEBO-TCP), London 1617, Stansby for Walter Burre. The original text is untouched; this file is the stored modern copy.

## p02274 · sig. 240a

§ I. Of DAN, where of Joppa *Ioppe*, Gath, Accaron, Ashdod *Azotus*, and other Towns *Townes*.

## p02275 · sig. 240a

Now following the coast of the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea, that portion of Land assigned to the Tribe of Dan, joins to Ephraim, whereof I spoke last: of which family there were numbered at Mount Sinai 62700. fighting men, all which leaving their bodies with the rest in the Deserts *Desarts*, there entered the Holy Land of their sons 66400. bearing arms. The first famous City in this Tribe on the Sea coast was Joppa, or Joppa, as in the 19. of Joshua *Iosua*: one of the most ancientest of the World, and the most famous of others on that coast, because it was the Port of Jerusalem *Hierusalem*. From hence Jonas *Ionas* embarked himself when he fled from the service of God, towards Tarshish *Tharsis* in Cilicia. In the time of the Macchabees this City received many changes: and while Judas *Iudas* 〈◊ 〉 governed the Jews *Iewes*, the Syrians that were Garrisoned *Garrisond* in Joppa, having their fleet in the Port, invited 200. principal Citizens aboard them, and cast them all into the Sea: which Judas revenged by firing their fleet, and putting the companies which sought to escape to the sword.

## p02276 · sig. 240a

It was twice taken by the Romans *Romanes*, and by Cestius the Lieutenant utterly burnt and ruined. But in the year of Christ 1250. Louis *Lodouick* the French King gave it new Walls and Towers: It is now the Turks *Turkes*, and called Jaffa *Iaffa*. There are certain Rocks in that Port, to which it is reported that Andromeda was fastened with chains: and from thence delivered from the Sea-Monster by Perseus. This fable( for So I take it) is confirmed by *Josephus* *Iosephus**, Solinus*, and *Pliny* *Plinie**. Marcus Scaurus *during his office of Aedileship, showed the bones of this Monster to the people of Rome. S. Jerome *Hierome* upon Jonas speaks of it indifferently.

## p02277 · sig. 240a

The next unto Joppa was Jamnia, where Judas Maccabeus *Macchabaeus* 〈◊ 〉 the rest of the Syrian fleet: the fire and flame whereof was seen at Jerusalem 240. furlongs off. It had sometime a Bishops seat, says Will. of Tyre; But there is no sign of it at this time that such a place there was.

## p02278 · sig. 240a

After Jamnia is the City of Gath *Geth* or Gath, sometime Anthedon, says Volaterran. And So Montanus seems to understand it. For he sets it next to Egypt *Aegypt*, of all the Philistine *Philistim* Cities, and in the place of Anthedon. But Volaterran gives neither reason nor authority for his opinion; for Ptolemy *Ptolomie* sets Anthedon far to the South of Joppa: And Gath was the first and not the last( beginning from the North) of all the great Cities of the Philistines *Philistims*: and about sixteen miles from Joppa; where S t. Jerome in his time found a great Village of the same name. It was sometime the Habitation and Seminary of the Anakim *Anakims*: strong and Giant-like-men, whom Joshua could not expel, nor the Danites after him: nor any of the Israelites, till Davids *Dauids* time: who slew Goliath, as his Captains *Captaines* did divers others not much inferior in strength and stature unto Goliath.

## p02279 · sig. 240b

Roboam the Son of Solomon *Salomon* rebuilt *Gath: Ozias *the son of Amaziah *Amazia* destroyed it again. It was also laid waste by Hazael *Azael* King of *Syria. 〈◊ 〉 *the fourth King of Jerusalem, built a Castle in the same place out of the old ruins. Whether this Gath was the same that Will. of Tyre in the holy War calls Ibelin *Ibijlin*, I much doubt: the error growing by taking Gath for Anthedon.

## p02280 · sig. 240b

Not far from Gath or Gath stands 〈◊ 〉 or the house of the Sun. In the fields adjoining to this City( as is thought) was the Ark of God brought by a yoke of two Kin, turned loose by the Philistines: and the Beth-shemites *Bethsemites* presuming to look therein, there were slain of the Elders 70. and of the people 50000 by the ordinance of God. After which slaughter and the great lamentation of the people: it was called the great * Abel, says St. *Jerome. Benedict* *Benedictus** Theologus *finds three other Cities of this name; one in a Nepthalim: another in Judah *Iuda*: and another in *Issachar* *Isachar**. Jerome *finds a fifth in Benjamin *Beniamin*.

## p02281 · sig. 240b

Keeping the Sea-coast, the strong City of Accaron offers it self, sometime one of the five Satrapies, or Governments *Gouernments* of the Philistines. S t. Jerome makes it the same with *Caesarea* *Caesaria** Palestine* *Palaestinae**. Pliny *confounds it with Apollonia: It was one of those that defended it self against the Danites and Judeans *Iudaeans*. It worshipped Beel-zebub the God of Hornets or Flies. To which Idol it was that Ahaziah King of Israel sent to enquire of his health: whose Messengers Eliah meeting by the way caused them to return, with a sorrowful answer to their Master. This City is remembered in many places of Scripture.

## p02282 · sig. 240b

Christian *Christianus* Schrot places Ashdod next to Gath, and then Accaron or Ekron. This Ashdod or Asdod was also an habitation of the Anakim, whom Joshua b failed to destroy, though he once possessed their City. Herein stood a sumptuous Temple dedicated to the c Idol Dagon: the same Idol which fell twice to the ground of it self, after the Ark of God was by the Philistines carried into their Temple; and in the second fall it was utterly broken and defaced. Near it was that famous d Judas Machabaeus slain by Bacchides and Alcimus, the Lieutenants of Demetrius. Afterward it was taken by Jonathas *Ionathas*: and the rest of the Citizens being put to the sword, all that fled into the Temple of Dagon, were with their Idol therein consumed with fire: near which also he overthrew Apollonius.

## p02283 · sig. 240b

Gabinius the Roman *Romane* rebuilt it. It had a Bishops seat while Christianity flourished in those parts. But in St. Jerome's *Hieromes* time it was yet a fair Village. And this was the last of the Sea-Towns *Sea-Townes* within the Tribe of Dan.

## p02284 · sig. 240b

The Cities which are within the Land Eastward from Ashdod, and beyond the Fountain of Ethiopia *Aethiopia*, wherein Philip the Apostle baptized the Eunuch, are Zorah *Tsorah*, or Zorah, and Eshtaol *Esthaol*, and between them Castra Danis near Hebron: though this place where Sampson was borne, may seem by the words Judg *Iud*.. 18. 12. to be in the Tribe of Judah, as the other also were bordering Towns between Dan and 〈◊ 〉.

## p02285 · sig. 240b

After these within the bounds of Judah, but belonging to the Danites, they find Gedor, or as it is 1. Mac. 15. Cedron, which Cendebaeus the Lieutenant of Antiochus fortified against the Jews, and near which himself was by the Macchabees overthrown.

## p02286 · sig. 240b

Then Modin the Native City of the Macchabees: and wherein they were buried, on whose Sepulcher the seven Marble Pillars, which were erected of that height as they served for a mark to the Sea-men, remained many hundreds of years after their first setting up, as Brochard and Breidenbach witness.

## p02287 · sig. 240b

There are besides these the City of Cariathiarim, that is, the City of the woods: seated in the border of *Judah, Benjamin*, and Dan, wherein the Ark of God remained twenty years in the house of Aminadab: till such time as David *Dauid* carried it thence to Jerusalem: Of this place( as they say) was Zacharias the son of Berechiah *Barachias*, or Jehoiada *lehoida*, who was slain between the Temple and the Altar: also Urias *Vrias* whom Joachim King of Jerusalem slaughtered as we find in Jeremiah *Ieremie*. Many other places which they place in this Tribe, rather as I take it upon presumption than warrant, I omit: as that of Caspin taken with great slaughter by Judas Machabaeus: and Lachish *Lachis*, whose King was slain by Joshua, in which also Amaziah was slain: The same which Sennacherib *Senacherib* took, Hezekiah *Ezechias* reigning in Judah.

## p02288 · sig. 241a

Of other Cities belonging to this Tribe, see in Joshua c. 19. from the Verse 41. where also it is added that the Danites portion was too little for their number of Families: and therefore that they invaded Leshem, and inhabited it: which City after amplified by Philip the brother of Herod Antipas, was called Caesarea Philippi, as before, and made the Metropolis of Ituraea, and Trachonitis: of which coasts this Philip was Tetrarch: but of this City see more in Nepthalim. In this Tribe there are no Mountains *Mountaines* of fame.

## p02289 · sig. 241a

It has two Rivers *Riuers* or Torrents: the Norther-most rises out of the Mountains of Judah: and passing by Modin, falls into the Sea by Sachrona. The other has the name of Sorek or Sorek *Sored*, whose banks are plentiful of Vines which have no seeds or stones: The wine they yield is red, of excellent colour, taste, and savour, &c. In this Valley of Sorek, So called from the River, inhabited Delilah *Dalila* whom Sampson loved.
