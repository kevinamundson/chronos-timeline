§. III. *Of the Amalekites and Ishmaelites* *Ismaelites*.

## p01941 · sig. 209a

OF the Kings of the Amalekites and Ishmaelites, I find few that are named, and though of the Ishmaelites there were more in number than of the rest( for they were multiplied into a greater Nation, according to the promise of God made unto Abraham) yet the Amalekites, who together with the Midianites were numbered among them, were more renowned in Moses time than the rest of the Ishmaelites. So also were they when Saul governed Israel. For Saul pursued them from Sur unto Havilah *Hauilah*, to wit, over a great part of Arabia Petraea, and the Desert. The reason to me seems to be this: That the twelve Princes which came of Ismael, were content to leave those barren Deserts *Desarts* of Arabia Petraea, called *Sur, Paran*, and Sin, to the issue of Abraham by Keturah *Cetura*, that joined with them( for So seem the Amalekites to have been, and So were the Midianites *Madianites*: ) themselves taking possession of a better soil in Arabia the Happy, and about the Mountains *Mountaines* of Gilead *Galaad* in Arabia Petraea. For Nabaioth the eldest of those twelve Princes planted that part of Arabia Petraea, which was very fruitful though adjoining to the Desert, in which Moses wandered, afterward called Nabathea: the same which neighbours Judea *Iudaea* on the East side. They also peopled a Province in Arabia the Happy, whereof the people were in after-times called *Nabataeans* *Napathei**( B*) changed into *( P) *.

## p01943 · sig. 209b

Adbeel sate down in the Desert Arabia, near the Mountains which divide it from the Happy: and gave name to the Adubens, which Ptolemy *Ptolomie* calls Agubens.

## p01944 · sig. 209b

Mibsam was the Parent of the Masamancuses, near the Mountain Zamath, in the same Arabia the Happy.

## p01945 · sig. 209b

The Raabens were of Mishma: who joined to the Orchens, near the Arabian gulf, where Ptolemy sets Zagmais.

## p01946 · sig. 209b

Of Duma were the Dumaeans, between the Adubens and Raabens: where the City Dumah *Dumeth* sometimes stood.

## p01947 · sig. 209b

Of Massa the Massani, and of Hadar, or Kedar *Chadar* the 〈◊ 〉, who bordered the Nabataeans in the same Happy Arabia.

## p01948 · sig. 209b

Thema begat the Themaneans, among the Arabian Mountains, where also the City of Thema is seated.

## p01949 · sig. 209b

Of Jetur *Ietur* the Ituraeans, or Chamathens: of whom Tohu was King in Davids *Dauids* time.

## p01950 · sig. 209b

Of Naphish *Naphri* the Nubian *Nubeian* Arabians: inhabiting Syria Zoba: over whom 〈◊ 〉 commanded, while David *Dauid* ruled Israel.

## p01951 · sig. 209b

Cadma, the last and twelfth of Ismaels sons, was the Ancestor of the Kadmonites *Cadmonaeans*: who were afterward called Asitae: because they worshipped the fire with the Babylonians.

## p01952 · sig. 209b

The Amalekites gave their Kings the name of 〈◊ 〉, as the 〈◊ 〉 the name of Pharaoh to theirs, and the ancient Syrians Adad to theirs, and the *Arabian Nabataeans, Aretas*, as Names of Honour.

## p01953 · sig. 209b

The Amalekites were the first that fought with Moses, after he past the Red Sea: when of all times they flourished most, and yet were vanquished.

## p01954 · sig. 209b

Afterward they joined with the Canaanites, and beat the Israelites near Cadesbarne. After the Government of Othniel *Othoniel*, they joined them with the Moabites: after Barak *Barac* with the Midianites: and invaded Israel. God commanded that as soon as Israel had rest; they should root out the name of the 〈◊ 〉: which Saul executed in part, when he wasted them from the border of Egypt *Aegypt*, to the border of 〈◊ 〉: from 〈◊ 〉 to Shur.

## p01955 · sig. 209b

In Davids time they took Siklag in Simeon: but David followed them, and surprised them, recovering his prisoners and spoils. And yet, after David became King, they again vexed him, but to their own loss.

## p01956 · sig. 209b

In Hezekiah *Ezekias* time as many of them as joined to Idumea *Edumaea* were wasted and displanted by the children of Simeon.
