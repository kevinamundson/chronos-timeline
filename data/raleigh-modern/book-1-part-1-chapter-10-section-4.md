## p01433 · sig. 145a

§. IIII. *Of the acts of *Nimrod and BELUS *BELVS*, *as far as now they are known.*

## p01434 · sig. 145a

But to return to the Story, it is plain in Moses, that Nimrod( whom Philo interprets transfugium ("a fugitive, a deserter"); and Julius *Iulius* Africanus surnamed Saturn) was the establisher of the Babylonian Monarchy, of whom there is no other thing written, then that his Empire in the beginning consisted of those four Cities before remembered, *Babel, Erec, Akkad* *Accad*, and Calneh *Chalne*: and that from hence he propagated his Empire into Assyria; and in Assyria built four more Cities( to wit) *Nineveh* *Niniue**, Rehoboth, Calah* *Celah*, and Resen. And seeing that he spent much time in building Babel itself and those adjoining, and that his travails were many ere he came into Shinar *Shinaar*: that work of Babel( such as it was) with the other three Cities, and the large foundation of Nineveh, and the other Cities of Assyria which he built( considered with the want of materials, and with other impediments) were of greater difficulty then any thing performed by his Successors in many years after: to whose undertakings time had given So great an increase of people; and the examples and patterns of his beginning So great an advancement and encouragement: in whose time( says Glycas) all these Nations were called *Meropes, a sermonis linguarum terraeque divisione; By reason that the earth and the speech were then divided.*

## p01435 · sig. 145a

Belus, or Bel, or Jupiter Belus, succeeded Nimrod, after he had reigned 114. years; of whose acts and undertakings there is little written. For it is thought that he spent much of his time in disburdening the low Lands of Babylon, and drying and making firm ground of all those great Fens *Fennes* and over-flown Marshes *Marishes* which adjoined unto it. For any of his Wars *Warres* or Conquests there is no report, other than of his begun enterprise against Sabatius King of Armenia, and those parts of Scythia which Berosus calls Scythia Saga, whose son and successor Barzanes became subject and Tributary to Ninus, that followed the war to effect, which was by his Father Belus begun.
