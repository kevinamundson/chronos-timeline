§. IIII. *Of other lesser Kingdoms* *Kingdomes** of the Syrians, which being brought under the Assyrians, never recovered themselves again.*

## p02424 · sig. 255a

OF Gessur we find two Kings named; to wit, Talmai, and his Father Ammihud *Ammihur*. To Talmai, whose daughter David *Dauid* married, it was that Absalon fled, who was his maternal grandfather. Of the Kings of Sophena or *Syria, Zobah* *Soba** *or Coele-Syria *Coelosyria*, there are two named, Rehob or 〈◊ 〉 the Father of Hadadezer *Adadezer*, and Hadadezer himself, and it is plain that after his death the seat of the Kings of Zobah was transferred to Damascus, a City better fitting their greatness. After Rezin became Lord of both Principalities. And the race of these Kings of Syria, ( which became So potent, and joined *Zobah, Damascus, Hamath* *Emath*, and the Desert of Arabia with other Provinces *Prouinces* into one, under Rezin the second of the Adads) as it began with David, So it ended at once with the Kingdom of Israel. For Ahaz King of Judah *Iuda* waged the Assyrian Teglatphalassar against Pekah King of Israel, and against Rezin the last King of Damascus: which Teglath first invaded Damascena, and the region of Zobah, and took Damascus itself, and did put to death Rezin the last, carrying the inhabitants captive. This was the second time that the Assyrians attempted Israel. For first, Pul *Phul* Belochus entered the borders thereof( Menahem governing Israel) who stopped the enterprise of Pul with a thousand talents of silver: for this Pul Belochus, whose pedigree we will examine hereafter, being scarce warm as yet in his seat at Babylon, which 〈◊ 〉, with the help of his companion Arbaces, had wrested from Sardanapalus: having besides this King of Syria in his way, who seemed to be a great and strong Prince, was content to take the composition of a thousand talents of the King of Israel for that present time. But his son Teglath following the purpose of his Father Belochus, and finding So excellent an occasion, as the war begun between Israel and *Judah, Pekah *commanding in the one, and Ahaz *Achaz* in the other, his neighbour Rezin being also wrapped in that war, and wasted in strength thereby, did willingly accept the offer of Ahaz King of Judah, his imprest and entertainment. So, first attempting Damascus, which lay in his path towards Israel, he carried it( as is before remembered) and then with great ease possessed himself of the Cities of Nephthalim; leading with him a great part of the people captive. And his son Shalmaneser *Salmanassar*, whom Ptolemy *Ptolomie* calls 〈◊ 〉, after the revolt of Hosea, forced Samaria: and rent that Kingdom asunder. So as the line and race of Ninus in Sardanapalus, whom Belochus supplanted; the race and Monarchy of the Syrian 〈◊ 〉 in Rezin: whom Teglath slaughtered; the Kingdom of Israel in Hosea, whom Shalmaneser overturned; happened 〈◊ 〉 about a time: that of Ninus in the days of Belochus, and the other two in the days of Teglatphalassar, and Shalmaneser his son. For Sardanapalus perished, Uzziah *Osia* ruling Judah; and the other two Kingdoms were dissolved, Ahaz yet living.

## p02425 · sig. 255a

Lastly, the Kingdom of Judah itself, being attempted by 〈◊ 〉, the son of Shalmaneser in vain, and preserved for the time by God miraculously, was at length utterly overturned. Jerusalem *Hierusalem* and the Temple burnt 132. years after the Captivity *captiuitie* of Israel, and Samaria: the destruction of Israel being in the ninth year of Hosea: that of Judah in the 〈◊ 〉 of Zedechia. Now the Emperors *Emperours* of Assyria and Babylon held also the Kingdom of Syria, from the eight year of Shalmaneser, to the last of Belshazzar *Baltassar*, whom 〈◊ 〉 calls Labynetus *Labynitus*: in all about 200. years. After these the Persians from Cyrus to Darius their last King, held Syria about 200. years.

## p02426 · sig. 255b

Then Alexander Macedon *Maccdon* took this among other Provinces of the Persian Empire, and his successors the Seleucidae reigned therein, till it became subject unto the power of the Romans *Romanes*, from whom it was wrested long after by the Saracens, and remains now in possession of the Turk *Turke*, as shall be showed in due place. Thus much of the nations bordering upon the Israelites, with whom they had most to do both in war and peace, being the only people, whose History in those ancient times carried an assured face of truth.
