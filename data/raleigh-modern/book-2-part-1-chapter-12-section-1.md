## p02428 · sig. 255b

§. I. *Of divers memorable places in the Tribe of Benjamin* *Beniamin**, whereof 〈◊ 〉, Gilgal, Mizpah* *Mitspa**, Bethel, Rama, Gobah and Gibeah* *Gibha**.*

## p02429 · sig. 255b

OF the Tribe of Benjamin, the twelfth and youngest son of Jacob *Iacob*, whom he had by Rachel, there were mustered at Mount Sinai 35000. able bodies: all which perishing in the Deserts *Desarts*, there entered the holy Land of their Issues 45600. fit to 〈◊ 〉 Arms: and these had their Territory on this side Jordan, between Judah *Iuda* and Ephraim: The Cities within this Tribe 〈◊ 〉 Jordan, are *Lod, 〈◊ 〉*, and Ono: of which Lod and Ono were built by Shemed a Benjamite: they were all three reinhabited with Benjamites *Beniamites*, after the return out of Captivity *Captiuitie*, as is mentioned, Nehem. 11. 35. and Esd. 2. 35. where Adrichem *Adrichomius* reading *Lod, Hadid, Ono*, makes besides Hadid in Nehemiah *Nehemia*, a City called Lod-hadid *Lodhadid*: This Hadid or Hadid *Chadid* was rebuilt by Simon Maccabeus *Macchabaeus*.

## p02430 · sig. 255b

Samarim or Zemaraim *Tsemaraim*, named of Zemar *Tsemary* one of the sons of Canaan, was another of their Cities; and further into the Land stands Jericho *lericho*, one of the Toparchies, and the last of Judah; seated in a most fruitful valley, adorned with many palm trees: and therefore elsewhere called the city of Palms *Palmes*. From the time of JOSVA *IOSVA*, who utterly destroyed it, it lay waste until the time of Ahab *Achab*: in whose days Hiel *Chel* of Bethel laid the new foundation of it, in the loss of Abiram his eldest son, and built the gates of it in the loss of his youngest son Segub: according to the curse of JOSVA: in which and other respects, Hos. 12. 14. calls JOSVA a Prophet. In after-times it was destroyed by 〈◊ 〉, and rebuilt by Adrian.

## p02431 · sig. 255b

To the Southeast of Jericho stood * Almon *Halmon* of the Levites *Leuites*, of which Josh.. 21. 18. To the South Beth-arabah *Betharaba*, of which Josh.. c. 15. and c. 18. Then that Gilgal of which there is So much mention in the Scripture, where Joshua *Iosua* first ate of the fruits of the land, circumcised all those borne in the Deserts, and celebrated the Passover *Passeouer*.

## p02432 · sig. 256a

The reason of the name, or rather a memorable application of the Etymology of this name( for it seems by the place, Deut. 11. 30. that the name was known before the coming of the Israclites into Canaan) is noted Josh.. 5. 9. *Ob 〈◊ 〉 probri Aegyptiaci* ("Into the wilderness in [illegible] [illegible]")*, *because their fore-skins( the people being there circumcised) were tumbled down the Hill: which from thence was called Collis praeputiorum ("Hill of the Foreskins"). This Gilgal was also called Geliloth, as appears by comparing the places, Josh.. 15. 7. and 18. 17. for it was in the borders of Jordan, of which Josh.. 22. 13. and Geliloth signifies borders. * It stood( though in some distance) directly Eastward, over against the two Hills Garizim and Ebal *Hebal*: upon the one of which the blessings, and on the other the cursings were to be read to the people, both being the 〈◊ 〉 of Ephraim. Further, for the situation of this Gilgal, it is to be noted, that both it, and Mizpah of Benjamin( of which also we read 〈◊ 〉 in the Scripture) were seated about the midst of the length of the land of Canaan: for which reason * Samuel chose these two places, to either of which he came yearly to give judgement to the Israelites; of which two, Gilgal( as is said) was near Jordan on the 〈◊ 〉 side of this Tribe; and Mizpah near the West Sea, towards the land of the Philistines *Philistims*.

## p02433 · sig. 256a

The third place, which is named with these two, whither also Samuel used yearly to come, is * Bethel: which also was seated in this Tribe of Benjamin. But to return to Gilgal which was the first place, where the Ark resided, after they past over Jordan( from whence it was carried to Silo, and thence to Kiriath-jeharim *Kiriath-ieharim*, & at length to Jerusalem *Hierusalem*) here in Gilgal it was that Joshua pitched up the twelve stones, which were taken out of the channel of Jordan, when it was dry, that the Israelites might passe over it: by which Story, as it is set down Josh.. 4. it appears, that the same day that they passed over Jordan; they lodged at Gilgal. At the same Gilgal, to omit many other memorable things, it was that Samuel hewed Agag the King of the Amalekites in pieces. And as for Mizpah, whither also Samuel came yearly to give Judgement *Iudgement*, there also were often the greatest meetings held: as that for the revenge of the Levites wife against Gibeah, and the *Benjamites, Judg* *Iud**.*. 20. 1. and another against the Philistines, 1. Sam.. 7. 12. Thither also Judas *Iudas* Maccabeus gathered the Jews *Iewes*, ( when 〈◊ 〉 was possessed by the Heathen) as it is 1. Mac. 3. 47. in which place this reason of their meeting is added; *Quia locus Orationifuerat Mitspaeantea Israeli*. Touching this Mizpah, to avoid confusion, it is to be remembered, that the Scriptures mention four places of this name: Mizpah of Judah, of which Josh.. 15. 38. * Mizpah of Gilead, of which we have spoken already in the Tribe of *Gad: Mizpah *of the Moabites, where David *Dauid* for a while held himself, commending his parents to the King of Moab, 1. Sam.. 22. 3. and lastly, this chief Mizpah of the Benjamites. And as in this place the chief meetings were held both before Jerusalem was recovered from the Jebusites *Iebusites*, and also in the time of the Macchabees( as we have said) when Jerusalem was held by the wicked under Antiochus, So also in the time of Jeremiah *Hieremie*, after the destruction of the Temple by the *Chaldeans* *Chaldeys**, 〈◊ 〉 *whom Nebuchadnezzar *Nabuchodonosor* left in Jewry, as Governor over those that were left in the land, held his abiding in this place: until( to the great hurt of the Jews) he was slain by the treason of Ismael, one of the royal blood of Judah, as it is Jer *Hierem*. 14.

## p02434 · sig. 256a

Near unto this Mizpah, the * Scripture mentions Beth-car, after called 〈◊ 〉, that is, the Stone of help: where Samuel pitched up the pillar or Stone, for a Trophy against the Philistines.

## p02435 · sig. 256a

Touching Bethel which( as it seems) was the third place where Samuel held his chief meetings for the ministering of Justice, that it was anciently called Luz, and how it was taken by the issue of Joseph *Ioseph*( though it belonged to the portion of Benjamin, as it is Nehem. 11. 31. and Josh.. 18. 22.) and how another City called Luz * near adjoining to it, was built by the man of the City which showed the entrance to the Spies, as it is Judg. 1. and of the occasion of the name from Jacobs *Iacobs* vision: and how Jeroboam *Ieroboam*, by erecting one of his calves here, of Bethel( which signifies the house of God) made it * Beth-aven *Beth-auen*, that is, the house of Vanity, Hos. 4. 15. and 10. 5. as also other memorable things of this place, they are So well known, out of the Histories of the Scripture, that we may well passe them over.

## p02436 · sig. 256b

The territory of Bethel, which at the first belonged to the Kingdom of the ten Tribes, from the time of the great victory of Abia against Jeroboam( of which 2. Chronicles *Chr*. 13. ) was taken from them, and adjoined to the Kingdom of Judah: and So it continued, as appears by the Story of 〈◊ 〉: which performed the Prophecies against the altar of Bethel, 2. Reg. 23. whence those coasts 1. Mac. 11. 34. are called Aphaerema, which Greek *Greeke* word signifies as much as, *A thing taken away*, to wit, from the ten Tribes. It was one of the three Seigniories or Prefectures *Praesectures* which 〈◊ 〉 in his Epistle mentions, as added by him to the Dition of the Jews, out of the Samaritan Country. A part of it, as appears 2. Chron. 13. 19. was Ephrain *Hephrauin*, which Josh.. 18. 23. is called Ephrain, belonging to this Tribe of 〈◊ 〉.

## p02437 · sig. 256b

Not far from this Bethel, in this Tribe, we find three other Cities often mentioned in the Scriptures, *Rama, Gibeah*, and Gebah. Of the name Rama, * it is noted already, in the description of Ephraim, that there were many towns So called, because of their high situation. But whereas they find out Rama in the Tribe of Judah( as it seems because Mat. 2. it appears that it bordered Bethlehem) and also out of Brochard and Breidenbach make Silo to have been called Rama, and find yet another Rama in Zebulun *Zabulon*; these three have no warrant in the Scripture. Of Rama in the Tribe of Asher *Asser*, as it seems, we have testimony Josh.. 19. 29. and of another in *Nephthalim, Josh.*. 19. 36. of a third Rama, where Samuel dwelt in Mount Ephraim 1. Sam.. 25. 1. which more often is called * Ramatha, and 1. Sam.. 1. 1. Ramathaim Tsophim: for which the Septuagint have Ramathaim-Zophim *Aramathaim-sophim*, taking the Article affixed in the beginning, for a part of the word, whence they think Joseph of 〈◊ 〉 Matt.. 27. 57. was denominated.

## p02438 · sig. 256b

Of a fourth Rama we read 2. Reg. 8. 29. which is Ramoth in Gilead *Gilehad*. The first, which is most often mentioned, is Rama of 〈◊ 〉, seated as we said, near Bethel the uttermost South-border of the Kingdom of the ten Tribes: for which cause Baasha in the time of Asa King of Judah, fortified it, to hinder those that did fly from him to Asa. Of this Rama or Ramatha I should rather think Joseph was, that buried Christ: because it was nearer to Jerusalem, and after the Captivity belonged to 〈◊ 〉, as it appears Esd. 2. 26. where in that it is joined with Gebah, it is plain that he speaks of that Rama with whose stones( after Baasha had ceased to build it) Asa( as it is 1. Reg. 25. 22.) built Gebah adjoining to it: both being in Benjamin. And as Rama was the South-border of the ten Tribes, So was Gebah the North-border of the Kingdom of Judah: whence 2. Reg. 23. 8. we read that Josiah *Iosiah* through all his Kingdom, even from 〈◊ 〉, which was the North-border, to Beer-Sheba which was the South-border, destroyed the places of Idolatry.

## p02439 · sig. 256b

The third City Gibeah which was the City of Saul( the wickedness of which City in the time of the Judges *Iudges* had almost utterly rooted out this Tribe) Adrichem confounds with Gebah, making one of two( as they are evidently distinguished Isa *Esai*.. 10. 27. of which word * Gibeah, in another form Gibeah, he imagines Gibeah another City in this Tribe, making two of one. The vicinity of this city also to Rama of Benjamin, appears Judg.. 19. 13. where the Levite *Leuite* with his wife not able to reach to Rama, took up his lodging at Gibeah. By that place of 1. Sa. 22. 6. it seems that there was in this Gibeah some tower or Citadel called Rama: where Junius *Iunius* reads in excelso, for in Rama: but it may be that the name of the Kings place in this city, was Rama: as it seems that in Rama of Samuel, the name of the chief place where Samuel with the College of Prophets abode, was Naioth. The great City of Hai overthrown by Josh.. which Josh.. 7. 2. is placed near Beth-aven upon the East of Bethel, was in this Tribe, as is proved Nehe. 7. 10. 30. though it be not named by 〈◊ 〉. 18. for it was burned by him and laid desolate, as it is Josh.. 8. 28. *In solitudinem in* ("Augustine concerning [illegible] of God,")* 〈◊ 〉 〈◊ 〉*; Another City of chief note reckoned Josh.. 18. 25. in this Tribe was Gibhon, the chief City of the 〈◊ 〉: whose cunning to bind the Israelites by oath to save their lives, is set down Josh.. 9. whence they were reckoned among the * Nethinim *Nethinaei* or Proselites: and were bound to certain public services in the house of God: which oath of saving these Gibeonites broken in part after by Saul, was by God punished by a famine 2. Sam.. 21. 1. This Gibeon or Gibhon with Almon and Geba *Iebah*( of both which we have spoken) and with Hanothoth the natal place of Jeremiah the Prophet, were said Josh.. 21. 28. to be given to the Levites by the Benjamites. Near to this Hanothoth was Nob. as appears. 1. Reg. 2 26. where 〈◊ 〉 the Priest, which was of Nob. it was destroyed by Saul, is sent to his grounds at Hanothoth: It is reckoned in the Tribe of *Benjamin, Nehem*. 9. 31. and though in the time of Saul the residing place of the Ark was at Kiriath-icarim: yet by the lamentable tragedy of bloodshed, which Saul raised in this place( as it is set down 1. Sam.. 21. and 22.) in the judgement of Junius, it is proved that the Tabernacle was there for a time.
