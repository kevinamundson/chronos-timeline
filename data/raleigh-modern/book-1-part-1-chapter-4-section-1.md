## p00997 · sig. 78a

§. I. *That the Tree of Life was a material Tree: and in what sense it is to be taken, that man by his eating the forbidden fruit, is made subject to death.*

## p00998 · sig. 78a

FOR eating the forbidden fruit of the Tree of Knowledge was Adam driven out of *Paradise, in exilium vita temporalis, into the banishment of temporal life, *says Bede *Beda*. That these Trees of Life and Knowledge were material Trees( though Figures of the Law and of the Gospel) it is not doubted by the most religious and learned Writers: although the wits of men, which are So volatile, as nothing can fix them, and So slippery, as nothing can fasten them, have in this also delivered to the World an imaginary doctrine.

## p00999 · sig. 78a

The Tree of Life( say the Hebrews *Hebrewes*) has a plural construction, and is to be understood, *Lignum vitarum, The Tree of lives, *because the fruit thereof had a property, to preserve both the growing, sensitive, and rational life of man; and not only( but for Adams transgression) had prolonged his own days, but also given a enduring continuance to all posterity; and that, So long, as a body compounded of Elements could last.

## p01000 · sig. 78a

And although it is hard to think, that flesh and blood could be immortal, but that it must once perish and rot, by the unchanged Law of God imposed on his creatures, Man( notwithstanding) should have enjoyed thereby a long, healthful, and ungrieved life: after which( according to the opinion of most Divines *Diuines*) he should have been translated, as Enoch was. And as before the Flood *Floud*, the days of men had the long measure of eight hundred or nine hundred years; and soon after the Flood, of two hundred years and upwards, even to five hundred: So if Adam had not disobeyed Gods first and easy Commandment, the lives of men on Earth might have continued double, treble, or quadruple to any of the longest times of the first age, as many learned men have conceived. *Chrysostom* *Chrysostome**, Rupertus, Tostatus*, and others were of belief, that( but for Adams fall and transgression) Adam and his Posterity had been immortal. But such is the infinite wisdom of God, as he foresaw that the Earth could not have contained Mankind; or else, that Millions of souls must have been ungenerated, and have had no being, if the first number, wherewith the Earth was replenished, had abode thereon for ever: and therefore that of Chrysostom must be understood of immortality of bodies, which should have been translated and glorified.

## p01001 · sig. 78a

But of what kind or Species this Tree of Life was, no man has taken on him to teach: in which respect many have conceived, that the same was not material, but a mere Allegory, taking their strength out of Solomon *Salomon*, where Wisdom is compared to the Tree of Life, and from other places, where also Christ is called the Tree of Life, and out of the *Apocalypse* *Apocalypsis**, I will give to him that over comes, to eat of the Tree* *of Life, which is in the Paradise of God. *But to this place Saint Augustine's *Augustines* answer may suffice, ( which is) That the one does not exclude the other, but that, as there was a terrestrial Paradise, So there was a celestial. For although Agar and Sara were Figures of the Old, and New Testament, yet to think that they were not Women, and the Maid and Wife of Abraham, were mere foolishness. And So in this place the sense of the Scripture is manifest. *For God brought out of the earth every tree fair to sight, and sweet to taste; the tree also of Life in the midst of the garden: *which shows, that among the trees, which the Earth by Gods commandment produced, the tree of Life was one, and that the fruit thereof was also to be eaten. The report of this Tree was also brought to the ancient Poets: for as from the undigested matter or *Chaos, Hesiod* *Hesiodus**, Homer, Ovid* *Ouid**, *and others, steal the invention of the created World; So from the Garden of Paradise, they took the Plat-form *Plat-forme* of the Orchard of Alcinous, and another of the Hesperides: and from the tree of Life, their Nectar and Ambrosia; for Nectar, according to 〈◊ 〉, signifies making young, and *Ambrosia, immortality*; and therefore said to be the meat and drink of the gods.
