§. V. THE TRIBE OF ZEBULUN *ZABVLON*.

## p02119 · sig. 227b

OF Zebulun or Zebulon, another of the sons of Jacob *Iacob* by Lea, there were mustered at Mount 〈◊ 〉 57400. able men besides women, children, and aged unable persons: all which dying in the Deserts *Desarts*, there entered the Holy Land of their issues 65000. fit to bear arms: who inhabited that part of Canaan, from Asher to the River Kishon *Chison*: Southward, and from the Sea of Galilee to the 〈◊ 〉, East and West.

The Cities within this Tribe which border Asher, are 〈◊ 〉 on the Sea shore, of which *Joseph* *Ioseph**. Ant*. 13. c. 19. Debbaset of * which Josh.. 19. 11. 〈◊ 〉 or 〈◊ 〉( whose King was a slain by Joshua *Iosua*, and the City was given to the Levites *Leuites*) and Geba *Gaba* after called the City of Horsemen, of a Regiment there garrisoned by Herode. Then the City which bears the name of Zebulun, or the *City of men*, exceeding ancient and magnificent, b burnt to the ground by 〈◊ 〉, Lieutenant of the Roman Army. Adrichem *Adrichomius* makes it the birth City of c Elon Judge of Israel, because 〈◊ 〉 is called Zebulonite *Zabulonita*: not marking that in the same place he is said to be buried at Aialon.

## p02121 · sig. 227b

To the East of this City of Zebulun is Kattath *Cateth*, of which Josh.. 19. 15. on the border of Asher: and beyond it the lesser d Cana of Galilee, where Christ converted Water into Wine: the native City of Nathaniel, and as it is thought of Simon Zealot *Zelotes*. Beyond it begin the Mountains *Mountaines* of Zebulun: and then the City of Kitron *Cethron*( in *Zeigler, 〈◊ 〉*) which defended it self against Zebulun. Then Bersabe which stands in the partition of the upper and nether Galilee, fortified by Josephus *Iosephus* against the Romans *Romanes*. Not far from hence stands Shimron of Meron whose King was slain by Joshua.

## p02122 · sig. 227b

Then Dimnah *Damna* or Dimnah, a City of the Levites: then Noa or rather Neah *Neha*, of which Josh.. 19. 13. Then Dothan or Dothan *Dothain*, where Joseph found his brethren feeding their flocks: the same wherein Elisha *Elisoeus* besieged by the Syrians, struck them all blind.

## p02123 · sig. 227b

Beyond it towards the East they imagine e Amthar or Amathar: then Rimmon *Remmon* of the Levites. The last of the Cities on the North border of 〈◊ 〉 is Bethsaida, one of the ten Cities of Decapolis, situate on the Galilean Sea, and watered by the springs of Capharnaum, the native City of the Apostles, *Peter, Andrew*, and Philip. Herein Christ did many miracles, but these people being no less incredulous then the Capernaumites *Capharnaims*, and others, received the same curse of threatened miseries, as *Woe be unto thee Bethsaida, &c.*

## p02124 · sig. 228a

Along the West border of Galilee, towards the South from Bethsaida, was the strong Castle of Magdalum, the habitation of Marie Magdalene *Magdalen*, not long since standing.

## p02125 · sig. 228a

And beyond it the strong and high seated City of Jotapata *Iotapata*: fortified by Josephus in the Roman War: but in the end after a long siege surprised by Vespasian: who slaughtered many thousands of the Citizens: and held 1200. prisoners, whereof Josephus the Historian was one.

## p02126 · sig. 228a

The last and greatest of the Cities on that * Sea and the Lake of Genezareth within Zebulun was that of Tiberias, from whence afterward the Galilean Sea also changed name, and was called the Sea of the City Tiberias, So named in honour of Tiberius Caesar, it was one of the ten Cities, and the Metropolis of the Region Decapolitan, and the greatest and last of the lower Galilee. From hence our Saviour called Matthew, from the toll or custom-house, to be an Apostle, and near unto it raised the daughter of Jairus *Iairus* from death: it was built( as Josephus reports) by Herod the Tetrarch, the brother of Philip, in the beginning of the reign of Tiberius Caesar: in the most fruitful part of Galilee; but in a ground full of *Sepulchres: Quum juxta nostras leges*( says he) *ad septem dies impurus habeatur, qui in talibus locis habitet; Where as by our law he should be 〈◊ 〉 days held as unclean, who inhabited in such a place: *by which words, and by the whole place of Josephus it appears, that this Tiberias is not( as some have thought) the same as the old Cinnereth, which was seated, not in Zebulun, but in Nepthalim.

## p02127 · sig. 228a

Near unto this Tiberias, at Emaus, there were hot baths, where Vespasian the Emperor encamped against Tiberias: More into the Land toward the South-West is Bethulia, seated on a very high Hill, and of great strength, famous by the story of Holofernes and Judeth *Iudeth*, such as it is. Near which stands Bethleem of Zebulun: and adjoining unto it, 〈◊ 〉 fortified by Josephus against the Romans: and Japha *Iapha* an exceeding strong place afterward forced by Titus: who in the entrance, and afterward in fury slew 15000. of the Citizens; and carried away above two thousand prisoners.

## p02128 · sig. 228a

On the South side are the Cities of Kartah *Cartha* of the Levites, and Gabara, of which Josephus in his own life, then Japhia *Iafie* according to Adrichem( of which Josh.. 19. 12.) for he thinks that it is not that Japha of which we spoke but now out of *Josephus. 〈◊ 〉 *of which Josh.. 19. 15. Jerome *Hierome* calls it Idalah *Iadela*: under it Westward, Legio, ( afterward a Bishops seat) and the City Belma, in ancient times exceeding strong, remembered Judeth 7. 3. otherwise Belma *Chelma*. Between Legio and Nazareth *Nazeret* is the City Saffa or Saffra, the birth-City of *Zebedee* *Zebedaeus**, Alphaeus, James* *Iames** *and John *Iohn*: Then Sephoris, or Sephora, according to *Josephus: Sephorum *according to Brochard: which afterward, says Hegesippus and Jerome, was called Diocaesaria; the City of Joachim and Anna, the Parents of the Virgin Mary, it was walled by Herod the Tetrarch: and by him, as Josephus speaks, made the head and defence of Galilee; in another place he says Urbium *Vrbium* Galilaearum *maximae Sephoris & Tiberias* ("Gilboa, in the valley near the mountains of Acrabattene,"). This Sephoris greatly vexed Vespasian 〈◊ 〉 he wan it. Herod Antipas when he made it the Regal seat of the nether Galilee, and So rounded it with a strong wall, called it Autocratorida, which is as much to say as Imperial, says Josephus; and it is now but a Castle called Safed *Zaphet*.

## p02129 · sig. 228a

To the South-West of this Sephoris or Diocaesaria was that blessed place of Nazareth, the City of Marie the Mother of Christ; in which he himself was conceived, it stands between Mount Tabor, and the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea. In this City he abode chiefly four and twenty years, and was therefore called a Nazarite, as the Christians afterward were for many years. It was erected into an Archbishopric in the following age. Near unto it are the Cities Buria( afterward well defended against the Turks *Turkes*) and Nahalal of which Josh.. 19. 15. and Judg *Iud*.. 1. 30. where it is called Nahalal: and Josh.. 21. 35. where it is a City of the Levites, near the Sea; adjoining to the River of Kishon is Sarid, noted in Joshua for the uttermost of Zebulun.

## p02130 · sig. 228b

In this Territory of Zebulun there are divers small Mountains: but Tabor is the most renowned, by the Apparition of Moses and Elias: and by the Transfiguration of Christ in the presence of *Peter, James*, and John: unto whom Moses and Elias appeared; in memory whereof on the top of the Mountain, the Empress Helen built a sumptuous Chappell.

## p02131 · sig. 228b

The chief River of Zebulun is Kishon, which rising out of Tabor, runs with one stream Eastward to the Sea of Galilee, and with an other stream Westward into the great Sea. This River of Kishon where it rises, and So far as it runs Southward, is called Chedumim or Cadumim: and for mine own opinion, I take it to be the same which Ptolemy *Ptolomie* calls Chorseus: though others distinguish them: and set Chorseus by Caesarea *Caesaria* Palestine *Palaestinae*. There is a second Torrent or Brook that rises in the Hills of 〈◊ 〉; and falls into the Sea of Galilee by Magdalum: and the third is a branch of a river rising out of the Fountains *Fountaines* of Capharnaum, which falls also into the same Sea, and near Magdalum: which Torrent they call Dothan, from the name of the City, from which it passes Eastward to Bethsaida, and So joining with Jordanis *Iordanis* 〈◊ 〉, which runs from the Valley of Jephthael *Iephthael* which Joshua reckons in the bounds of Zebulun, it ends in the Sea of Galilee.
