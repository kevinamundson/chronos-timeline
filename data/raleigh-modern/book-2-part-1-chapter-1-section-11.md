## p01604 · sig. 165b

§. XI. Of TIDAL another of the sour Kings.

## p01605 · sig. 165b

The fourth King by Abraham overthrown was Tidal, king of the Nations. The Hebrew writes it Goijm, which Vatablus takes to be a proper name: 〈◊ 〉 of mixed people: Calvin *Caluin* of renegades without habitation: Pereira *Pererius* out of Strabo, finds that Galilaea was inhabited by divers Nations, which were a mixed people: namely of *Egyptians* *Aegyptians**, Arabians*, and *Phoenicians. Nam tales sunt qui Galilaeam habitant; Such are the inhabitants of Galilee, *says Strabo: and therefore was Tidal called king of these Nations, as they suppose. And it may be So: but the authority of Strabo is nothing in this question. For Galilaea was not peopled at this time, as it was in the time of Strabo. For when Abraham came into Canaan, the Canaanite *Cananite* was then in the Land, howsoever they might be afterwards mixed; which I know not. But there are many petty kingdoms adjoining to Phoenicia, and Paloestina; as *Palmurena* *Palmvrena**, Batanea, Laodicene, Apamena, Chalcidice, Casstotis, Chalibonitis, *and all these do also join themselves to Mesopotamia, on the North, and to Arabia on the East. And that these Nations gathered themselves together under Tidal, I take to be the most probable conjecture.
