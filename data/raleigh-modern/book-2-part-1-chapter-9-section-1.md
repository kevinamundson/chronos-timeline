§. I. Of the memorable places in the Tribe of EPHRAIM.

## p02224 · sig. 236a

HAVING now past over Phoenicia, we come to the next Territory adjoining: which is that of Ephraim: sometimes taken per excellentiam for the whole Kingdom of the ten Tribes. Ephraim was the second son of Joseph *Ioseph*, whose issues when they left Egypt *Aegypt* were in number 45000. all which dying in the Deserts *Desarts*( 〈◊ 〉 excepted) there entered the Holy Land of their children grown to be able men 32500. who sate down on the West side of Jordan, between Manasseh *Manasse*, and Benjamin *Beniamin*: who bounded Ephraim by the North and South; as Jordan, and the Mediterranean *Mediterran* Sea, did by the East and West:

## p02225 · sig. 236a

The first and chief City which Ephraim had, was Samaria, the Metropolis of the Kingdom of Israel, built by Omri *Amris* or Homri King thereof, and seated on the top of the Mountain 〈◊ 〉, which over-looks all the bottom, and as far as the Sea-coast. It was afterward called Sebaste, or Augusta, in honour of *August. Caesar*. This City is often remembered in the Scriptures: and magnificent it was in the first building; for as Brochard observes, the ruins which yet remain, and which Brochard found greater than those of Jerusalem *Hierusalem*, tell those that behold them, what it was when it stood upright: for to this day there are found great store of goodly marble pillars, with other hewn and carved stone in great abundance, among the rubble.

## p02226 · sig. 236a

It was beaten to the ground by the Sons of Hyrcanus *Hircanus* the high Priest: restored and built by the first Herod the son of Antipater: who to flatter Caesar called it Sebaste. Herein were the Prophets Helisaeus, and Obadiah *Abdias* buried: and So was John *Iohn* Baptist. It now has nothing but a few Cottages filled with Grecian Monks *Monkes*.

## p02227 · sig. 236a

Near Samaria toward the South, is the Hill of Bethel, and a town of that name: on the top of which Mountain Jeroboam *Ieroboam* erected one of his golden Calves, to be worshipped: with which he seduced the Israelites.

## p02228 · sig. 236a

In sight of this Mountain of Bethel, was that ancient City of Shechem *Sichem*; after the restoration called Neapolis, now Neapolis *Pelosa*, and Neapolis: It was destroyed by Simeon and Levi *Leui*, in revenge of the ravishment of their sister Dina: and after that by Abimelec cuened¹ with the soil. Jeroboam raised it up again: and the Damascens a third time cast it down.

## p02229 · sig. 236b

Under Shechem toward the Sea stands Pirathon *Pharaton* or Pirathon on the Mountain Amalek *Amelec*, the City of Abdon Judge of Israel. And under it Bethoron of the Levites *Leuites*, built as it is said by Sara, the Daughter of Ephraim. Near to this City Judas *Iudas* Maccabeus *Macchabaeus* overthrew Seron and Lysias, Lieutenants to Antiochus. This City had Solomon *Salomon* formerly repaired and fortified.

## p02230 · sig. 236b

Between Bethoron and the Sea, stands Samir, of which Josh.. 10. And Saron whose King was slain by Joshua *Iosua*: it is also mentioned Acts 9. 35. and of this Saron the Valley takes name, which beginning at Caesarea Palestine *Palaestinae*, extends it self along the coast as far as Joppa *Ioppe*, says Adrichome. Though indeed the name Sarona is not particularly given to this Valley, but to every fruitful plain Region; for not only this Valley is So called, to wit, between Caesarea and Joppa, but that also between the Mountain Tabor and the Sea of Galilee: for So S. Jerome *Hierome* upon the five and thirtieth Chapter of Isaiah *Esay* interprets the word Saron: and So does the same Father in his Commentaries upon Abdia, read Saron for Sharon *Assaron*: understanding thereby a Plain near Lidda: which Lidda in his time was called Diospolis, or the City of Jupiter, one of the Toparchies of Judea *Iudaea*, the fist in dignity( or the third after Pliny *Plinie*) where Saint PETER( *non suased Christi virtute* ("Leaving on the right the maritime places Antipatris and Joppa, passing through the wide-spreading plain of Eleutheria, [illegible] which is Diospolis,")) cured *Aeneas. Niger *calls all that Region from Anti-Lebanon *Anti-libanus* to Joppa Sarona. This Joppa was burnt to the ground by the Romans *Romanes*, those Ravens and spoilers of all Estates, disturbers of Common-weals *Common-weales*, usurpers of other Princes Kingdoms *Kingdomes*; who with no other respect led than to amplify their own glory, troubled the whole World: and themselves, after murdering one another, became a prey to the most salvage and barbarous Nations.

## p02231 · sig. 236b

In Diospolis( says Will. of Tyre) was S. George beheaded, and buried: in whose honour and memory Justinian the Emperor caused a fair Church to be built over his Tomb, these be Tyrius his words: *Relicta a dextris locis maritimis Antipatride, & Joppa, per late patentem planitiem Eleutheriam pertranseuntes, 〈◊ 〉 quae est Diospolis* ("where also [the tomb] of the excellent Martyr [is];")*,* *ubi & egregij Martyris* ("even to this day the sepulchre is shown, they came, whose church, when a pious and orthodox prince of the Romans, [built it] to the honour of the same Martyr,")* *GEORGIJ *usque hodte Sepulchrum ostenditur, peruenerunt, eius Ecclesiam quum ad honorem eiusdem Martyris pius & orthodoxus Princeps Romanorum* ("Hiddai from one of the valleys of Gaash")*,* AVGUSTUS *AVGVSTVS* IVSTINIANUS *IVSTINIANVS* *multo studio & devotione prompta aedificari praeceperat, &c. They having left*( says he) *on the right hand, the Sea Towns* *Townes** Antipatris, and Joppa, passing over the great open plain of Eleutheria, came to Lidda, which is Diospolis: where the sumptuous Tomb of the famous Martyr *S. GEORGE *is at this day showed; whose Church, when the Godly and Orthodox Prince of the Romans, High and Mighty *JUSTINIAN *had commanded to be built, with great earnestness and present devotion, &c. *Thus far Tyrius, by whose testimony we may conjecture that this S. George was not that Arrian Bishop of Alexandria; but rather some better Christian: for this of Alexandria was slain there in an uproar of the people, and his ashes cast into the Sea, as Ammianus Marcellinus reports. And yet also it may be, that this Georgius was a better Christian, than he is commonly thought: for his words of the Temple of GENIVS, *How long shall this Sepulchre stand? *occasioned the uproar of the people against him: as fearing lest he would give attempt to overthrow that beautiful Temple. This also Marcellinus reports; who though he say that this Georgius was also deadly hated of the Christians, who else might have rescued him: yet he adds that his ashes, with the ashes of two others, were therefore cast into the Sea, lest if their Relics *Reliques* had been gathered up, Churches should be built for them, as for others. But for my part, I rather think that it was not this Georgius, whose name lives in the right honourable Order of our Knights of the Garter, but rather another, whom Tyrius, above cited, witnesses to have been buried at Lidda or Diospolis. The same also is confirmed by Vitry *Vitriac*. S. Jerome affirms that it was sometime called Tigrida, and while the Christians inhabited the Holy Land, it had a Bishop Suffragan.

## p02232 · sig. 236b

Near to Lidda or Diospolis stands Ramatha of the Levites, or Arimathea *Aramathia*: afterward Rama, and Ramleh *Ramula*, the native City of Joseph, which buried the body of Christ. There are many places which bear this name of Rama; one they set in the Tribe of Judah *Iuda* near Thecua in the way of Hebron *Hcbron*; another in Naphtali *Nephtalim*, not far from Sephet; a third in Zebulun *Zabulon*, which, they say, adjoins to Sephoris; a fourth, which they make the same with Silo, 〈◊ 〉 a fifth, which is this Rama, in the Hills *Hils* of Ephraim, called Rama-Zophim *Rama-Sophim*, where Samuel lived; and wherein he is buried.

## p02233 · sig. 237a

From hence to the North along the coast are Helon, or Aialon of the Levites, of which 1. Chron.. Apollonia, of which Josephus *Iosephus* in his Antiquities, and in the War of the Jews *Iewes*. Also Baal-shalishah *Balsalisa*( for which Innius, 2. Reg. 4. 42. reads planities Shalistae) they place hereabout in this Tribe of Ephraim *Epbraim*; but Junius *Iunius* upon 1. Sam.. 6. where we read of the Land of 〈◊ 〉, finds it in Benjamin.

## p02234 · sig. 237a

On the other side of the Mountains *Mountaines* of Ephraim stands Gophna *Gosna*, one of the Toparchies or Cities of government, the second in dignity, of which the Country about it takes name.

## p02235 · sig. 237a

Then Timnath-sara *Thamnath-sara*, or according to the *Hebrew, Thimnath-Serah* *Thimnath-Serach*: one also of the ten Toparchies or 〈◊ 〉 of 〈◊ 〉, which they call Timnath *Thamnitica*; a goodly City and strong, seated on one of the high Hills of Ephraim; on the North of the Hill called Gaas; which City and Territory Israel gave unto their Leader Joshua; who also amplified it with buildings, near which he was buried. His Sepulchre remained in S. Hieroms time, and over it the Sun engraved, in memory of that greatest of wonders, which God wrought in Joshua's *Iosua's* time.

## p02236 · sig. 237a

In the places adjoining stands Adasa *Adarsa*, or Adasa; where Judas Maccabeus with 3000. Jews overthrew the Army of Nicanor, Lieutenant of Syria; near to Gezer *Gaser* or Gezer which Joshua took, and hung their King; a City of the Levites. It was afterward taken by Pharaoh *Pharao* of Egypt; the people all slain, and the City razed; Solomon rebuilt it.

## p02237 · sig. 237a

To the East of this place is the Frontier City of Japhlet *Iefleti*, of which Josh.. 16. 3. otherwise Pelethites *Pelethi*, whence David *Dauid* had part of his Praetorian Soldiers *Souldiers*, under the charge of Benaiah *Benaia*. Then that high and famous Mountain and City of Silo, whereon the Ark of God 〈◊ 〉 kept So many years, till the Philistines *Philistims* got it.

## p02238 · sig. 237a

To this they join the City of Machmas or Michmas: in which Jonathas *Ionathas* Maccabeus inhabited, a place often remembered in the Scriptures. It stands in the common way from Samaria towards Jerusalem: and is now called Byra.

## p02239 · sig. 237a

Then the Village of Naioth where Saul prophesied; and near it Ephron, one of those Cities which Abijah recovered from Jeroboam; after the great overthrow given him. Then Kibzaim *Kibtsaim* of the Levites, of which Joshua 21. 22. which Junius thinks to be the same with Jokmeam *Iokmeham*, of which 1. Chron *Chro*.. 6. 28. As for Absaloms Baal-hazor *Baalasor*, which they find hereabout, Junius reads it the *Plain of Chatzor*; and finds it in the Tribe of Judah; as Josh.. 15. we read of two Hazors *Chatzors* in that Tribe; one near Kedesh v. 23. and the other the same as Hezron *Chetzron* v. 25.

## p02240 · sig. 237a

In this Tribe also they find the City of Millo *Mello*; whose Citizens, they say, joined with the 〈◊ 〉 in making the Bastard Abimelec King: adding that for the building thereof with other Cities, Solomon raised a Tribute upon the people. But it seems that 〈◊ 〉 or Millo is a common name of a strong Fort or Cittadell: and So Junius for domus Millo, reads incolae munitionis, and for *Solomon aedificabit Millo*, he reads aedificabat munitionem, and So the Septuagint read 〈◊ 〉 in that place. And without doubt the Millo which Solomon built, cannot be that of Shechem, but another in Jerusalem.

## p02241 · sig. 237a

The other Cities of mark in Ephraim, are Taphuach, whose King was slain by Joshua; and 〈◊ 〉 or Janoah *Ianoah* spoiled by *Teglatphalassar; Pekah *then governing Israel; with divers others, but of no great fame.

## p02242 · sig. 237a

The Mountains of Ephraim sometime signify the greatest part of the Land of the Sons of Joseph, on the West of Jordan: several parts whereof are the Hill of Samaria *Samron* or Samaria, 1. Reg. 16. 24. * the Hill of *Gaash* *Gahas**, Judg* *Iudg*. 2. 9. the Hill of Zalmon *Tsalmon* or *Salmon, Judg*. 9. 48. the Hills of the Region of Zuph *Tsuph* or *Tsophim, Judg*. 9. 5. where Rama-Tsophim stood, which was the City of Samuel.

## p02243 · sig. 237b

The great plenty of fruitful Vines upon the sides of the Mountains, was the occasion that Jacob *Iacob* in the Spirit of Prophecy, Genes. 49. 22. compared Josephs *Iosephs* two branches, Ephraim and Manasseh, to the branches of a fruitful Vine planted by the Well side, and spreading her a Daughter-branches along the Wall: which Allegory also Ezekiel c. 22. in his Lamentation for Ephraim( that is, for the ten Tribes, whose head was Ephraim) prosecutes: as also in his Lamentation for Judah, he follows the other Allegory of *Jacob, Gen. *49. 9. comparing Judah to a Lyon. Upon the top of one of the highest of these Hills of 〈◊ 〉, which over-looks all the plains on both sides of Jordan, they find the Castle called Docus *Dok*: which they make to be the same with Dagon, of which Joseph. 1. *Bell. Judg. c*. 2. in which Castle as it is 1. Maccab. 16 Ptolemy *Ptolomie* most traitorously, at a banquet, slew Simon Maccabeus his Father-inLaw.

## p02244 · sig. 237b

Among the Rivers *Riuers* of this Tribe of Ephraim, they name Gaas, remembered in the second of *Samuel, c*. 23. v. 30. where though 〈◊ 〉 read *Hiddai ex una vallium Gahasi* ("Its walls with [which he fortified it];"): yet the Vulgar and Vatablus read 〈◊ 〉 of the River of Gaas. Also in this Tribe they place. the River of Cherith *Carith*, by which the Prophet Elijah *Eltas* abode during the great drought: where he was b fed with the Ravens: and after that the River was dried up, he travailed( by the Spirit of God guided) towards Sidon: where he was relieved by the poor Widow of Zarephath *Zarepta*, whose dead son he revived, and increased her pittance of Meal and Oil: whereby she sustained her life.
