§. III. *That the good knowledge in the ancient 〈◊ 〉 is not to be condemned: though the Devil here as in other kinds has sought to obtrude evil things, under the name and colour of good things.*

Seeing therefore it is confessed by all of understanding, that a 〈◊ 〉( according to the 〈◊ 〉 word) is no other than, *〈◊ 〉 〈◊ 〉 & interpres: A 〈◊ 〉 observer and 〈◊ 〉 of divine things: *and the Art it self( I mean the Art of natural Magic) no other, *Quam naturalis Philosophiae absoluta consummatio; Then the absolute perfection of 〈◊ 〉 Philosophy: *Certainly then it proceeds from common ignorance, and no way sorts with wise and learned men 〈◊ 〉 and without difference and distinction, to confound lawful and praise-worthy knowledge with that impious, and( to use Saint Paul's *Pauls* words) *with those beggarly 〈◊ 〉, *which the Devil has shuffled in, and by them bewitches and befools graceless men. For if we condemn natural Magic, or the wisdom of Nature, because the Devil( who knows more than any man) does also teach Witches and Poysoners the harmful parts of Herbs *Herbes*, Drugs *Drugges*, Minerals, and Excrements: then may we by the same rule condemn the Physician, and the Art of healing. For the Devil also in the Oracles of *Amphiaraus, Amphilochus, Trophonius*, and the like, taught men in Dreams *Dreames* what Herbs and Drugs were proper for such and such diseases. Now no man of judgement is ignorant, that the Devil from the beginning has sought to thrust himself into the same employment among the Ministers and Servants *Seruants* of God, changing himself for that purpose into an Angel of Light. He has led men to Idolatry as a doctrine of Religion; he has thrust in his Prophets among those of the true God; he has corrupted the Art of Astrology, by giving a divine power to the Stars *Starres*, teaching men to esteem them as Gods, and not as instruments. And( as Bunting observes) it is true, that judicial 〈◊ 〉 is corrupted with many superstitions: but the abuse of the thing takes not away the Art; considering that heavenly bodies( as even general experience shows) have and exercise their operation upon the 〈◊ 〉. For the Sun, and the Star of 〈◊ 〉 do dry; the Moon does moisten, and govern the Tides of the Sea. Again, the Planets, as they have several and proper names, So have they several and proper virtues: the Stars do also differ in beauty and in 〈◊ 〉; and to all the Stars has God given also their proper names, which( had they not influences and virtues different) needed not: *He counts the number of the Stars, and calls them by their names. *But into the good and profitable knowledge of the celestial influences, the Devil ceases not to 〈◊ 〉 in his 〈◊ 〉: and So to the knowledge of the secret virtues of Nature has he fastened his doctrine of Characters, Numbers, and Incantations; and taught men to believe in the strength of Words and Letters: ( which without Faith in God are but Ink or common breath) thereby either to equal his own with the All-powerful *All-powerfull* Word of God, or to diminish the glory of Gods creating Word, by whom are all things.

Moreover, he was never ignorant, that both the wise and the simple observe when the Sea-birds forsake the shores and fly into the Land, that commonly some great storm follows; that the high flying of the Kite and the Swallow betoken fair weather; that the crying of Crows *Crowes* and bathing of Ducks *Duckes* foreshow rain: for they feel the Air moistened in their Quills *Quils*. And it is written in Jeremiah *Hieremie* the Prophet, *Even the Stork in the air knows her appointed times, and the Turtle, and the Crane, and the Swallow. *Hereupon, this enemy of Mankind working upon these as upon the rest of Gods creatures, long time abused the Heathen by teaching them to observe the flying of Fowls *Fowles*, and thereby to judge of good or ill success in the War: and( withal) to look into their entrails for the same, as if God had written the secrets of unsearchable providence in the livers and bowels of birds and beasts. Again, because it pleased God sometimes by Dreams, not only to warn and teach his Prophets and Apostles, but Heathen Princes also; as Abimelech to restore 〈◊ 〉 to ABRAHAM; because he admonished Joseph *Ioseph*, and by Dream informed *Jacob* *Iacob**, Laban, 〈◊ 〉, Solomon* *Salomon**, Paul, Ananias, *the Magi of the East, and others. For as it is remembered in JOB *IOB*: *In Dreams and Visions of the night when sleep falls upon men, &c. Then* *God opens the ears, that he might cause man to return from his enterprise: *therefore, I say, does the Devil also practise his Divinations *Diuinations* by Dreams, or( after Parisiensis) *divinitatis imitations, his mock-divinity*. This in the end grew So common, as ARISTIDES compiled an Ephemerides of his own Dreams: Mithridates of those of his Concubines. Yea the Romans *Romanes* finding the inconvenience hereof, because all Dreams( without distinction of causes) were drawn to Divination, forbade the same by a Law, as by the words of prohibition *( aut narrandis somnijs occultam aliquam artem divinandi* ("(or by telling dreams [they practise] some hidden art of divination)")*)* it may appear. Likewise by the Law of God in Deuteronomy cap. 13. seducing Dreamers were ordered to be slain. Yet it is to be contemned, not that Marcus Antonius was told a remedy in his Dream for two grievous diseases that oppressed him; nor that of Alexander Macedon for the cure of Ptolemies *Ptolomies* poisoned wound; nor that which Saint AVGUSTINE *AVGVSTINE* reports of a Milanese *Millanoise*; whose son( the Father dead) being demanded a debt already payed, was told by his Father in a Dream where the Acquittance lay to discharge it: nor that of Astyages of his Daughter, and many others of like nature. Of the reason of all which, inasmuch as the cause is not in our selves, this place denies dispute.
