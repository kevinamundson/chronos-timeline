§. IIII. *Of the vain and malicious reports of Heathen writers, touching the ancient Jews* *Iewes**.*

## p02457 · sig. 258b

OF the original of the Jews, profane writers have conceived diversely and injuriously. Quintilian speaks infamously of them; and of their leader; who( says he) gathered together a pernicious Nation. Diodorus *Diodore* and 〈◊ 〉 make them Egyptians *Aegyptians*. Others affirm that while Isis governed Egypt *Aegypt*, the people were So increased, as Jerosolymus *Ierosolymus*, and Judas *Iudas* led thence a great multitude of that Nation, with whom they planted the neighbour Regions; which might be meant by Moses and Aaron: for the name of Moses was accidental, because he was taken up and saved out of the waters. But Justin *Iustine*, of all other most malicious, does derive the Jews from the Syrian Kings; of whom, Damascus, says he, was the first: and to him succeeded *Abraham, Moses*, and Israel. He again supposes( somewhat contrary to himself) that Israel had ten sons, among whom he divided the land of Judah *Iuda*; So called of Judas his eldest, who had the greatest portion. The youngest of the sons of Israel he calls Joseph *Ioseph*: who being brought up in Egypt, became learned in magical Arts, and in the interpretations of Dreams *Dreames*, and signs prodigious, and this Joseph( says he) was father to Moses: who with the rest, by reason of their foul diseases, and lest they should infect others, were banished Egypt. Further, he tells how these men thus banished, when in the Deserts *Desarts* they suffered extreme thirst and famine, and therein found relief the seventh *seuenth* day, for this cause ever after observed the seventh day, and kept it Holy; making it a Law among themselves, which afterward became a branch of their Religion. He adds also that they might not marry out of their own Tribes, left discovering their uncleanness, they might also be expelled by other Nations, as they were by the Egyptians. These and like fables has Justin.

## p02458 · sig. 258b

Cornelius Tacitus does as 〈◊ 〉 belie them, in affirming, That in the inmost Oratory of their Temple, they had the golden head of an Ass, which they adored. But herein Tacitus forgets himself, having in the fifth *fift* book of his own History truly confessed of the Jews, that they worshipped one only God: and thought it most profane to represent the Deity by any material figure, by the shape of a man, or any other creature; and they had therefore in their Temples, no Image or representation, no not So much as in any City by them inhabited. Somewhat like this has Alexander Polyhistor, in Stephanus; who also makes Judas with 〈◊ 〉, the first parents of the Jews.

## p02459 · sig. 258b

〈◊ 〉 Jolaus *Iolaus* draws them from 〈◊ 〉, whose parents were Sparton and Thebis; whence it came that the Spartans or 〈◊ 〉 〈◊ 〉 kindred of the Hebrews *Hebrewes*: but they did it as descended of Abraham, says Josephus *Iosephus*. Some of these reports seem to have been gathered out of divine letters; though wrested and 〈◊ 〉, according to the custom of the Heathen. For So have they 〈◊ 〉 and altered the Story of the Creation, of Paradise, of the Flood *Floud*; and given new names to the children of Adam in the first age: to Noah and his sons, in the 〈◊ 〉: and So to *Abraham, Isaac*, and *Jacob* *Iacob**, Moses*, and the rest of the fathers, and leaders of the Hebrews: all which feignings, as touching the Jews and their originals, Josephus against Appion, and Tertullian have sufficiently answered. For that the Hebrews were the Children of Arphaxad and Eber *Heber*, no man doubts: and So 〈◊ 〉 originally, taking name either of Eber, the son of Salah *Sale*, or else( says Montanus) of wandering, as is before remembered. And therefore does Stephanus, the 〈◊ 〉 Grammarian, derive the Hebrews or 〈◊ 〉, from Abraham *Abrabon*; having mistaken the name of Abraham, who was the son of Eber, in the sixth *sixt* descent. Their ancient names were first changed by the two grand-children of Abraham: for after Jacob, otherwise Israel, the chief part were called Israel, another part after Esau or *Edom, Edomites*; at length the remnant of Jacob, being most of the Tribe of Judah, honoured the name of Judas, the son of Jacob, and became 〈◊ 〉 or Jews: as also for a time in the name of Ephraim the son of Joseph, the chief of the Patriarches of the ten Tribes; the rest of the ten Tribes were comprehended: but were first rooted out when the Kingdom of Israel fell. The Judeans *Iudaeans* continued their names, though they suffered the same servitude not long after, under 〈◊ 〉.

The government which this Nation under-went, was first paternal: which continued till they served the 〈◊ 〉. They were secondly ruled by their Captains *Captaines* and leaders, Moses and Joshua *Iosua*, by a policy Divine. Thirdly, they subjected themselves to Judges *Iudges*. Fourthly, they desired a King, and had Saul for the first: Of whom and his successors, before we entreat, we are first to speak of their Government under Judges, after the death of Joshua: with somewhat of the things of Fame in other Nations about these times.
