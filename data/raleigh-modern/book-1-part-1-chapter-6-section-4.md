§. IIII. That from the relics of ancient Records among the Ae gyptians and others the first Idols and Fables were invented: and that the first JUPITER *IVPITER* was *CAIN, VULCAN* *VVLCAN**, TUBALCAIN* *TVBALCAIN**, &c.*

BUT in So great a confusion of vanities, were among the Heathens themselves there is no agreement or certainty, it were hard to find out from what example the beginnings of these inventions were borrowed, or after what ancient pattern they erected their building, were it not certain, that the Egyptians *Aegyptians* had knowledge of the first Age, and of whatsoever was done therein, partly from some inscriptions upon stone or metal remaining after the Flood *Floud*, and partly from Mizraim the son of Ham *Cham*, who had learnt the same of Ham, and Ham of his Father Noah. For all that the Egyptians write of their ancient Kings, and date of times cannot be feigned. And though other Nations after them had by imitation their Jupiters *Iupiters* also, their *Saturnes, Vulcans*, and Mercuries with the rest, which S. Augustine out of *Varro, Eusebius *out of many profane Historians; *Cicero* *Cicere**, Diodorus Siculus, Arnobius, *and many more have observed, to wit, the *〈◊ 〉, Phrygians, Cretians, Greeks* *Greekes**, *and other Nations; yet was Cain the son of Adam( as some very learned men conceive) called and reputed for the first and ancient 〈◊ 〉; and Adam for the first Saturn: for Jupiter was said to have invented the founding of Cities; and the first City of the World was built by Cain, which he called Enoch, of whom were the Henochij before remembered. And So much may be gathered out of Plato in Protagoras, which also Hyginus *Higinus* in his 275. Chapter confirms. For besides that, many Cities were founded by divers men; *Tamen primam latissimam a primo & antiquissimo* ("Yet the first, the widest, from the first and most ancient [division];")* *Iovae *〈◊ 〉: yet the first and largest was built by the first and most ancient *JUPITER, seated in the East parts, or in India, according to that of MOSES: And 〈◊ 〉 *dwelt towards the East side of Eden, & c. *where also the Henochij were found after the Flood. And therefore was Jupiter by the Athenians called Polieus, a Founder of Cities, and Herceios, an Encloser or strengthener of Cities; ( say Cornutus *Phornutus* and Pausanias) and that to Jupiter Herceios there were in very many places Altars & Temples erected. And that there were Cities built before the Flood, Plato also witnesses, as may be gathered in this his affirming, that soon after Mankind began to increase, they built many Cities; which as his meaning he delivers in plain terms, in his third Book of Laws *Lawes*: for he says, that Cities were built an exceeding space of time before the destruction by the great Flood.

This first Jupiter of the Ethnickes was then the same Cain, the son of Adam, who marrying his own Sister( as also Jupiter is said to have done) inhabited the East, where *Stephanus de urbibus* ("On Cities")* *places the City Henochia. And besides this City of *Enoch* *Henoch**, Philo 〈◊ 〉 *conceives that Cain built six others, as *Maich, Jared* *Iared**, Tehe*, *〈◊ 〉, Selet *and Gebat: but where Philo had this I know not. Now as Cain was the first Jupiter, and from whom also the Ethnickes had the invention of Sacrifice: So were *Jubal* *Iubal**, Tubal *and Tubalcain( Inventors *Inuentors* of Pasturage, Smiths-craft, and Music) the same, which were called by the ancient profane Writers, *Mercury* *Mercurius**, Vulcan *and Apollo; and as there is a likelihood of name between Tubalcain and Vulcan: So does Augustine expound the name of Noema or Naamah *Naamath*, the Sister of Tubalcain, to signify Venusta, or beautiful Voluptas, or pleasure; as the wife of Vulcan is said to be Venus, the Lady of pleasure and beauty. And as Adam was the ancient and first *Saturn, Cain *the eldest *Jupiter, Eva, Rhea*, and Noema or Naamah the first Venus: So did the Fable of the dividing 〈◊ 〉 the World between the three Brethren the Sons of Saturn arise, from the true Story of the dividing of the Earth between the three Brethren the Sons of Noah: So also was the Fiction of those Golden Apples kept by a Dragon taken from the Serpent, which tempted Eve: So was Paradise itself transported out of Asia into Africa, and made the Garden of the Hesperides: the prophecies, that Christ should break the Serpents head, and conquer the power of Hell, occasioned the Fables of Hercules killing the Serpent of Hesperides, and descending into Hell, and captivating Cerberus: So out of the taking up of Enoch by God, was borrowed the conversion of their Heros *Heroes*( the Inventors of Religion, and such Arts as the life of man had profit by) into Stars *Starres* and Heavenly Signs *Signes*, and( withal) that leaving of the World, and ascension of 〈◊ 〉; of which Ovid *Ouid*:

For although thereby the Ethnickes would understand Justice it self to have failed, as it is a virtue abstract, and may be considered without a person; yet as it is usual among the ancient Potes to describe Virtues *Vertues* and Vices by the persons of Men and Women, as Desire by Cupid, Valour by Mars, Beauty or Lust by Venus, So do they also the persons of Men by like Virtues and Vices, and therefore by Justice and *〈◊ 〉, Enoch*: the Justice and piety of Enoch being in the same manner expressed, as that of Noah was by Moses, for Noah was said to be a just man; And NOAH walked with God. And of Enoch it is written, *that he walked with God, and he was no more seen:* *for God took him away*.

We find also many remembrances of Seth, the paternal Ancestor of Enoch and Noah: for Amenhotep *Amenophis*, the same King of Egypt *Aegypt*, which reigned at such time as Moses carried thence the children of Israel, ( as of late some learned men mistaking his time supposed) called his Son and Successor Sethos *Setho*, of Seth, and of the same Seth( as many men of good judgement have granted) were the Princes of Thrace called Seuthes, whereof there were many very famous. But herein was the memory of Seth most manifestly preserved, that the Egyptians worshipped Seth, as their most ancient parent, and of the first tradition: in honour of whom they called a principal Province Setheitica. We also find in Bithynia the City of Sethia, and others of the same name elsewhere. And sure from the Egyptians did the 〈◊ 〉 borrow this kind of Theology, though they scorned to acknowledge any antiquity preceding their own; and that they might not seem to learn elsewhere, they gave the same names to their own Idols which the Egyptians did to theirs.
