§. VIII. Of Ashkenaz *ASCANEZ* and RIPHATH, the two elder Sons of GOMER.

## p01251 · sig. 119b

Ashkenaz was the Father of those which the Greeks *Greekes* call Regini, ( says Josephus *Iosephus*) but he gives no reason why.

## p01252 · sig. 119b

Eusebius makes Ashkenaz the Father of the Goths. The Jews *Iewes* in their Targum *Thargum* make him the root of the Germane Nation, but their expositions are commonly very idle. Pliny *Plinie* finds Ascania in Phrygia, near the Rivers *Riuers* of Hylas and *Cios: Melanchthon* *Melanchton** *being of the same opinion, that the Tuiscones were descended of the Ashkenaz, ( for Tuiscones, says he, is as much to say, as of the *Ashkenaz, praeposito articulo die *Ashkenaz) and that the word signifies a Religious Keeper of fire: it being an ancient superstition to pray at the fire of Sacrifices, as afterwards at the Tombes of Martyrs. Not far from Phrygia was the Lake Ascania, known by that name in the Romans *Romanes* time. And among the Kings which came to the succour of Troy, was *Ascanius( Deo similis* ("Ascanius (like unto God"), says *Homer) like 〈◊ 〉 God: *because he was beautiful and strong: for in the same manner does Virgil grace *Aeneas, Os humcrosque Deo similis, in face and body like one of the Gods. Virgil *also remembers such a River together with the Hills *Hils* Gargara: as, *Illas 〈◊ 〉 amor trans Gargara, transque sonantem* ("[That] love [carries] them beyond Gargara, and beyond the sounding [sea];") ASCANIVM, *Appetite leads them both over the Mountains* *Mountaines** Gargara, and the roaring *ASCANIVS. But this Pliny makes more plain in the description of Phrygia. For he places the City of Brillion upon the River 〈◊ 〉, which is adjoining to 〈◊ 〉. and is near the border of the 〈◊ 〉 Empire: and the Lake Ashkenaz he directs us to find by the description of Prusia, founded by Hannibal at the foot of Olympus, which Iyeth far within the Countries of Bithynia: and then from 〈◊ 〉 to 〈◊ 〉 are accounted five and twenty miles, in which way this Lake lies, even between 〈◊ 〉 and Nicaea *Nicea*. And So 〈◊ 〉( as I conceive him) takes them of Ashkenaz, to be the inhabitants of Pontus, and Bithynia, and those North parts of *Asia. Stephanus de 〈◊ 〉 *makes it a City of Troas, built by 〈◊ 〉 the son of Aeneas: saying, that there was another of that name in Mysia. Of Ascania a Lake of *Bithynia, Ptolemy* *Ptolomie** *witnesses: and Strabo gives Ascania both a Lake, a River, and a Town in Mysia, near unto Cio; which also agrees with Pliny. For Pliny finds Prusia( before spoken of) near Cio, and calls the Islands before Troy Ascanes.

Now, whether these places took name of 〈◊ 〉 the son of Gomer, or of Ascanius the son of Aeneas, it might be questioned: sure it is, that Ascanius which brought succour to the Trojans *Troians*, could not take his name from Aeneas son, who was then either exceeding young, or rather unborn: and it seems that the Countries whence those succours came, were not out of any part of Phrygia or Mysia, but farther off, and from the North parts of all Asia the less, which by Jeremiah *Hieremie* is called Ashkenaz, by the figure Synecdoche, as 〈◊ 〉 thinks. Out of those testimonies therefore which deceive not, we may confidently determine. For of the Prophet Jeremiah we shall learn of what Nation the Ascenez were, whose words are these: *Set up a Standard in the Land, blow the Trumpet among the Nation against her, call* *up the Kings of Ararat, 〈◊ 〉, and Ashkenaz against her, & c. *meaning against the *〈◊ 〉. Ararat *was Armenia the greater, as most Interpreters consent, So called of the Mountains of Ararat which run through it: Minni the lesser *Armenia: Armenia *being compounded of Aram and Minni. For Minni was the ancient name: ( says Junius *Iunius* and others before him) and Aram anciently taken for Syria, which contained all that Tract from Euphrates to the Sea-coasts of Phoenicia and Palestine *Palaestina*; and therefore 〈◊ 〉 being in elder times but a Province of Syria, the Scriptures difference it in the Story of 〈◊ 〉 and 〈◊ 〉, and call it Aram-padam. Then if these two Nations were of the 〈◊ 〉, and 〈◊ 〉 joined with them( who altogether united under Cyrus and Darius, came to the spoil of the Babylonian Empire) we shall er much to call Askenaz Germany *Germanie* or Almain, for we hear of no Swart Routiers *Ruttiers* at that siege. But the Askenaz were of those Nations which were either subject or allied to the Medes: of which, if any of them came afterward into Phrygia, I know not: for the dispersion of Nations was in aftertimes without account. But for the opinion of 〈◊ 〉, who makes them to be Goths; or that of Josephus, who calls them Rhegini; or of the Jews, who will have them to be Germans *Almaines*; when they confirm it either by Scriptures or Reason, I will think as they do.

## p01254 · sig. 120a

Of Riphath the second son of Gomer there is mention in the first of *Chronicles. Beroaldo* *Beroaldus** *and Pereira *Pererius* think that he wandered far off from the rest of his Brothers, and therefore no memory of his plantation. But I see nothing to the contrary, but that he might seat himself with the rest of his Family: for there wanted no room or soil in those days for all the sons and Grand-children of Noah. Therefore I take it to be well understood, that the Riphei were of Riphath, which the Greeks afterwards( according to Josephus) called the Paphlagonians *Paphlagones*: and Riphei( says Melanchthon) signifies Giants. These people were very famous in the North parts, and in Sarmatia: the most of number and power among them, *Sarmatarum gens maxima Heneti, The greatest number of the Sarmatians were the Heneti*; who spoke the ancient Polish *Polac*: which being first called Riphei( for the love of some of their Leaders or Kings) changed their names and became Heneti, ( a custom exceeding common in those times) and dwelt first in Paphlagonia, as Homer witnesses, and So does Apollonius in his Argonautica *Argonauticks*: Now, when these Rephei( afterward Heneti) sought new Regions, they came along the shores of Euxinus, and filled the North part of Europe, containing *Russia, Lituania*, and Polonia. From thence they crossed thwart the Land, and peopled Illyria, desirous( says Melanchthon) of a warmer soil of fruit and Wine. These Heneti or Veneti, whom Melanchthon takes to be one people, filled all that Land between the Baltic and Adriatic Sea; and to this day the name of the Gulf Venedicus is found in Russia. This Nation, after they were possessed of Lituania and Polonia, disturbed the plantation of the Boii *Boij* and Hermunduri *Hermondurij*. Therefore, it seems to me, that of Riphath came the Riphei, afterward Heneti; and So thinks Arias Montanus, first seated in Paphlagonia, but in course of time Lords of Sarmatia, and those other parts before remembered, chiefly between the Rivers of Vistula and Elbe *Albis*. The name( says Melanchthon) signifies wandering or Wanderers, or Nomads *Nomades*: a people which lived by White-meats *White-meates* and fruits, as( indeed) all Nations did in the first Ages.
