§. III. Of MOSES his omitting sundry things concerning CAINS Generation.

## p01025 · sig. 82a

BUT of the remembrance and testimonies of the name of the City of Enoch *Henoch* in profane Story, thus much may suffice; Now it follows to answer some few objections against certain particulars in the fourth and fifth Chapter of Genesis: against which for the first it is demanded, how it was possible for Cain( having no other assistance then his son Enoch) to perform such a work as the building of a City, seeing there is thereto required So many hands, and So great a mass of all sorts of Materials? To which it is answered, that we are first to consider, That of Cain( because he was the Parent of an impious race) MOSES uses no ample declaration; and So it best agrees with his divine reason, seeing that he contains the whole Story of the first race, which wasted by the least account, 1656. years, in five short Chapters. Yet thus much may every man borrow of his own weakest reason, That seeing it pleased God to bestow on the first generations of men's lives So long a measure, as 800. and 900. years, that in such a space Cain had not want of leisure and means to build many such Cities as Enoch, be the capacity answering to what other of the World soever: for in what age of Cains life he built it, the Scriptures are silent: as of whose times, and the times of his issues Moses had the least care. And as it was said of Cain, that he built a City: So was it said of Noah, that his three sons peopled all the World; but in both, the process of time required to be understood: which advice seeing Moses uses where the space less requires it, as knowing that he writ the Scriptures to reasonable men, we may easily understand, that such was his meaning also in all reports of like nature. For in making but a difference between the birth of Abel, and oblation of Cain, he spoke it in this sort, *Fuit autem post dies multos* ("But it came to pass after many days [that Cain brought an offering];")* *or *a fine dierum*( that is) in process of time, *it came to passe that *CAIN *brought an Oblation*. And therefore it is in like sort to be understood of Cain, that many years fore-gone, and when his people were increased, he built the City of Enoch or Enoch.

## p01026 · sig. 82a

And where it is written, as of Cain, that he built Enoch, So of Solomon *Salomon*, that he built the Temple of Jerusalem *Hierusalem*; yet it is well known of Solomon, that he employed in that work, 150000. Labourers: for this phrase or speech is common with our selves to say, The King invaded; when he caused an invasion to be made: and he built, when he commanded such a building. And therefore seeing we find, that Moses had no regard to the ages, to the birth, or to the death of any of Cains issues, it is not to be marvelled at, why he also passes over in a word the building of Enoch, without addition of any circumstance: for of *Cain, Moses *writes in this manner: CAIN *also knew his wife, who conceived and bare *Enoch, *and he built a City, and called the name of the City after the name of his son *Enoch. And to Enoch was borne IRAD, and IRAD begat MEHVIAEL, and MEHVIAEL begat METHUSAEL *METHVSAEL*, and METHUSAEL LAMECH.

## p01027 · sig. 82a

Now of *Seth, Moses *writes far otherwise, and in this manner. And SETH 〈◊ 〉 105. *years, and begat *ENOCH, and SETH *lived after he begat *ENOCH 807. *years, and* *begat Sons and Daughters: So as all the days of *SETH were 912. *years, and he died*: as for the years and times of the wicked, they were not numbered *in libro viventium* ("in the book of the living"), says CYRIL. But in SETH was the Church of GOD established, from whom Christ descended, as touching his manhood: and therefore this way and work Moses walked in, and finished it with care, passing over the Reprobate Generation( as aforesaid. Of the Line of Adam by *Cain, Moses *remembers but eight Generations, reckoning Adam for one, and of the Line of Adam by Seth ten, counting Adam also therein, as follows:

## p01028 · sig. 82b

❧ 1. Adam. 2. Cain. 2. Seth. 3. Enoch. 3. Enosh. 4. Irad. 4. Kenan *Cainan*. 5. Mehujael *Mahuiael*. 5. Mahaleel. 6. Methusael. 6. Jared *Iarad*. 7. Lamech, who by Ada had 7. Enoch. 8. Jubal *Iubal* and Tubal, and by Silla Tubalcain, and Noema. 8. Mathusalem. 9. Lamech, and 10. Noah.

## p01029 · sig. 82b

These be the Generations of Adam by Cain, which the Scriptures mention: but Josephus *Iosephus* gives unto Lamech threescore and seventeen Sons and Daughters, by his two wives Ada, and Silla: and to these three sons of *Lamech, Moses *ascribes the invention of Pasturage, of Music, and the working in Metal; for it seems that Jubal first gathered together, and made familiar those beasts which formerly were untamed, and brought them into Herds *Heards* and Droves *Droues*: Tubal invented Music, and Tubalcain the working in Brass and Iron: the one being addicted to Husbandry, the other was Mechanical, the third given to idleness and pleasure. In whom began these three meaner degrees of Sheep-herds *Sheep-heards*, Handy-crafts-men, and Musicians. And in the issues of Seth began the Services *Seruices* of God, Divinity, Prophecy, and Astronomy: the children of the one beheld the Heavens, the other the Earth.
