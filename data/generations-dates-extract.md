- **Copyright:** the five books (six PDFs: both editions of *Preparing the World for Jesus*) are © Generations and are **not** in this repository. Only the dates as printed, PDF page numbers and short notes in our own words are kept. Each source link opens the original PDF on Generations' S3 at that page (`#page=N`), except the 2nd edition of *Preparing the World for Jesus*, which is a private copy with no public link.
- **Editions:** *Preparing the World for Jesus* is imported in both editions. The 2nd edition (2024) is the primary Generations reading; a 1st-edition (2020) entry on a record the 2nd edition also dates is kept but marked `superseded_by` and shown collapsed, and it never sets a sort. 1st-edition entries the 2nd edition does not date are flagged "not in 2nd ed.".
- **Sort:** Generations is a secondary display chronology and never one of the four tiers. Records that no tier reaches use the first Generations date as a **provisional** sort (badged on the site); see A8 in `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS-FOR-KEVIN.md`.
- **Agreement column:** "agrees" means the same year as the record's tier 1–4 sort year, or a printed range that contains it. "CONFLICT" gives the gap in years (no year 0). "NEW" means a record created by this import (provisional sort).

- Obvious misprints: "AD 1074" (p. 413; the context is Agag, 1074 BC), "around AD 586" (p. 407), "AD 609" (p. 526), "Darius's sixth year would have been in 616 BC" (p. 541; 516 BC is meant).

- Scripture time spans and AM arithmetic (e.g. the 390 and 430 years, the seventy weeks) where no BC year is printed.
