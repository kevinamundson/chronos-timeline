**How Jordan numbers years:** Anno Mundi starts at 0 for Adam's creation. In BC 5:8 (Dec. 1993) he began giving B.C. years in his "SBC" (strictest Biblical chronology). SBC puts the decree of Cyrus at 456 B.C. instead of 536 B.C., so his B.C. years run about 74-75 years later than Ussher's. He also quotes "CCC" (current consensus chronology) years from ISBE 1979. Those CCC years are his comparison figures, not his own dates. In the Daniel series (1995) he uses conventional B.C. years for the Persian period.

### BC 8:12 — Countdown to Exile IV: Ezekiel and the Structure of History

- Read at: https://biblicalhorizons.com/1996/12/01/8_12/
- Mirror: https://www.garynorth.com/public/27127.cfm
  - We have seen that Josiah reunited Judah and Israel in his 12th year of rule (628-627 BC).
  - Israel had been taken into captivity by Assyria in 719 BC, about 91 years earlier.
  - We can guess that it happened 40 years before Josiah reunited the nation, and thus in 667 BC.
  - In 732 BC, Pekah was already dead.
  - If, however, we put Pekah's attack against Ahaz in the last year of the former's reign, in 736 BC, and then add 65 years, we come to 671 BC.
  - If we add 40 years, we come to 631 BC, which is the 8th year of Josiah, the year he began to seek Yahweh's face.
  - This leaves us 351 years to count backwards from the end of Northern Israel in 671, which brings us to 1022 BC, the end of David's reign.
  - If, however, we count from the 8th year of Josiah to the destruction of Jerusalem in 586 BC, we get 45 years, to be subtracted from the total 390 years of Israel.
  - This leaves 345 years to count backwards from 671 BC; which brings us to 1016 BC, the 5th year of Solomon's reign.
  - Or, we might count backwards from when the investiture of Jerusalem began, in 588 BC, which takes us to the third year of Solomon, when there were still people rebelling against his God-appointed rule.
  - Accordingly the 430 years of sin against God's presence in Jerusalem begin with David's numbering the people in 1022 BC, in the 38th year of his 40-year reign.
  - We move 351 years to 671 BC, when Israel ceased to exist and only Judah remained.
  - We move 40 years, for Judah, to 631 BC, the year Josiah set his heart to restore the Temple and reunite the land.
  - Then we continue to 592 BC, 39 more years, the year of Ezekiel's prophetic theater announcing Jerusalem's doom.
  - By this time, in 591 BC, Ezekiel is shown that in their hearts, the leaders of Israel were bowing down to all kinds of abominable things.
  - In Ezekiel 24, the prophet is told that on that very day, the 10th of the 10th month of the kings' ninth year (588 BC), Nebuchadnezzar had laid siege to Jerusalem, and would bring God's judgment upon her.
