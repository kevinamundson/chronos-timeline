# James B. Jordan: dates extract (backlog)

Everything below was read in the two sources the owner supplied. Nothing is inferred or converted.

- Biblical Horizons, "Biblical Chronology Newsletter" category: https://biblicalhorizons.com/category/biblical-chronology/ (9 pages, 83 posts; read 2026-10-17)
- Gary North archive, "Archive: Biblical Chronology (1989-1996)": https://www.garynorth.com/public/26646.cfm (82 issues; read 2026-10-17)

Citation shorthand: **BC v:n** = Jordan, *Biblical Chronology* vol. v, no. n. Full article URLs are in Part B and in `data/sources.json` (ids `jordan-bc-*`).
Copyright: each issue says "Copyright © James B. Jordan [year]". The articles are free to read online but they are copyrighted, not public domain. This file quotes short excerpts for reference only.

**How Jordan numbers years:** Anno Mundi starts at 0 for Adam's creation. In BC 5:8 (Dec. 1993) he began giving B.C. years in his "SBC" (strictest Biblical chronology). SBC puts the decree of Cyrus at 456 B.C. instead of 536 B.C., so his B.C. years run about 74-75 years later than Ussher's. He also quotes "CCC" (current consensus chronology) years from ISBE 1979. Those CCC years are his comparison figures, not his own dates. In the Daniel series (1995) he uses conventional B.C. years for the Persian period.
